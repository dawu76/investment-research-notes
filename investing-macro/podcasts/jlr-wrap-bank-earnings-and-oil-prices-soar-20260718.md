# The Wrap: Bank Earnings and Oil Prices Soar | The Julia La Roche Show [Chris Whalen]

Detailed overview of the interview featuring Chris Whalen, Chairman of Whalen Global Advisors, on *The Julia La Roche Show*, published on July 18, 2026.

---

### I. Executive Summary
Chris Whalen breaks down the disconnect in bank earnings, noting that while trading and IB fees are soaring, core net interest margins and loan yields are shrinking due to aggressive competition from private credit. He also reiterates his forecast for a return to double-digit inflation driven by a looming severe shortage of refined energy products (diesel) ahead of the November elections. Whalen highlights a $4 trillion hidden risk to the US banking system from direct non-recourse loans to private credit funds backing unprofitable zombie companies.

### III. Detailed Key Takeaways

#### 1. The Private Credit Zombie Risk
Banks are losing middle-market lending business to private credit giants (Apollo, Blackstone) and are compensating by lending directly to these funds.
*   **Non-Recourse Leverage:** Banks have extended $1.5 trillion in non-recourse loans, plus $3 trillion in undrawn commitments, to private credit funds.
*   **Zombie Portfolios:** Many companies backed by private credit are unprofitable and survive only on cheap debt. If they default, banks bear the systemic risk.

> "Half of them are unprofitable... The banks often make these loans on a non-recourse basis, which means they can't go after the fund... these are essentially zombie portfolios."
> — *Chris Whalen*

#### 2. The Refined Product Crisis and Resurgent Inflation
Whalen warns that cooler CPI prints are temporary, and double-digit inflation will return by fall due to refined product constraints.
*   **Diesel Squeeze:** Global refining capacity is extremely tight due to geopolitical disruptions in the Middle East.
*   **Transport Bottleneck:** Shortages of diesel fuel will push up transportation costs, feeding back into the price of all goods.

> "None of us consume oil. We consume gasoline. We consume other products... by the time we get to Labor Day, you're going to see very significant shortages, not of oil. There's a glut of oil out there, Julia. What's really important is refined product."
> — *Chris Whalen*

#### 3. Housing Market Parallels to 2005
*   **DSCR Speculation:** The housing market is propped up by investors using Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) loans to buy properties.
*   **Credit Quality Decay:** This private-label credit expansion mirrors the early credit cracks seen in 2005 prior to the subprime crisis.

### IV. Portfolio Positioning & Action Items
*   **Avoid Money Center Banks:** Stay away from large commercial banks with massive exposure (drawn and undrawn) to private credit funds.
*   **Accumulate Gold & Silver:** Maintain exposure to physical metals, particularly silver, which is supported by strong Eastern commercial demand.
*   **Mortgage REITs for Yield:** Allocate to Agency mortgage REITs like Annaly (NLY) that earn spreads on government-backed mortgage securities.
