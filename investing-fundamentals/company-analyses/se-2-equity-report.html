Section 1

Executive Summary

Profitable and growing ~35-48%, but lopsided: Shopee is 72% of revenue and ~25% of segment EBITDA, while Garena earns the most ($1.66B). The market doubts the reinvestment will stop.

Sea runs three businesses in Southeast Asia, Taiwan and Brazil. Shopee is the region's largest e-commerce marketplace (FY2025 GMV $127.4B; ~52% of Southeast Asian GMV in 2024 per Morningstar/Momentum Works). Monee lends to Shopee's buyers and sellers, and increasingly to people outside Shopee (loan book $11.1B, +62%). Garena publishes games, mainly Free Fire. FY2025 revenue was $22.9B (+36%), net income $1.6B and adjusted EBITDA $3.4B.

The mix of revenue and profit is lopsided. Shopee earns about 72% of revenue but only ~25% of segment adjusted EBITDA, because it runs at ~0.7% of GMV against a long-run target of 2-3%. Garena, a one-hit gaming franchise, earns the most profit ($1.66B adjusted EBITDA in FY2025). Monee grows fastest but is a credit business whose margins are falling as it lends to riskier, off-platform borrowers. The investment case is that Shopee's margin roughly triples over five years while Monee compounds. The risk is that competition from TikTok Shop, Temu/PDD and Mercado Libre forces Shopee to keep reinvesting, so the margin never arrives.

In plain English: Southeast Asia's Amazon-plus-bank, funded by a mobile game. It is profitable and growing ~35-48%, but the market doubts management will ever stop reinvesting the profits.

How They Make Money

Shopee is transaction-based (core take rate ~11.1%), Monee is spread-based on an $11.1B loan book, and Garena sells in-game items. In Q2 2026 Garena still earned the most adjusted EBITDA.

  • Shopeetransaction-based. Core marketplace revenue (commissions, transaction fees and ads) was $4.26B in Q2 2026 (+65.6%), an ~11.1% take rate on $38.3B GMV.

    Shopee:

    transaction-based. Core marketplace revenue (commissions, transaction fees and ads) was $4.26B in Q2 2026 (+65.6%), an ~11.1% take rate on $38.3B GMV.

    Value-added services revenue (mainly logistics) was $0.68B (-9.0%). Shopee raises seller fees regularly.

    Shopee Brazil lifted its fixed commission on items under R$80 from R$4.00 to R$4.50 on October 1, 2026 (MS). MS expects the core take rate to rise to 11.3% in Q3 2026. Morningstar models mid-teens by the mid-2030s, driven mainly by ads.

  • Moneeinterest and fee income on a $11.1B loan book ($10.0B on balance sheet, $1.1B sold or funded off-book), plus payments fees.

    Monee:

    interest and fee income on a $11.1B loan book ($10.0B on balance sheet, $1.1B sold or funded off-book), plus payments fees. It is spread-based and recurring while the book is outstanding, but sensitive to credit losses.

  • Garenain-game purchases (microtransactions). Revenue is recognized over the life of the virtual items, so bookings lead revenue.

    Garena:

    in-game purchases (microtransactions). Revenue is recognized over the life of the virtual items, so bookings lead revenue.

    It is hit-driven and swings with content (anime collaborations such as Naruto and Jujutsu Kaisen drive spikes).

Revenue Quality

Most revenue repeats but is not contracted. GMV growth is slowing on tougher comparisons, and adjusted EBITDA grew 10.6% against 48% revenue growth in Q2.

  • Recurring vs. one-offmost revenue repeats but is not contracted. Shopee's commissions recur with consumer spending (13.9B orders in FY2025, so no customer concentration).

    Recurring vs. one-off:

    most revenue repeats but is not contracted. Shopee's commissions recur with consumer spending (13.9B orders in FY2025, so no customer concentration).

    Monee's interest income is the most predictable stream while the book grows. Garena's is the least predictable: one title, content-driven, with history of collapse (bookings fell 40% in 2022 and 34% in 2023 after the India ban and fading engagement; Morningstar).

  • Geographic concentrationIndonesia is the largest market for Shopee (181M Shopee MAU in September 2026, ~a third of worldwide MAU; MS/Sensor Tower).

    Geographic concentration:

    Indonesia is the largest market for Shopee (181M Shopee MAU in September 2026, ~a third of worldwide MAU; MS/Sensor Tower). Brazil is the main growth market outside Asia.

    Currency exposure is broad: MS expects a ~3-4% FX headwind to Q3 2026 GMV as the rupiah weakened 9% y/y.

  • Growth is slowing on tougher comparisonsGMV growth was ~30% in Q1 2026 and 28.4% in Q2, with ~25% guided for 2026. MS models 25% for Q3, then 20% in 2027 and 16% in 2028. Revenue growth...

    Growth is slowing on tougher comparisons:

    GMV growth was ~30% in Q1 2026 and 28.4% in Q2, with ~25% guided for 2026. MS models 25% for Q3, then 20% in 2027 and 16% in 2028. Revenue growth (+48% in Q2) is running well ahead of GMV because take rates are rising and Monee is growing.

  • Profit quality is mixedadjusted EBITDA grew only 10.6% in Q2 against 48% revenue growth.

    Profit quality is mixed:

    adjusted EBITDA grew only 10.6% in Q2 against 48% revenue growth. Shopee and Monee are spending on users (S&M +64.5% to $1.66B, or 21% of revenue) and Monee provisions rose 71.5% to $555M.

    The effective tax rate is moving to the mid-to-high 30s as more entities turn profitable (management, March 2026), so net income grows more slowly than EBIT.

Capital Intensity

Capex is light because Sea rents its facilities; Monee absorbs capital as the loan book grows. Sea Cloud is the new swing factor.

  • Capex is light by design.Sea rents logistics hubs, sorting centers, warehouses and trucks (Morningstar; CEO on the Q2 call: "we don't own land or warehouses").

    Capex is light by design.

    Sea rents logistics hubs, sorting centers, warehouses and trucks (Morningstar; CEO on the Q2 call: "we don't own land or warehouses"). This keeps capex low but moves logistics investment into operating expenses.

  • Working capital is favorable in e-commerce and heavy in fintech.Shopee collects from buyers before paying sellers, so its float funds the business.

    Working capital is favorable in e-commerce and heavy in fintech.

    Shopee collects from buyers before paying sellers, so its float funds the business. Monee's loan growth absorbs capital: the book rose from $9.2B (December 2025) to $11.1B (June 2026).

    Funding comes mainly from MariBank deposits, the balance sheet and off-book sales.

Penetration, take rate, the Shopee margin ramp (0.67% toward 2-3%), Monee credit and Brazil. MS models 0.85% in 2027 and 1.1% in 2028.

Section 8

Competitive Edge

Narrow moat (Morningstar) from Shopee's network effect. Share has held, but the moat does not yet show up in margins, and TikTok and Pinduoduo are growing faster.

  • Moat classificationMorningstar rates the group Narrow, from Shopee's two-sided network effect (more buyers bring more sellers and selection, which brings more buyers).

    Moat classification:

    Morningstar rates the group Narrow, from Shopee's two-sided network effect (more buyers bring more sellers and selection, which brings more buyers). Garena and Monee get no moat: Free Fire is one hit with no proven repeat, and Monee is small next to regional banks.

  • Evidence forShopee keeps 50%+ GMV share in most markets despite TikTok Shop's entry and the Tokopedia merger.

    Evidence for:

    • Shopee keeps 50%+ GMV share in most markets despite TikTok Shop's entry and the Tokopedia merger.
    • Shopee and TikTok both raised fees in Q4 2025 and 2026. Morningstar reads this as a rational market where higher revenue can offset competitive costs.
    • Shopee says its in-house logistics costs are below third-party rates, and instant-delivery cost per order fell ~20% in Indonesia (Q2 call).
    • Monee's 90-day NPL ratio has held at ~1.0-1.1% while the book grew 62%. That suggests Shopee's transaction data helps underwriting.
  • Evidence againstShopee's margin (0.67% of GMV) is a fraction of Alibaba's (~2.5% per Morningstar) or Mercado Libre's. A strong network effect usually shows up as...

    Evidence against:

    • Shopee's margin (0.67% of GMV) is a fraction of Alibaba's (~2.5% per Morningstar) or Mercado Libre's. A strong network effect usually shows up as pricing power, and Shopee still has to subsidize shipping and vouchers to hold share.
    • TikTok Shop was projected to match Shopee's parcel volume in 2026 (Momentum Works via Morningstar, Feb 2026). J&T Express's Southeast Asian parcel volume rose 74% in Q4 2025, mostly from TikTok Shop.
    • TikTok MAU grows faster than Shopee's (+15% vs. +5% in ASEAN, September 2026; MS).
    • PDD's Pinduoduo app is gaining downloads in Malaysia and Singapore even as Temu tapers (MS). Pinduoduo's main app launched in Malaysia in December 2025, dropped its free-shipping minimum in August 2026 and reached No. 1 in shopping-app downloads there. DBS estimates 10-13% of Shopee's GMV is in markets exposed to it (October 1, 2026 downgrade).
    • Shopee's response costs money: quick commerce, VIP benefits and its Migoo AI shopping assistant are the spending DBS cites for forecasting 2027 e-commerce adjusted EBITDA 5% below consensus.
    • Switching costs for buyers are low (Morningstar's bear case).
  • Emerging threatAI shopping agents such as Meta's Muse, which completes purchases for users and takes a cut, could route buyers around Shopee's search and ad...

    Emerging threat:

    AI shopping agents such as Meta's Muse, which completes purchases for users and takes a cut, could route buyers around Shopee's search and ad placements (see market-newsletter-digest-2026-09-22 and market-newsletter-digest-2026-09-24). MS lists "potential Muse disruption" among Sea-specific concerns.

    Meta's Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp are heavily used in Southeast Asia, which makes this more relevant to Shopee than to most marketplaces.

  • Durability assessmentShopee's scale advantage looks durable. Its pricing power does not yet.

    Durability assessment:

    Shopee's scale advantage looks durable. Its pricing power does not yet.

    The moat shows up in market share and in the ability to raise fees, but not yet in margins. Until Shopee's EBITDA/GMV moves above ~1%, the claim that Southeast Asian e-commerce will look like China's (profitable leaders) is a forecast, not evidence.
