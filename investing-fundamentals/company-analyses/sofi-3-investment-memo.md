# SoFi Technologies ($SOFI): Investment Memo

**Price:** ~$14.52 (intraday October 16, 2026), down 56% from the $32.73 high (November 2025). It hit a 52-week low of $13.86 on October 14, when hotter inflation data hit lenders. **Market cap** ~$18.8B (1,291.6M shares); ~$19.3B on ~1.33B diluted shares. **Tangible book** $7.34 a share (June 30, 2026). **Next catalyst:** Q3 2026 results on October 27.
Business background: [[sofi-2-equity-report]]. Valuation figures marked "this memo" are new arithmetic. Sell-side figures come from secondary aggregators and are marked as such.

## 1. Executive Summary

**Thesis.** SoFi has built a profitable digital bank fast: revenue grew 35% in FY2025, deposits reached $45.5B, members 15.8M, and it has posted eleven straight GAAP-profitable quarters. Returns have not caught up with the growth story, though. At ~7-9%, ROTCE is less than half of what the price needs. At $14.52 the stock trades at ~2.0x tangible book, ~24x 2026 adjusted EPS guidance and ~18x 2027 consensus. Holding ~2x book requires SoFi to sustain about 17-20% ROTCE, the bottom of management's 20-30% target. The business is carrying a lot of weight at once: personal loans held at fair value (~2.9x tangible book), dependence on private-credit buyers for loan sales, and capital that growth used up in 2026 (CET1 from 22.8% to 18.7% in six months). It enters a period when rates are still rising and consumers are slowing. This memo's probability-weighted value is ~$14 (end-2027 basis; ~$12 discounted to today). That is close to the current price, with a wide spread: ~$6 bear, ~$24 bull. The bull/bear stress test ([[sofi-4-stress-test]]) leaned bear on the ROTCE path and found no margin of safety at ~$14.50. Its sizing guide is a 1-2% starter position at most, adding only if Q3 clears the bar listed under the October 27 milestone in Section 4.

* **Bull case:** Management's plan holds: adjusted revenue grows 30%+ a year and 2028 adjusted EPS reaches ~$1.03-1.12. Cross-buy and deposits keep lowering acquisition and funding costs. ROTCE reaches only ~11-12% in 2028 (EPS of ~$1.08 on roughly $9.5 of tangible book) but keeps rising toward 20% after that, and the market pays ~22x (about 2.5x tangible book) for that path. Value ~$24 (+64%).
* **Bear case:** A 2027 consumer-credit downturn coincides with high rates. Fair-value marks and a pullback by loan buyers cut 2028 EPS to ~$0.55, and SoFi is valued like a bank at ~11x earnings, about 1x stressed tangible book. Value ~$6 (-58%).
* **Target investor:** growth-at-a-reasonable-price or special-situation investors who can track credit and capital quarterly and hold through fair-value volatility. Not for income investors: there is no dividend, and the Fed policy statement leaves no room for one. Not for anyone wanting low-volatility financials either.

| ROTCE needed to justify P/TBV (12.5% cost of equity, 5-8% long-run growth) | ~17-20% | ~15-17% |

The ROTCE rows use P/TBV = (ROTCE − g) ÷ (cost of equity − g). The cost of equity reflects a 10-year Treasury near 5.2% plus a premium for a high-beta stock. Current ROTCE is ~7% (H1 2026 annualized) and ~8.6% on 2026 guidance. At ~$14.52 the market is paying for SoFi to reach about the low end of its target. At $11.50 it pays for most of the way there. Neither price prices in a credit recession: the recession-case tangible book below is ~$6.11.

**Street reference points (secondary sources):** average target ~$20-21 (about 15-25 analysts; range $15-30). KBW is Underperform with a $15 target (cut October 12), citing a >$1B estimated Q3 headwind from fair-value accounting, higher rates and balance-sheet growth. Goldman Sachs is Neutral, target cut to $15 from $18 (October). StoneX initiated at Buy with a $25 target (October 13). Citi is Buy, $30. Benchmark upgraded to Strong Buy in October 2026. Q3 consensus EPS is ~$0.17.

## 2. Business Model & Unit Economics

**Revenue breakdown (Q2 2026; GAAP $1.219B, +43%):**

| Segment | Net revenue | YoY | Contribution | Margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lending | $724.8M | +63% | $399.0M | 55% |
| Financial Services (incl. LPB $143.3M) | $466.3M | +29% | $212.7M | 46% |
| Technology Platform | $84.5M | -23% | $11.8M | 14% |

All revenue is U.S. By type, net interest income was $788.2M (65%) at a 5.98% net interest margin. Fee-based revenue was $443M in Q4 2025 (~$1.8B annualized).

**Unit economics:**
* **Personal loans:** 12.89% weighted coupon, 4.77% assumed annual default rate, 25.8% prepayment rate, origination FICO 742.
  * *Cushion before losses:* coupon minus funding (~4%) minus losses leaves ~4% a year before operating costs. Losses would have to roughly double (to ~9%) before carry turns negative (this memo's arithmetic).
  * *Cumulative losses:* 6.8% so far on 2020-2026 cohorts with 62% of principal repaid. Management's ceiling is 7-8%.
* **Funding:** deposits cost 156bp less than warehouse lines (~$713M a year of savings). About 98% of deposits are insured.
* **Member acquisition:** sales and marketing ran $1.095B in FY2025 (30% of revenue) for ~3.6M net new members, about $300 per net new member (rough; it also funds brand building).
  * *Cross-buy:* 51% of new products came from existing members in Q2 2026, which lowers the effective cost per product.
  * *Revenue per member:* adjusted revenue was about $300 per average member in Q2 2026, annualized (this memo's arithmetic).
* **Loan Platform Business:** $3.1B of originations earned $143.3M in Q2 2026, about 4.6% of volume, using little of SoFi's own capital.

**Operating leverage:**
* *Financial Services:* clear. Contribution margin went from 37% to 51% in FY2025.
* *Company-wide:* the adjusted EBITDA margin rises from 29% in FY2025 to a guided 33-34% in 2026. But 2026 adjusted net income guidance stayed at ~$825M when revenue guidance rose ~$100-200M in July, so net margin falls to ~17%. The tax rate moves from 8.5% to ~22%.
* **H2 2026 is demanding:** reaching $825M needs ~$498M of adjusted net income in H2, ~$249M a quarter, against $160M in Q2 (+55%).

**Working capital and cash conversion:** not meaningful in the usual sense. Loans originated for sale run through operating cash flow, which was -$3.7B in FY2025. The useful measures are:
* tangible book per share: $7.34, +56% year over year. Most of the $2.62 rise came from the ~$1.6B equity raise at $27.50; trailing earnings explain only about $0.40-0.50 of it ([[sofi-4-stress-test]])
* CET1: 18.7%
* the share of originations sold rather than held

## 3. Competitive Position & Moat

* **Market position:** SoFi is the largest digital prime personal lender by origination volume.
  * *Volume against rivals:* $10.7B in Q2 2026, against $4.2B at Upstart and $3.1B at Happen (formerly LendingClub) (company figures via Zacks).
  * *Market share:* roughly 9% of a ~$280B U.S. personal-loan market by balances (the market estimate is secondary).
  * *Brand:* awareness is 10.4% unaided.
  * *Other segments:* in deposits and investing SoFi is small against incumbent banks and brokers.
* **Moat classification:**
  * *Regulatory:* the national bank charter gives deposit funding and preempts state usury caps ([[fintech-usury-tila-regulatory-exposure-2026]]).
  * *Scale and brand:* the origination engine and member funnel.
  * *Switching costs:* moderate, from direct deposit and multiple products per member.
  * *Not a moat:* loan pricing. Personal-loan borrowers shop on rate.
* **Durability:** partly shown.
  * *For:* deposits grew every year since the charter ($7.3B at end-2022 to $45.5B at June 2026), cross-buy is rising, and Financial Services margins are expanding.
  * *Against:* ROTCE (~7-9%) is still below a ~12-13% cost of equity, so the franchise has not yet shown it creates value at the bank level.
  * *Against:* the Technology Platform lost a large client (revenue -23%, accounts -16%), and it carries ~$1.4B of goodwill.
* **Key competitors:**
  * *Personal loans:* Upstart, Happen/LendingClub, Discover (now Capital One), Goldman's exited Marcus book, and credit unions.
  * *Deposits and app:* Chime, Ally, Capital One 360, Robinhood ([[hood-3-investment-memo]]), Cash App.
  * *Platform:* Marqeta, Fiserv, FIS.
  * *What differentiates SoFi:* a prime-only credit box and one app holding a bank charter.

Probability-weighted value: **~$14.0** (-4%). These are end-2027 values on 2028 EPS, the convention of this wiki's other memos. Discounted ~1.2 years at 12.5%, they are worth ~$12 today.

**Deep-bear tail (not weighted):** stagflation, with loss rates +150% and discount rates +300bp. That is a ~$6.3B pretax hit, CET1 ~9%, and tangible book ~$3.66. At 0.8-1.0x book the stock is ~$3-4 (-75%).

**Credit and rate stress (from the Q2 10-Q sensitivities, scaled linearly; this memo's arithmetic, 25% tax, no offsetting earnings):**

| Scenario | Loss rates | Discount rate | Pretax hit | CET1 after | Tangible book per share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mild slowdown | +25% | +50bp | ~-$1.0B | ~17.1% | ~$6.73 |
| Recession | +50% | +100bp | ~-$2.1B | ~15.5% | ~$6.11 |
| Severe (2009-like for unsecured) | +100% | +200bp | ~-$4.2B | ~12.2% | ~$4.89 |
| Stagflation tail | +150% | +300bp | ~-$6.3B | ~9.0% | ~$3.66 |

Inputs: +10% loss rates is -$211M pretax; +100bp is -$1.04B of fair value. In a typical recession Treasury yields fall, and each -100bp adds ~$1.23B, offsetting much of the rate column. The worst case for SoFi is credit stress while rates rise. Every scenario keeps CET1 above the 7.0% minimum plus buffer. The personal book's short life (25.8% prepayment) frees capital quickly if SoFi slows originations.

**Timeline and milestones:**
* **October 27, 2026 (Q3):** size of the fair-value marks (personal-loan discount rate vs. 4.97%), adjusted net income against the ~$249M a quarter that H2 needs, CET1, LPB volume, and charge-offs with and without delinquent-loan sales.
  * *Discount-rate benchmark:* compare the 4.97% with the 2-year Treasury (~4.8%), not the 10-year. Personal loans prepay 25.8% a year, so their duration is short.
  * *Bar for adding to a position (from [[sofi-4-stress-test]]):* a Q3 mark of about $1B or less before hedging, the ~$825M adjusted net income guide held, and tangible book of at least ~$6.75 a share.
* **January 2027 (Q4 and 2027 guidance):** whether the 2025-2028 targets survive, and 2027 EPS against consensus ~$0.82.
* **2027:** the consumer-credit cycle (unemployment, card delinquencies) and the private-credit buyer market.
* **March 2029:** $862.5M of 2029 convertible notes mature.

## 5. Risk Framework with Failure Modes

Ranked by severity × probability.

| # | Risk | Trigger | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Credit and rates hit together** | Unemployment rises while yields stay above 5% (stagflation) | Fair-value marks hit earnings and capital in the same quarter: -$1B to -$6B pretax across the scenarios above | Prime borrowers (FICO 742-760); short loan life; recent vintages tracking better than 2017 |
| 2 | **Loan buyers retreat** | Private-credit outflows or wider spreads on consumer ABS | About $6.3B of personal loans a quarter leaves the balance sheet ($3.1B through LPB plus $3.24B of own-book sales), ~59% of Q2 personal originations. LPB ($143M a quarter, ~12% of revenue) and loan-sale gains shrink. SoFi holds more loans (using capital) or originates less. Reported charge-offs rise toward ~3.7% as delinquent-loan sales slow | Deposit funding lets SoFi hold more loans: one extra quarter of sold volume (~$6.3B of risk-weighted assets) takes CET1 from 18.7% to ~16.5%. SoFi can also cut LPB originations, which costs fee revenue but no capital. Buyer base not disclosed |
| 3 | **Capital consumed by growth / dilution** | Risk-weighted assets keep growing ~30% a year | CET1 falls further (22.8% to 18.7% in H1 2026). A new raise at ~$14.5 needs ~1.9x the shares per dollar of the $27.50 raise | Retained earnings (~$825M in 2026); loan sales; slower origination growth |
| 4 | **2026 guidance miss / earnings quality** | Q3 fair-value marks; tax rate up to ~22% | H2 needs $249M a quarter of adjusted net income vs. $160M in Q2. A miss would hurt the 2028 targets' credibility, and the multiple with them | Revenue guidance raised in July; strong origination momentum |
| 5 | **Technology Platform erosion and multiple compression** | More client losses; fintech de-rating | Platform revenue -23%; goodwill of ~$1.4B is tested each October 1 (GAAP charge only; already outside tangible book and CET1); the market values SoFi on bank multiples | Tech Solutions wins (Peach Finance); the segment is small (~7% of Q2 revenue) |

**Other risks:** student-loan policy changes; regulatory risk from crypto and SoFiUSD; brand spending (~30% of revenue) that is hard to cut without slowing growth; stock-based compensation ~7% of revenue; high retail ownership and volatility (52-week range $13.86-32.73).

**Pre-mortem.** If this investment fails by 2029, the most likely reason is a consumer-credit downturn in 2027 that arrived while rates were still high. Fair-value marks took most of a year's earnings, private-credit buyers stepped back from personal loans, and SoFi had to choose between shrinking originations and using up capital. ROTCE stayed below 10%, the 2028 targets were dropped, and the market valued SoFi as an ordinary consumer lender at about 1x tangible book.

**Assumptions that must hold:**
1. Personal-loan lifetime losses stay within management's 7-8% ceiling. Recent vintages are at 4.68% with 35% of principal still outstanding.
2. Loan buyers keep absorbing ~$6B a quarter through LPB ($3.1B) and whole-loan sales ($3.24B), about 59% of personal originations.
3. Deposits keep growing at a stable spread below wholesale funding, and rates do not rise much further.
4. ROTCE climbs from ~7-9% to ~11-12% in 2028 (management's own EPS path) and to the mid-teens by 2029-2030. At ~$14.50 the price needs about 17-20% eventually.
5. Growth is funded by retained earnings and loan sales, without another equity raise at depressed prices.
