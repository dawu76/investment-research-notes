## 1. Executive Summary & Thesis

Sezzle Inc. ($SEZL) represents one of the most asymmetric risk/reward profiles in the public financial technology sector. By pioneering the **subscription-gated BNPL model** (Sezzle Anywhere at $17.99/month), Sezzle has successfully transformed a commoditized 0% APR e-commerce checkout button into a high-margin, recurring consumer utility with **854,000+ paid subscribers** and **>80% Free Cash Flow conversion**. The market's recent August 2026 post-earnings selloff (down ~34% from $175 to $125 due to sequential 2H growth normalization) creates a rare entry point to acquire an elite **85% Rule of 40 compounder** generating **50.0% Adjusted EBITDA margins** at just **21.5x Forward GAAP P/E** (0.61x PEG) and **12.6x EV/EBITDA**.

* **Bull Case ($385.00 | +208.0% Upside):** Sezzle scales paid subscribers to 1.5M+, accelerates *SezzleCash* direct advances, secures an OCC national bank charter to insource deposit economics, and sustains +32% 3-year revenue CAGR ($15.10 FY28 EPS). Multiple expands to 25.5x P/E.
* **Base Case ($232.00 | +85.6% Upside):** Sezzle sustains +20% 3-year revenue CAGR ($1.05B revenue in FY28), maintains 48% EBITDA margins ($10.30 FY28 EPS), and trades at a normal 22.5x P/E multiple (+22.9% Annualized IRR).
* **Bear Case ($82.50 | -34.0% Downside):** Subprime consumer credit defaults surge past 4.5% NCO, subscriber churn increases following fee fatigue, and OCC charter approval stalls. Revenue growth slows to +7.5% CAGR ($5.50 FY28 EPS) and multiple compresses to 15.0x P/E.
* **Target Investor Profile:** **Growth at a Reasonable Price (GARP) & High-ROIC Quality Compounder**.

### Moat Classification & Structural Fragility Points
* **Consumer Subscription Moat:** By decoupling BNPL from specific integrated merchants and issuing virtual Mastercards via *Sezzle Anywhere*, Sezzle captured consumer wallet share across non-integrated merchants (Target, Walmart, groceries). 854k subscribers generate strong recurring LTV.
* **Underwriting Agility:** The 6-week Pay-in-4 duration enables rapid algorithmic model tuning, preventing the multi-year credit deterioration that impairs traditional card issuers.
* **Fragility Points:** High recurring fee ($216/yr) risks "buy-and-cancel" episodic churn; OCC bank charter approval timeline (18–36 months) could trap $150M–$250M in regulatory equity capital.

## 7. Volatility Anatomy & Key Disclosures Driving Analyst Models

### The "Guidance Treadmill" & Low-Float Dynamics
Sezzle’s historical drawdowns (such as the -50% decline from Aug 2025 to May 2026 and the -34% drop in August 2026) illustrate the **Guidance Treadmill Effect** in high-beta fintech:
1. **Priced for Acceleration:** When Sezzle reports blowout numbers (e.g. Q1 2025, Q1 2026), its compact float and short covering trigger violent +70%–100% upside rallies.
2. **The Beat-and-Decelerate Trap:** When management subsequently issues prudent guidance signaling sequential growth moderation (e.g., 2H 2026 slowing to ~30% from >50% in 1H 2026) or CAC frontloading (Q3 seasonal marketing), fast-money funds aggressively exit, creating 30%–50% drawdowns despite underlying GAAP profitability remaining elite.
3. **The Investor Opportunity:** For fundamental long-term investors, these guidance-driven drawdowns create compelling mispricings where an 85% Rule of 40 compounder can be purchased at 12x–13x EBITDA and ~20x GAAP P/E.

### The 3 Master Metrics That Shift Consensus Price Targets
* **Metric A — Subscription Net Adds (Sezzle Anywhere):** Every 100k subscriber net addition adds ~$21.6M in high-margin recurring ARR, increasing fair value by ~$8.00–$10.00/share.
* **Metric B — Net Charge-Off (NCO) % GMV:** If NCO stays below 2.50%, operating cash flow conversion exceeds 80%. A breach above 3.50% prompts Wall Street to cut EBITDA estimates by 15%–20%.
* **Metric C — Revenue Yield Normalization Trajectory:** Whether revenue take rate settles at 16% or 18% drives a $75M swing in annual EBITDA.
