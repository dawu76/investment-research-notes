# Zscaler, Inc. (NASDAQ: ZS) — Bull/Bear Stress-Test

**Format:** Adversarial debate — independent bull analyst, independent bear analyst, arbitrator
**Data as of:** Q2 FY2026 (January 31, 2026), reported February 26, 2026

## Bull Case

### 1. Zero Trust Secular Tailwind ($96B TAM)

- U.S. Executive Order 14028 creates floor demand — FedRAMP High and DoD IL4/IL5 certifications are a regulatory moat, not just a sales credential; government procurement cannot be displaced by competitive bundling
- ZS at 3.4% TAM penetration ($3.3B ARR / $96B TAM) with ~34% SSE market share — dominant at the top with decades of runway below
- Zero Trust Security market: $41.7B → $88.8B by 2030 (16.3% CAGR); SASE market: $15.7B → $133.8B by 2035 (24% CAGR)
- 40% of Global 2000 secured — 60% still unaddressed; the next multi-year growth wave hasn't started

### 2. Revenue Quality

- $6.1B RPO (+31% YoY) = ~1.8x forward annual revenue already locked in; RPO growing *faster* than revenue guidance (31% vs. 24%) means the forward revenue stream is accelerating
- 115% NRR on $3.3B ARR base = ~$500M incremental ARR annually from existing customers before a single new logo; the installed base is a self-funding growth engine
- 80% non-GAAP gross margins + 22.2% non-GAAP operating margin; FY2026 FCF guidance raised to 26.5–27% (~$880M annual FCF) — real cash at scale
- Non-GAAP operating margin trajectory: 15% (FY2023) → 19% (FY2024) → 22% (FY2025) → 22.2% (Q2 FY2026); structural leverage is confirmed, not hoped for

### 3. Platform Expansion Signals Re-Acceleration

- **ZFlex:** $290M+ TCV in Q2, +65% sequentially; typical 8-figure, 4-year commitments pull forward ARR and compress future churn risk simultaneously
- **Zero Trust Everywhere:** 550 enterprise customers (up from 130 a year ago — 4x YoY); each adoption carries 2–3x ARR step-up; thousands more eligible in the installed base
- **$1M+ ARR customers** grew from 664 → 728 in one quarter (+10%); Americas closed 2x the $1M+ deals YoY — winning where PANW competes hardest
- **Red Canary ARR guidance raised** from $95M to $130M within one quarter of closing — 37% upward revision signals strong cross-sell; ZDX Advanced Plus surpassed $100M LTM bookings (+80% YoY)

### 4. AI Agent Proliferation Is a Structural Tailwind

- ZS sits in the data path for all enterprise AI traffic by architectural necessity — nearly 1 trillion AI transactions flowed through ZS in calendar 2025; this is not optional overlay, it is the inspection layer
- 3,400+ unique enterprise AI apps detected inside the customer base quantifies a massively expanding attack surface; AI Protect landed eight-figure Fortune 500 deals in Q2 FY2026 — immediate monetization
- AI-generated threats (polymorphic malware, LLM-assisted phishing, automated exploitation) increase both volume and sophistication, driving upsell on ZS's inline inspection model
- SPLX acquisition secures AI-SPM (AI Security Posture Management for LLM workloads) — nascent category with no dominant incumbent; ZS entered before commoditization

> **Bull Summary:** Zscaler is a rule-of-~50 compounder — 24% revenue growth plus 27% FCF margin — with $6.1B RPO growing 31% YoY, platform acceleration on every emerging product (ZFlex +65% QoQ, Zero Trust Everywhere 4x YoY, $1M+ cohort +10% in one quarter), and a mandatory data-path position for all enterprise AI traffic. At 3.4% TAM penetration, the opportunity ahead dwarfs what has already been captured.

## Bear Case

### 1. SaaS-pocalypse: AI Agents Structurally Erode the Seat-Based TAM

- ZS pricing is fundamentally per-user and per-workload — enterprises replacing 100 human workers with 10 AI agents directly reduce the denominator of ZS's addressable market; this is permanent TAM contraction, not a cyclical risk
- Anthropic MCP and agentic frameworks enable AI agents to interact with networks directly without the human-in-the-loop identity model ZS's Zero Trust was designed to secure; the architectural premise of ZS — verify the human, secure the session — is misaligned with non-human agents
- A 20% enterprise headcount reduction across the installed base translates directly to lower seat counts at renewal, compressing NRR from 115% toward 105% without a single customer technically churning
- ZS has no credible agentic security product in production; the Zero Trust Exchange was not designed to authenticate AI agent traffic at scale

### 2. PANW and Microsoft Bundling: Pure-Play Is a Procurement Liability

- PANW's platformization strategy offers SASE/SSE at zero cost or deeply discounted as part of NGFW renewal bundles — ZS cannot win a price war against *zero*; PANW holds the largest NGFW installed base in the world
- Microsoft Global Secure Access bundled into M365 E5 at no incremental cost; for mid-market buyers, "good enough bundled" defeats "excellent standalone" in procurement — the question is not "is ZS better?" but "is ZS worth an additional six-figure contract?"
- Enterprise buyers are consolidating vendors, not adding them; ZS's pure-play positioning is a liability in a world where CFOs are asking CISOs to cut vendor count
- Total customer count growth has stagnated relative to historical pace — the bundling effect is already visible in the new business line

### 3. Red Canary Is a Fig Leaf Over Decelerating Core Growth

- Strip out Red Canary's ~$114M ARR and organic net new ARR grew only **7% YoY in Q2 FY2026** — the most current data point; that is not a growth company number, that is a maturing product number
- Zscaler abandoned calculated billings reporting beginning Q1 FY2026 — coinciding precisely with implied billings growth of ~+10% YoY against stated ARR growth of +25%; discontinuing a metric when it turns unflattering is a red flag, not a disclosure improvement
- H1 FY2026 organic net new ARR improvement (1% → 10% YoY) is lapping an extraordinarily weak comp — not genuine reacceleration
- Paid ~5x ARR ($692M) for Red Canary, which is now churning ("elevated churn" per management's own words), in an MDR market structurally dominated by CrowdStrike Falcon Complete and PANW Unit 42

### 4. Multiple Compression: The Valuation Math Is Punishing

- At ~12x NTM revenue, ZS is priced for sustained 20%+ growth (Rule of 45+); the organic data — 21% ARR ex-Red Canary, 7% net new ARR ex-RC in Q2 — implies Rule of 35 at best
- Historical comps show SaaS stocks crossing below 18–20% growth re-rate from 10–12x to 6–7x revenue within 2–4 quarters; applied to ZS's ~$3.3B NTM revenue, a de-rating to 7x implies 40–45% stock downside
- GAAP still deeply negative: -$0.21 EPS/quarter, -6% GAAP operating margin; SBC is real economic dilution systematically obscured by non-GAAP reporting
- The double-demotion risk (estimate cuts + multiple compression simultaneously) is the most asymmetric setup in large-cap security

> **Bear Summary:** Organic net new ARR grew just 7% YoY in Q2 ex-Red Canary; the acquisition is churning and masking core deceleration; billings disclosure was abandoned when the metric turned damaging. PANW and Microsoft bundling is procurement-driven — ZS cannot win a price war against free. Headcount-driven TAM contraction from AI automation directly erodes the seat-based model with no architectural response in sight. At 12x revenue with negative GAAP earnings, there is no valuation cushion against any of these risks materializing.

### The 3 Critical Divergence Assumptions

**Assumption 1: Does AI expand or contract ZS's addressable TAM?**

- **Bull world:** AI is incremental traffic volume — 1T transactions already flow through ZS; AI Protect and SPLX convert this data-path position into new ARR; every AI threat is an upsell
- **Bear world:** AI agents replace human seats, shrinking the pricing denominator at every renewal without a churn event; ZS's architecture authenticates humans, not machines; no credible agentic product exists
- **Why it dominates:** This determines whether the $96B TAM claim is growing or structurally shrinking — it is the question beneath all other questions
- **Current evidence verdict:** **Lean bull, narrow margin.** AI transactions are measurably flowing through ZS today and generating revenue (eight-figure Fortune 500 wins in Q2). The bear thesis is logically sound but is a 2027–2029 risk; ZS has a 1–2 year runway before renewal impact materializes at scale.

**Assumption 2: Is PANW/Microsoft bundling slowing new logos structurally or cyclically?**

- **Bull world:** "Not much change" in competitive dynamics per management; Americas $1M+ deals doubled YoY — ZS is winning where it competes hardest
- **Bear world:** Total customer count growth has stagnated; net new ARR ex-RC at 7% YoY is the bundling effect showing up in the new business line, not yet in the renewal base
- **Why it dominates:** If new logos can't be acquired economically, the NRR engine eventually runs out of installed base to expand into
- **Current evidence verdict:** **Lean bear, moderate margin.** Management says competition is stable, but the data shows net new ARR stagnating and customer count slowing. The $1M+ cohort growth reflects upsell within the existing base. The competitive pressure is showing up in new business before it shows in renewals.

**Assumption 3: Is organic core deceleration a trough or the new trajectory?**

- **Bull world:** H1 net new ARR improvement (1% → 10% YoY) + ZFlex (+65% QoQ) + Zero Trust Everywhere (4x YoY) = re-acceleration is in train; management has H2 pipeline visibility
- **Bear world:** ZFlex TCV pull-forward could be a one-time bolus; 10% improvement is lapping a destroyed comp; structural growth rate of the core, stripped of contract restructurings, is 15–18% — below what justifies the multiple
- **Why it dominates:** At 12x NTM revenue, the market prices 22–25% sustained growth; if organic core settles at 18%, multiple compresses 40–50%; at 15%, it's existential
- **Current evidence verdict:** **Too close to call.** Improvement trend is real and ZFlex/Zero Trust Everywhere metrics are credible leading indicators. But H2 pipeline is management assertion, not yet in revenue or billings. **Q3 FY2026 (~May 2026) is the pivotal earnings event** — seasonally strongest billings quarter, clearest signal.

### Closing Assessment

The single most important thing an investor must be right about to own ZS is whether organic net new ARR ex-Red Canary re-accelerates to 15%+ YoY in H2 FY2026 — this one data point simultaneously validates the competitive moat, disproves the bundling narrative, and justifies the 12x revenue multiple. **Q3 FY2026 earnings (~May 2026)** is the pivotal event: it is ZS's seasonally strongest billings quarter and provides the clearest signal without seasonal noise distortion.

**Portfolio sizing implication:** Cap initial exposure at 2–3% portfolio weight until Q3 FY2026 confirms H2 re-acceleration. A miss at current valuation implies 35–45% downside; a beat re-rates the stock meaningfully. The asymmetry is compelling on confirmation — but the near-term risk/reward does not support full-conviction sizing before the H2 data lands.
