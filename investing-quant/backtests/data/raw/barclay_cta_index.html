Barclay CTA Index

About the Barclay CTA Index

The Barclay CTA Index is a leading industry benchmark of representative performance of commodity trading advisors. There are currently 305 programs included in the calculation of the Barclay CTA Index for 2026. The Index is equally weighted and rebalanced at the beginning of each year. To see historical data on the number of programs included in the Barclay CTA Index, click here.

The monthly performance for the Barclay CTA Index since inception is available on the Indices Report

To qualify for inclusion in the CTA Index, an advisor must have four years of prior performance history. Additional programs introduced by qualified advisors are not added to the Index until after their second year. These restrictions, which offset the high turnover rates of trading advisors as well as their artificially high short-term performance records, ensure the accuracy and reliability of the Barclay CTA Index.

Barclay CTA Flash Report

If you need accurate, timely comparisons of CTA returns, the BarclayHedge CTA Flash Report is your go-to source. Every month, we email you the latest returns on more than 200 CTA managed-futures programs. It's the fastest, most cost-effective way to compare CTA managers - ideal for academics and researchers who need an at-a-glance view of the CTA ecosystem.

Order Online

  • Timely - Arrives in your inbox by the fourth business day of every month. Returns cover the month just ended.
  • Comprehensive - Provides data from 215 programs, covering 95% of CTAs with more than million under management.
  • Contextual - Compares monthly, year-to-date, and 12-month returns, plus assets under management. Includes performance of Barclay CTA Index and Barclay BTOP50 Index
  • Convenient - Uses Excel format for easy sorting.
  • Economical - Just per year for a single user license; per year for each additional license (same address, up to ten additional users).
