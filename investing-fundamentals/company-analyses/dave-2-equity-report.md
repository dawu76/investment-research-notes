## Executive Summary

Dave Inc. is a leading mobile-first neobanking and consumer liquidity platform that has engineered one of the most profitable financial turnarounds in consumer fintech history.^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/dave-1-document-sources.md] Founded by CEO Jason Wilk and backed early by Mark Cuban, Dave provides an ethical alternative to predatory legacy bank overdraft fees and high-interest payday lending. Its flagship product, **ExtraCash™**, provides short-term liquidity advances of up to $500 with zero interest, zero late fees, and mandatory underwriting powered by its proprietary **CashAI** machine-learning engine.^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/dave-1-document-sources.md]

The company monetizes through four high-velocity streams: ExtraCash overdraft service fees (a transparent 5% fee structure with $5 min / $15 cap and express disbursement options), monthly membership subscriptions, Dave Card interchange revenue via sponsor bank partner **Coastal Community Bank (CCB)** and Visa, and in-app survey/gig-economy partner commissions. Dave has inflected from cash-burning hypergrowth (-$82.5M Adj. EBITDA in 2022) to elite profitability, guiding FY2026 Revenue of **$725M–$735M** (+45%+ YoY) and Adjusted EBITDA of **$315M–$325M (44% margin)** with Adjusted Diluted EPS of **$17.00–$17.50**. This performance is underpinned by industry-leading unit economics: an ultra-low **$19 Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)**, **$222 annualized ARPU**, **2.12% 28-day delinquency**, and extraordinary capital velocity (>35x annual capital turnover driven by ~7–10 day advance durations).^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/dave-1-document-sources.md]

**Synthesis:** *Dave Inc. has transformed small-dollar consumer liquidity into an algorithmic cash-flow compounding machine, generating 44% EBITDA margins and >35x capital turnover by pairing an ultra-low $19 CAC with proprietary CashAI underwriting precision.*

### Core Products & Services
1. **ExtraCash™:** Flagship short-term liquidity advance allowing members to access up to $500 ahead of their next paycheck. Unlike payday loans or credit card cash advances, ExtraCash charges 0% interest and $0 late fees, deducting repayment automatically upon the member\s next payroll direct deposit.
2. **Dave Banking & Dave Card:** A full-featured neobanking deposit account and physical/virtual Visa debit card issued through FDIC-insured partner **Coastal Community Bank (CCB)**. Offers early direct deposit (up to 2 days ahead), high-yield savings (4.0%+ APY), automated budgeting tools, and cashback rewards.^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/dave-1-document-sources.md]
3. **Monthly Subscriptions:** Recurring software memberships ($1.00 to $3.00/month) providing members with continuous account monitoring, automated balance-drop alerts to prevent negative balances, credit builder tools, and budgeting insights.
4. **Side Hustle & Surveys:** An in-app marketplace connecting members with gig-economy employment opportunities (DoorDash, Instacart, Uber) and paid survey tasks, providing non-credit earning tools.
5. **CashAI Engine (v6.0):** Proprietary machine-learning underwriting engine that integrates with consumer bank accounts via open-banking APIs (Plaid/Finicity), analyzing 24+ months of granular cash inflows, payroll consistency, spending volatility, and balance trajectories in real time (<200ms) without checking bureau FICO scores.

### Customer Profile & Target Demographics
- **Target Consumer (3.08M Monthly Transacting Members):** Everyday working Americans with median household incomes between $35,000 and $75,000 who regularly navigate timing mismatches between bi-weekly payrolls and recurring expenses (rent, utilities, groceries, gas).
- **Consumer Value Proposition & ROI:** Traditional banks charge $35+ per overdraft incident, extracting >$10B annually from lower-income depositors. A typical Dave member saves an estimated **$300 to $500+ annually** in avoided overdraft charges and payday loan interest.

### Core Operational & Service Differentiators
1. **CashAI Proprietary Cash-Flow Telemetry:** Traditional banks rely on FICO scores that miss real-time cash flow volatility. CashAI v6.0 analyzes direct deposit timing and liquidity velocity, enabling Dave to underwrite consumers traditional banks reject while keeping past-due rates at ~2.12%.
2. **Structural Acquisition Cost Advantage ($19 CAC):** Organic word-of-mouth and optimized app store acquisition deliver an insurmountable CAC advantage over peers ([[hood-2-equity-report|hood]], Chime, SoFi).
3. **Capital Velocity Advantage (>35x):** 7–10 day duration turns working capital 35x–45x annually, generating >65% ROTE.
4. **Transparent 5% Fee Architecture:** Proactive early 2025 shift to a flat 5% service fee ($5 min / $15 cap) with no late fees provides complete pricing transparency, avoiding the tip-shaming friction of EarnIn or the steep forced $10–$15/month subscription costs of Brigit and Cleo.
5. **Moat Vulnerabilities & Regulatory / Legal Litigation Risks:**
   - *Active FTC & DOJ Federal Lawsuit (Case No. 2:24-cv-09419, C.D. Cal.):* In November 2024, the FTC sued Dave alleging deceptive "up to $500" marketing, undisclosed express delivery fees ($3–$25), deceptive tipping screens, and recurring $1/mo negative-option subscriptions violating ROSCA. In December 2024, the **U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) joined the lawsuit**, filing an amended complaint adding CEO Jason Wilk as an individual named defendant and seeking civil monetary penalties and permanent injunctions. While Dave shifted toward a flat 5% service fee structure, adverse court rulings or mandated APR disclosures pose significant headline and monetary restitution risks.
   - *City of Baltimore Predatory Lending Lawsuit (Dec 2025):* Filed under Maryland Consumer Loan Law, alleging ExtraCash advances function as unlicensed payday loans with effective APRs exceeding 2,500%.
   - *TILA / Reg Z & State Usury Exposure:* See [[fintech-usury-tila-regulatory-exposure-2026]] for complete cross-fintech legal analysis and quantitative downside valuation bridges.
   - *Sponsor Bank Dependency:* Reliance on Coastal Community Bank requires ongoing compliance with 2023/2024 FDIC and OCC Interagency Third-Party Lending Guidance.
6. **Valuation & Cross-Reference:** Connected to analytical frameworks on [[valuations]], [[pvgo]], [[funding-short-squeeze]], and [[fintech-usury-tila-regulatory-exposure-2026]], Dave represents an extraordinary combination of high return on capital, GAAP profitability, and secular share gain from legacy bank overdraft models, counterbalanced by active federal court litigation.
