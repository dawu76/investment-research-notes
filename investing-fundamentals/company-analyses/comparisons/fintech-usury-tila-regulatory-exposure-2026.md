# FinTech Regulatory & Usury Risk: TILA Regulation Z, True Lender Exposure, and Downside Valuation Bridges

### Executive Summary

A defining structural vulnerability across modern consumer financial technology is **Regulatory & Usury Exposure**—the legal and accounting friction arising when fintech platforms monetize short-duration consumer credit via non-traditional fee structures (subscription tiers, expedited transfer fees, or voluntary tips) rather than explicit simple interest.

This comparison synthesizes the regulatory risk spectrum across public and prominent private fintech lenders—**Dave Inc. ($DAVE)**, **Sezzle Inc. ($SEZL)**, **Affirm Holdings ($AFRM)**, **SoFi Technologies ($SOFI)**, **MoneyLion ($ML)**, **Klarna ($KLAR)**, and **Earned Wage Access (EWA) providers (EarnIn, Brigit)**. It deconstructs the two core legal mechanisms (FDIA Section 27 "True Lender" rate export vs. TILA Regulation Z disguised finance charges), analyzes the active federal enforcement landscape (DOJ/FTC and CFPB), and models the quantitative stock price downside bridges if regulatory reclassifications succeed.

## 3. Deep-Dive: High-Risk vs. Insulated Business Models

### High-Risk Group: Dave ($DAVE), Sezzle ($SEZL), MoneyLion ($ML)

#### Dave Inc. ($DAVE) — *In Active Federal Court Litigation*
* **The Legal Action:** In November 2024, the FTC sued Dave in U.S. District Court (Central District of California). In December 2024, the **U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) intervened**, filing an amended complaint that added CEO Jason Wilk as an individual defendant.
* **The Specific Claims:** Deceptive "up to $500" advertising (rarely awarded), coercive tipping screens with false charitable claims ("meals for hungry children"), undisclosed express delivery fees ($3–$25), and negative-option $1/mo subscriptions violating ROSCA.
* **Municipal Action:** In December 2025, the City of Baltimore sued Dave under Maryland Consumer Loan Law, alleging ExtraCash advances operate as unlicensed payday loans with effective APRs >2,500%.

#### Sezzle Inc. ($SEZL) — *The $17.99/mo Subscription Usury Overhang*
* **The Accounting Classification:** Sezzle books ~$183M in ARR (854k subscribers @ $17.99/mo) under **ASC 606 as software subscription revenue**, reporting >90% gross margins and 50% EBITDA margins.
* **The Regulatory Conflict:** Under TILA Regulation Z (12 CFR § 1026.4), fees required to obtain credit are legally finance charges. On $200 of monthly spending, $17.99/mo yields an effective APR >100%, breaching state usury caps (NY 16% civil usury).
* **The Hindenburg (Dec 2024) Scorecard:** While solvency/cash-flow fears were resolved by $250M FCF, the regulatory usury vulnerability and open ICFR material weakness (PwC auditor transition) remain active risks.

---

### Insulated & Immune Group: Affirm ($AFRM) & SoFi ($SOFI)

#### Affirm ($AFRM) — *Best-in-Class Compliance Moat*
* **No Junk Fees:** Affirm explicitly bans late fees, penalty interest, and subscription gates.
* **Strict 36% Ceiling:** When charging interest, Affirm uses simple interest with transparent upfront dollar disclosures capped at 36.0% APR, aligning with the Military Lending Act and TILA.

#### SoFi Technologies ($SOFI) — *Structural Immunity via OCC National Bank Charter*
* **Full Bank Charter:** Acquired Golden Pacific Bancorp in 2022 to become **SoFi Bank, N.A.** Regulated directly by the OCC, Fed, and FDIC.
* **Federal Preemption:** Possesses direct federal interest rate preemption under Section 85 of the National Bank Act, rendering state usury caps inapplicable without third-party bank rent-a-charters.

## 5. Key Regulatory Leading Indicators to Monitor

1. **DOJ / FTC vs. Dave Inc. Summary Judgment Rulings:** Monitor docket filings in C.D. Cal. (Case No. 2:24-cv-09419). Any preliminary injunction restricting tipping screens or express fee flows triggers immediate P&L re-modeling.
2. **CFPB Final EWA & BNPL Rule Implementation:** Watch for formal enforcement actions reclassifying fintech subscription tiers as TILA finance charges.
3. **Sezzle OCC National Bank Charter Milestones:** Progress on OCC approval determines whether Sezzle can establish permanent federal interest rate preemption or remains exposed to state usury enforcement.
4. **State AG Joint Enforcement Actions:** Monitor multi-state investigations from NY DFS, CA DFPI, and CT AG regarding high-APR fintech fee structures.
