# Short-Term AI Fear, Long-Term Compute Scarcity | 22V Research

Detailed overview of the weekly macro & AI strategy presentation by **Jordi Visser**, President & Head of AI / Macro Strategy at 22V Research, published on July 26, 2026.

---

### I. Executive Summary

Jordi Visser argues that current equity market anxiety over hyperscaler AI CapEx represents temporary short-term sentiment noise, contrasting sharply with structural long-term compute scarcity. S&P 500 fundamentals remain exceptionally robust: Q2 earnings season (~1/3 reported) boasts an **86% beat rate** (an 8:1 beat-to-miss ratio vs. 76% 10-year average), net profit margins have expanded to **14.4%** (ex-Google), and 3-month estimate revisions overlay forward EPS to $305. Meanwhile, China has unleashed **$9B in state equity purchases**, insurance capital mandates, and eased IPO standards to backstop its domestic AI ecosystem (DeepSeek, Kimi K3), framing AI as a vital national strategic race.

On AI infrastructure, Visser highlights **Jevons Paradox**: model abundance and plummeting token prices drive exponential intelligence consumption rather than reduced spend. Corporate leverage is shifting from zero-interest-rate financial engineering (stock buybacks) to **"intelligence liquidity"**, where a 5% expense reduction via agentic workflow automation yields a 20% EPS surge. Visser sees a 70–75% probability of hyperscaler CapEx value-accretion, identifies Nvidia's **Vera Rubin** rack transition (~$630B/quarter run-rate capacity) and **optical/photonics step-functions** (`MRVL`, `AOI`) as key catalysts, and emphasizes that persistent memory demands in agentic AI will eventually catalyze massive global crypto volume expansion.

### III. Detailed Key Takeaways

#### 1. S&P Earnings Durability & Profit Margin Expansion
Market fears of an imminent recession or AI-driven earnings collapse are refuted by empirical earnings metrics:
*   **Q2 Beat Rates:** Through the first third of Q2 earnings reporting, **86% of S&P 500 companies have beaten estimates**, producing an 8:1 beat-to-miss ratio compared against 10-year historical averages of 76%.
*   **Margin Expansion:** Net profit margins across the S&P 500 expanded to **14.4%** (excluding one-off charges in Alphabet), while corporate profits relative to nominal GDP remain at all-time highs. Historical recessions (e.g., 2000 Dot-com, 2008 GFC) were preceded by deteriorating profit margins; current margins continue to accelerate upward.
*   **Estimate Revisions Factor:** Three-month estimate revisions remain strongly positive, tracking forward S&P 500 EPS toward **$305**. Since 2010, estimate revisions have served as the single most reliable factor overlaying market trajectory.

> "We're on an 8-to-1 ratio of beats to misses... Without the Google number, S&P profit margins are at 14.4%. Corporate profits relative to nominal GDP are at all-time highs... Profits are accelerating right now. If you're going to get bearish on this, you're taking a low-probability bet."
> — *Jordi Visser*

#### 2. China's $9B Capital Market Intervention in the AI Geopolitical Race
China has signaled that capital markets are vital to achieving its national AI ambitions:
*   **State Intervention:** Beijing deployed state-owned funds to purchase nearly **$9 billion in equity shares**, directed five major state-backed insurers to increase equity holdings, and relaxed exchange listing standards to accelerate tech IPO inclusions.
*   **Two-Horse Race:** The global AI landscape is splitting into two distinct paradigms: the U.S. relies on capitalist per-seat pricing and high-margin monetization, while China prioritizes open-source models, state backing, and "intelligence abundance."
*   **Domestic Drivers:** With Chinese real estate prices declining monthly, Beijing requires stable capital markets to maintain domestic financial stability and fund AI leaders like DeepSeek ($7B raise with government voting rights) and Moonshot AI (Kimi K3 seeking a $50B valuation).

> "China unleashed broad state support to halt the tech selloff... State-owned funds bought nearly 9 billion shares... Capital markets have become critical to achieving its AI ambitions. This is a two-horse race: China leans open-source and abundance, while the U.S. leans capitalism."
> — *Jordi Visser*

#### 3. Short-Term AI CapEx Anxiety vs. Long-Term Compute Scarcity & Jevons Paradox
Visser addresses investor skepticism regarding hyperscaler CapEx ($190B–$250B) and free cash flow compression:
*   **Contracted Demand:** Analyzing Google's CapEx disclosures via LLM evaluation indicates that hyperscalers are not spending on faith—incremental CapEx is chasing contracted customer backlog (e.g., renting Nvidia clusters from SpaceX at ~$920M/month). Visser assigns a **70–75% probability** that current CapEx programs prove accretive over a 3–5 year horizon.
*   **Jevons Paradox:** Drawing from conversations across *All-In*, *Moonshots* (Emad Mostaque), and *Forward Guidance*, Visser emphasizes that model abundance and falling inference costs do not reduce spending. Lower token prices trigger exponential growth in intelligence consumption.
*   **Workflow Routing:** Enterprise adoption is shifting from unconstrained "token maxing" to efficient workflow routing, where tasks are dispatched across open-weight and proprietary models based on cost and privacy.

> "The backlog is the whole ball game. They are not spending on faith—the incremental spending is chasing demand they can already see... Jevons paradox applies: cheaper intelligence means more intelligence consumed. Intelligence can be copied; powered compute cannot."
> — *Jordi Visser*

#### 4. "Intelligence Liquidity": Corporate Operational Leverage replacing ZERP Financial Leverage
During the ZERP/QE era, corporations engineered EPS growth through financial leverage—borrowing at 2% to buy back stock yielding 6%. In the AI era, operational leverage takes over:
*   **5% Cut = 20% EPS Surge:** Referencing research by Daniel Cornum, Visser demonstrates that a modest **5% reduction in operating expenses** via agentic workflow automation yields a **20% surge in EPS**—a far higher multiplier than legacy share buybacks.
*   **Prime S&P Candidates:** Large, high-headcount S&P 500 corporations with significant administrative friction—such as UPS, Cisco, Jabil, Centene, and Walmart—stand to gain immense bottom-line expansion as agentic workflows displace routine overhead.

> "Under QE and ZERP, companies used cheap money to engineer earnings... You borrow 2%, buy back stock yielding 6%, and watch EPS rise even if profit stood still. But now, a mere 5% reduction in expenses yields a 20% EPS jump. You get far more leverage reducing expenses through AI."
> — *Jordi Visser*

#### 5. Hardware Step-Functions: Vera Rubin Architecture & Optical Inflection
GPU availability has re-tightened after a brief pause, driving the next hardware upgrade cycle:
*   **Nvidia Vera Rubin:** Production ramps for Nvidia's Vera Rubin architecture are estimated at up to **1,000 racks per day** (~$630B per quarter run-rate capacity), delivering the lowest cost per token globally.
*   **Optical/Photonics Step-Function:** As 800V DC power architectures and dense GPU clusters scale, optical interconnects and co-packaged photonics face massive upward earnings revisions. Visser highlights Marvell (`MRVL`, his largest position) and Applied Optoelectronics (`AOI`) as prime beneficiaries of this optical step-function.

> "GPU availability is tightening back up... Nvidia could make 1,000 Vera Rubin racks per day, driving performance per watt to the lowest cost token worldwide... I believe we're going to see a step-up function in optical and photonics—it's the reason Marvell (MRVL) is still my biggest position."
> — *Jordi Visser*

#### 6. The "50 First Dates" Analogy: Agentic AI Memory & Global Crypto Integration
Visser links consumer agentic AI adoption directly to global blockchain and crypto rails:
*   **The "50 First Dates" Analogy:** Current LLM agents operate like Drew Barrymore's character in *50 First Dates*—isolated within single context windows with zero persistent memory of past user interactions. Transitioning to true consumer agents requires massive persistent memory storage (recalling lifetime user experiences, preferences, and context).
*   **Crypto & Agentic Workflows:** As agentic AI scales, autonomous agents will require global, friction-free settlement rails, driving exponential crypto transaction volumes. S&P launching its first Bitcoin crypto index and global institutional framework adoption (Japan, Korea, Sandy Call / Citi paper) signal structural convergence.
*   **Retail Sentiment Gauge:** Dogecoin (`DOGE`) has traded below its 20-day moving average for **67 consecutive days** (the longest stretch in history), demonstrating that retail spec energy remains completely absent until memecoins break out.

> "Current LLMs are like Drew Barrymore in 50 First Dates—they only remember things inside one context window. For agentic AI to work, the memory necessary to know everything about a user is massive... When we reach consumer agents, crypto volume will explode because agents need friction-free global rails."
> — *Jordi Visser*
