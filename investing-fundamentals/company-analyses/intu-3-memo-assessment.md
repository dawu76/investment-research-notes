# Intuit Inc. ($INTU) — Investment Memo Assessment & Follow-up (April 2026)

**Assessment Date:** April 21, 2026  
**Status:** Updated for Post-Tax Season 2026 Developments  
**Subject:** Follow-up on the $INTU Investment Memo and Equity Report

---

## 1. Executive Summary of Assessment

The original investment memo for Intuit ($INTU) correctly identified the "platform shift" toward AI and the importance of mid-market expansion (IES). However, as of April 21, 2026, the risk/reward profile has shifted dramatically due to a combination of **political developments (the cancellation of IRS Direct File)** and **macro-sector sentiment (the "SaaSpocalypse")**.

While the "Bear Case" regarding government competition has effectively collapsed, a new "Structural Bear Case" regarding the obsolescence of per-seat pricing models has emerged. Intuit is now a battleground stock: its fundamentals remain best-in-class, but its valuation is being tested by the transition from a software-license model to an AI-agent-outcome model.

---

## 2. Key Follow-up Findings (April 2026)

### A. Major Risk Elimination: IRS Direct File Cancellation
The highest-severity risk in the original memo was the expansion of the IRS Direct File program. 
*   **Update:** Following the passage of the "One Big Beautiful Bill Act" (OBBBA) in July 2025, the **IRS Direct File program was discontinued for the 2026 season**.
*   **Impact:** This removed a multi-billion dollar TAM risk for the Consumer segment. An estimated 30 million taxpayers who would have used the expanded government tool were forced back into the commercial market (TurboTax/H&R Block) for the tax season that ended on April 15, 2026. This represents a massive, unexpected tailwind for Q3 FY2026 results.

### B. The "SaaSpocalypse" and Pricing Model Risk
Since February 4, 2026, the software sector has faced a deep correction driven by "agentic AI" tools (e.g., *Claude Cowork*).
*   **The Problem:** Investors fear that AI agents will replace human employees, causing a sharp contraction in "seat-based" software licenses.
*   **Intuit's Position:** Intuit’s stock has dropped ~30-50% from its 2025 highs, trading at a compressed forward P/E of 20-30x. 
*   **Strategic Pivot:** Intuit is aggressively moving toward **outcome-based and token-based pricing**. They also secured **FedNow certification** in April 2026, signaling a shift toward monetizing "instant cash flow" and "financial outcomes" rather than just user access.

### C. IES (Intuit Enterprise Suite) Momentum
The mid-market strategy is proving more resilient than a simple "upsell" of existing users.
*   **Traction:** The **IES Construction Edition** (Beta) launched in early 2026 is seeing significant adoption, capturing a vertical where QuickBooks already had 17% market share.
*   **AI Integration:** A new partnership with **Anthropic** (April 2026) has integrated "Claude-powered financial agents" directly into IES, allowing the suite to automate multi-entity consolidations that previously required human CFOs.

### D. Q3 FY2026 Preview (Tax Season Performance)
*   **Consensus:** Analysts expect non-GAAP EPS of ~$12.50.
*   **Key Metric:** Investors are looking for **TurboTax Live** penetration. If the assisted category grew 25%+ without IRS competition, it would validate the "Quality Compounder" thesis.

## 3. Critical Clarifications & Remaining Questions

1.  **Margin Quality:** Does the rapid growth of "TurboTax Live" (assisted) actually compress gross margins? We need to verify if AI "experts" in the loop are truly reducing the human-to-customer ratio.
2.  **Churn in SMB:** Is the "pricing fatigue" reported by some analysts in early 2026 leading to higher churn in the low-end QuickBooks segment, or are those users simply being "absorbed" into the AI-automated free tiers?
3.  **Credit Karma Delinquencies:** With consumer credit markets under pressure in April 2026, is Credit Karma's ~20% growth sustainable, or is it masking a buildup of subprime risk in its referral engine?

---

## 4. Investment Recommendation (Revised)

*   **View:** **Opportunistically Bullish.**
*   **Rationale:** The market has "sold the story" of the SaaSpocalypse (valuation compression) while the company is "delivering the numbers" (17%+ revenue growth). 
*   **Next Catalyst:** Q3 FY2026 Earnings (Late May 2026). If Intuit demonstrates that it can successfully charge for **AI outcomes** rather than **human seats**, the stock is likely significantly oversold at current levels.
