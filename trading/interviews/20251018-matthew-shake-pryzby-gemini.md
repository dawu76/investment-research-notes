### The "Stepper" Trade & Finding a New Path

It wasn't that there were these great trades that made me change; it was the awful trades that we were kind of being forced into taking. We had to take these "stepper" trades. A stepper is, you see a million-share bid come in at $50, right? You see him step, the bid goes $50 to $50.10. You get up, and you scream "Stepper!" Everyone in the room has to take this trade. Back to the stepper: you see that stepper step from $50 to $50.10, you buy a thousand shares. You see the stepper step from $50.10 to $50.20, you buy a thousand shares. As long as you don't see that stepper printing his size, you keep buying. As soon as you see that million-share bid start printing, you scream "Printing!" and you have to market out. Here's the thing: you're battling 200 traders in the same room for that same liquidity. Here's what happened to me on the day that completely changed my trading: a stepper trade happens.
