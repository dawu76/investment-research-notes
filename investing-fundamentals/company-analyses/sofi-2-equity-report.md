# SoFi Technologies ($SOFI): Equity Report

**Price:** ~$14.52 (intraday October 16, 2026; Yahoo Finance). **Market cap:** ~$18.8B on 1,291.6M shares outstanding (July 31, 2026). **52-week range:** $13.86-$32.73 (the high was November 2025; the low was October 14, 2026). **Next report:** Q3 2026 on October 27, 2026, before the open.
Sources: the documents in [[sofi-1-document-sources]]. Figures are from SEC filings unless marked otherwise. "Adjusted" figures are company non-GAAP measures.

## Executive Summary

SoFi is a nationally chartered bank that operates as a digital consumer-finance app. It makes most of its money lending to prime, high-income borrowers: personal loans, student-loan refinancing and home loans. It funds those loans with $45.5B of mostly insured consumer deposits (June 30, 2026). Net interest income was 61% of FY2025 revenue. The rest is fees, mainly the Loan Platform Business, which originates personal loans for outside buyers for a fee, plus interchange, brokerage, referral fees and the Galileo technology platform.

The economics improved fast. FY2025 GAAP revenue was $3.61B (+35%), the fourth straight year above 30% growth, and net income was $481M after losses through 2023. Deposit funding costs about 156bp less than the warehouse lines SoFi used to rely on. The quality of those profits is the main question. Loans are carried at fair value, so changes in credit expectations or interest rates hit earnings immediately. Profits also depend on selling loans to private-credit buyers, and returns on tangible equity are still only ~7-9% against management's 20-30% target.

In plain English: SoFi is a fast-growing online bank for prime borrowers whose profits depend on the credit cycle and on Wall Street's appetite for its loans.

## What They Sell and Who Buys

**Products (June 30, 2026: 15.8M members, +35%; 24.4M products, +42%; 1.54 products per member):**
* **Lending (3.1M products):**
  * *Personal loans:* unsecured, typically debt consolidation. The weighted origination FICO was 742 in Q2 2026, and the average coupon in the fair-value model is 12.89%.
  * *Student loans:* mainly refinancing for graduates (origination FICO 760).
  * *Home loans:* mortgages and home equity (origination FICO 754).
  * *Small-business loans:* launched in Q2 2026.
* **Financial Services (21.3M products):**
  * *SoFi Money:* checking and savings, 7.9M products.
  * *Relay:* financial tracking, 8.0M.
  * *SoFi Invest:* 3.9M. It now counts four products, including the AI-driven Composer.
  * *Crypto:* 388K products (launched Q4 2025, the first national bank to offer consumer crypto trading).
  * *SoFi Plus:* paid membership with 206K subscribers.
  * *Also in this segment:* the credit card, the SoFiUSD stablecoin, and the Loan Platform Business (LPB).
* **Technology Platform (Galileo and SoFi Tech Solutions):** card-issuing, payments and core-banking software sold to fintechs and banks. Enabled accounts fell 16% to 134.8M (Q2 2026) after a large client left the platform before December 31, 2025.

**Customers:** U.S. consumers, mostly young, high-income professionals ("HENRYs"), acquired through heavy brand spending. Unaided brand awareness is 10.4% (Q2 2026), and SoFi Stadium naming rights are part of that spend. Enterprise customers are fintechs and banks (Technology Platform) and loan buyers such as private-credit funds (LPB).

**Core need:** cheaper credit than credit cards (personal loans), a lower rate on student debt, and one app for banking, investing and borrowing. The strategy, which SoFi calls the "Financial Services Productivity Loop," is to acquire members cheaply through deposits and other free products, then sell them loans. Cross-buy, the share of new products opened by existing members, reached 51% in Q2 2026, up from 35% a year earlier.

## How They Make Money

**Revenue model:** hybrid. Recurring net interest income on loans held, plus transaction fees from loan origination, loan sales and LPB, plus recurring platform and interchange fees.

| Lending | $1.849B | +24% | $1.017B | 55% | 48% |
| Financial Services | $1.542B | +88% | $793M | 51% | 40% |
| Technology Platform | $450M | +14% | ~$144M | ~32% | 12% |

**Q2 2026 (latest quarter):** total GAAP revenue $1.219B (+43%). By segment:
* *Lending:* $724.8M, contribution $399.0M (55%).
* *Financial Services:* $466.3M, contribution $212.7M (46%). This includes LPB revenue of $143.3M, earned on $3.1B of personal loans originated for third parties.
* *Technology Platform:* $84.5M (-23% year over year), contribution $11.8M (14%).

**Interest vs. fees:** net interest income was $788.2M in Q2 2026 (65% of revenue; +52%), with a net interest margin of 5.98%. Fee-based revenue was a record $443M in Q4 2025 (+53%), which the company annualizes at about $1.8B.

## Revenue Quality

* **Recurring:** net interest income on ~$46.6B of loans held at fair value (June 30, 2026: personal $27.5B, student $16.9B, home $2.2B), interchange, and Technology Platform fees. These are predictable over a quarter. Over a cycle they depend on loan growth, deposit costs and credit.
* **Transactional and market-dependent:**
  * *Loan sales and LPB:* in Q2 2026 SoFi sold or transferred $4.1B of personal and home loans, including $3.24B of personal-loan sales from its own book.
  * *LPB:* revenue depends on outside buyers' demand for SoFi-originated loans.
  * *Fair-value changes:* these flow through noninterest income every quarter.
  * *Where this shows up:* the Lending segment's noninterest income, which carries origination, sale and securitization gains and fair-value changes, and the Financial Services segment's LPB fees.
* **Concentrations:**
  * *Product:* personal loans are 59% of fair-value loans and drive most lending profit.
  * *Funding channel:* LPB and loan sales rely on a small set of private-credit and institutional buyers. The release does not name them. In Q2 2026 about $6.3B of personal loans left the balance sheet this way ($3.1B through LPB and $3.24B of own-book sales), ~59% of the $10.7B of personal originations.
  * *Technology Platform:* exposed to large clients. One client's exit cut segment revenue 23%.
* **Accounting effects to watch:**
  * The personal-loan charge-off rate of 2.62% (Q2 2026) is lowered by selling late-stage delinquent loans. Excluding those sales, the all-in rate is about 3.7%.
  * FY2025's 8.5% effective tax rate ($44.5M on ~$526M pretax) rises to about 22% in 2026 guidance, which slows reported EPS growth.

## Cost Structure

* **Interest expense:** $352M in Q2 2026, of which deposits were $320M. Deposits were over 90% of average liabilities. The average deposit rate was 156bp below warehouse funding, which the company estimates saves ~$713M a year.
* **Credit costs:** most loans are at fair value, so expected losses run through fair-value marks rather than a provision. The provision ($13.8M in Q2 2026) covers only the small amortized-cost book (credit card and others, $1.28B net).
* **Sales and marketing:** $1.095B in FY2025 (30% of revenue; 30% in FY2024). This is the largest operating cost and pays for brand building and member acquisition.
* **Technology, product and G&A:** these include stock-based compensation of $262M (7.3% of FY2025 revenue).
* **Margins:**
  * *Adjusted EBITDA:* 29% in FY2025 ($1.054B on $3.591B adjusted net revenue) and 30% in Q2 2026. 2026 guidance is ~$1.6B, a 33-34% margin.
  * *Adjusted net income:* 2026 guidance is ~$825M, a ~17% margin.
  * *Segment contribution:* Lending ~55%, Financial Services 46-51%, Technology Platform 14% (down from 30%).
* **Scalability:** strong in Financial Services, where FY2025 contribution margin rose from 37% to 51% as revenue grew 88% and costs 49%. Weaker in Lending, where growth needs balance sheet and capital, and the contribution margin slipped from 60% to 55%.

## Capital Intensity

* **Physical capex is small:** the 10-Q says capital expenditures have been "less significant relative to our operating and financing cash flows."
* **The real capital need is regulatory equity behind loans.**
  * Risk-weighted assets rose from $37.2B (December 2025) to $48.7B (June 2026).
  * Company CET1 fell from 22.8% to 18.7%; the bank's is 15.2%. The well-capitalized minimum is 6.5%, and the minimum plus buffer is 7.0%.
  * SoFi raised ~$1.6B of equity in December 2025 and January 2026 (57.75M shares at $27.50). Total equity rose from $6.5B to $10.5B during 2025.
* **Cash flow:**
  * Operating cash flow is negative every year (-$3.74B in FY2025) because loans originated for sale are classified as operating. It is not a useful measure of efficiency here.
  * The better measures are returns on tangible equity and tangible book growth. Tangible book per share was $7.34 at June 30, 2026, up from $4.72 a year earlier (+56%). Most of the $2.62 rise came from the ~$1.6B equity raise at $27.50; trailing earnings explain only about $0.40-0.50 of it. Annualized H1 2026 adjusted net income of ~$654M is a return on average tangible equity of ~7%.
* **Liquidity (June 30, 2026):**
  * $13.7B available: cash $3.1B, securities $3.5B, undrawn warehouse lines $5.7B, FHLB $1.2B.
  * About 98% of deposits are insured. Time deposits due within a year are $4.4B (up from $1.2B).
* **Debt:** $3.3B, consisting of warehouse lines, the revolver and convertible notes. The $862.5M of 1.25% 2029 notes are convertible (an ~43M-share dilution effect in Q2 diluted EPS). The 2026 notes matured October 15, 2026, settled in cash up to principal.
* **Interest-rate sensitivity:** liability-sensitive. +100bp cuts net interest income by $159M a year and the fair value of rate-sensitive assets by $1.04B. -100bp adds $161M and $1.23B.

## Growth Drivers

1. **Personal lending and the Loan Platform Business (structural with a cyclical swing).**
   * *Scale:* personal-loan originations were $10.7B in Q2 2026, including $3.1B through LPB. Total originations were $14.8B, a record, up $2.6B from Q1.
   * *Why LPB matters:* it lets SoFi earn fees on loans that never use its capital. Loan buyers' appetite is cyclical, though, and it is tied to private-credit fund flows.
2. **Deposits and cross-buy (structural).**
   * Deposits grew $5.3B in Q2 2026 alone.
   * Each deposit dollar funds loans more cheaply than warehouse lines.
   * Cross-buy (51%) and products per member (1.54) are rising, so each member costs less to acquire relative to what they buy.
3. **New products (structural, early):**
   * *Home loans:* originations were a record $1.39B in Q2 2026.
   * *Other launches:* small-business loans, crypto and SoFiUSD, SoFi Plus subscriptions, and the Peach Finance acquisition for Tech Solutions.
4. **Medium-term targets (management, January 2026):** adjusted net revenue growing at least 30% a year and adjusted EPS growing 38-42% a year, both from 2025 to 2028. From FY2025 adjusted EPS of $0.39, that implies 2028 adjusted EPS of about $1.03-1.12 (this report's arithmetic). Long-term ROTCE target: 20-30%.
5. **Cyclical factors:**
   * Credit is benign: vintages from Q4 2022 to Q3 2025 have 4.68% cumulative net losses with 35% of principal still outstanding, against 6.43% for the 2017 vintage at the same point.
   * Rates are a headwind. The 10-year Treasury is above 5%, and higher discount rates mark down fair-value loans.

## Competitive Edge

* **Bank charter and deposit funding:**
  * *What it is:* SoFi Bank, N.A. has been regulated by the OCC since 2022. Its national charter makes SoFi exempt from the state usury caps that hit non-bank fintech lenders ([[fintech-usury-tila-regulatory-exposure-2026]]).
  * *What it gives SoFi:* deposits cut funding costs by ~156bp against warehouse lines. Non-bank rivals such as Upstart cannot match that.
* **Scale in prime personal loans:** $10.7B of personal-loan originations in Q2 2026, against $4.2B at Upstart and $3.1B at Happen (formerly LendingClub) in the same quarter (company figures via Zacks). The U.S. personal-loan market is roughly $280B in balances (secondary source). SoFi's $26.1B on-balance-sheet principal is about 9% of that.
* **Brand and member funnel:** 15.8M members, 10.4% unaided awareness, and rising cross-buy. This is a real distribution advantage, but SoFi pays for it with marketing at 30% of revenue.
* **Switching costs:** moderate. Direct-deposit and multi-product members are sticky, but personal-loan borrowers shop on rate.
* **Durability evidence (mixed):**
  * *For:* Financial Services margins expanded (37% to 51%), deposit growth is steady, and SoFi has been GAAP-profitable for eleven straight quarters through Q2 2026 (the Q4 2025 release counted nine).
  * *Against:* ROTCE is still only ~7-9%. Technology Platform revenue fell 23% after one client left. Lending margins are slipping (60% to 55%). Much of the growth depends on loan-buyer demand and fair-value marks.
* **Moat assessment:** narrow and still forming. The charter and deposit franchise are real advantages. The rest of the moat (brand, cross-buy) has yet to show up in bank-level returns, and the 20-30% ROTCE target is far above today's high single digits.
