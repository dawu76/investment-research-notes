## Executive Summary

Affirm Holdings, Inc. is a leading financial technology platform operating a two-sided digital payment network and next-generation consumer underwriting engine. Founded by PayPal co-founder Max Levchin, Affirm replaces legacy, opaque revolving credit card products with transparent, fixed point-of-sale (POS) installment loans featuring zero late fees, zero compounding interest, and deterministic repayment schedules.^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/afrm-1-document-sources.md]

The company monetizes through a diversified model spanning merchant discount rates (MDR), consumer simple-interest income, card interchange on the Affirm Card, gain-on-sale premiums to institutional capital partners, and loan servicing fees. The core unit economic driver is **Revenue Less Transaction Costs (RLTC)**, which measures net revenue after funding costs, credit loss provisions, processing fees, and loan purchase commitments. Affirm has navigated the highest interest rate environment in two decades, executing an operational turnaround from cash-burning hypergrowth to full GAAP net profitability ($52.2M net income in FY2025; run-rate >$340M in FY2026) while scaling Gross Merchandise Volume (GMV) toward $48B. Growth is propelled by the viral adoption of the hybrid Affirm Card, native embedded checkout distribution via Apple Pay and [[shop]] (Shopify), and an asset-light $28.2B institutional funding marketplace.^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/afrm-1-document-sources.md]

**Synthesis:** *Affirm is transitioning from a cyclical point-of-sale checkout feature into an institutional-grade consumer payments and lending infrastructure platform, generating compounding RLTC margins by capturing everyday offline wallet spend and leveraging AI SKU-level underwriting precision over legacy revolving credit card banks.*

Affirm provides transparent, flexible point-of-sale financing and digital payment solutions designed to increase merchant conversion and basket size while giving consumers clear budgeting discipline.

### Core Products & Services
1. **Pay-in-4:** Short-duration financing where the purchase price is split into 4 equal bi-weekly payments. Always 0% APR for consumers; subsidized entirely by the merchant via higher merchant discount rates (sub-$250 average order values).
2. **Monthly Installments:** Fixed-term installment loans ranging from 3 to 60 months for higher-ticket purchases ($250 to $10,000+). Offered either at 0% APR (merchant subsidized) or simple, non-compounding interest with risk-based APRs (typically 10% to 36%).
3. **Affirm Card:** A physical and digital Visa debit/credit hybrid card that links directly to a user’s existing bank account. Cardholders can choose to "Pay Now" with zero fees via standard ACH/debit rails or split eligible purchases into installment plans before or after swiping via the Affirm mobile app.
4. **Affirm App & Marketplace:** A direct-to-consumer digital commerce portal featuring personalized merchant promotions, search and discovery, rewards, high-yield savings accounts, and tailored credit lines.
5. **Affirm for Business (B2B):** Commercial installment financing enabling small-to-medium businesses (SMBs) to finance inventory, equipment, and operational procurement at checkout.

### Bank Partner Origination Architecture
Affirm operates through an industrial bank partner model to originate loans nationwide. Primary FDIC-insured originating bank partners include **Cross River Bank** (New Jersey state-chartered) and **Celtic Bank** (Utah state-chartered).^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/afrm-1-document-sources.md] 
- Loans originated by bank partners are subsequently purchased by Affirm or directly by institutional forward-flow partners under pre-agreed forward flow agreements.
- This bank-partner structure enables Affirm to export uniform interest rates across all 50 states under Section 27 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (FDIA), insulating the platform from individual state usury caps while maintaining strict compliance oversight.

### Customer Profile & Target Demographics
- **Consumers (24.1M+ Active Users):** Skewed historically toward Millennials and Gen Z who actively avoid traditional revolving credit cards due to punitive compounding interest, compounding penalty fees, and hidden charges. Demographics are broadening rapidly into affluent, prime consumers (average FICO >700) driven by Apple Pay checkout integration and luxury/travel merchant additions. Average transactions per active consumer has climbed to **5.2x annually** (up from 3.6x in FY2022).^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/afrm-1-document-sources.md]
- **Merchants (419,000+ Active Merchants):** Ranging from retail giants (Amazon, Shopify, Target, Walmart) to category leaders across consumer electronics, home furnishings, sporting goods, and travel (Expedia, Peloton, Williams-Sonoma).
- **Economic Value Proposition for Merchants:** Affirm drives measurable ROI: 20%–30%+ increases in checkout conversion, 60%–85% increases in Average Order Value (AOV), and higher repeat purchasing without requiring merchants to bear credit underwriting or default risk.

## How They Make Money

Affirm operates a two-sided payment network, capturing economics from both merchants and consumers through five primary revenue streams.

```
                      Affirm Revenue Streams (FY2025 / FY2026 Mix)
   ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
   │ ■ Interest Income (46%)                                                 │
   │ ■ Merchant Network Fees / MDR (31%)                                      │
   │ ■ Gain on Sale of Loans (11%)                                            │
   │ ■ Card Network Interchange (8%)                                          │
   │ ■ Servicing Fees (4%)                                                    │
   └──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

| Revenue Stream | % of Total Rev | Economic Mechanism | Key Pricing Dynamics |
|:---------------|:---------------|:-------------------|:---------------------|
| **Interest Income** | ~45% – 48% | Simple, fixed-rate interest earned on consumer loans retained on balance sheet | 10% to 36% simple APR; calculated on declining principal without late fees or compounding |
| **Merchant Network Revenue** | ~30% – 33% | Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) fee charged to merchants per transaction | 1.5% to 3.0% for standard checkout; 4.0% to 7.0%+ for 0% APR merchant-subsidized promotions |
| **Gain on Sale of Loans** | ~10% – 12% | Net premium realized when selling originated whole loans to institutional capital partners | 101% to 103% of par value, reflecting credit quality and spread over SOFR |
| **Card Network Revenue** | ~6% – 8% | Interchange fees received when consumers swipe Affirm Card or virtual Visa cards | Standard interchange fees (~1.5% to 2.2%) split with payment networks |
| **Servicing Income** | ~3% – 4% | Contractual asset servicing fees for managing and collecting sold loan portfolios | ~100 to 150 bps annualized fee on outstanding off-balance-sheet principal |

### Key Economic Metric: Revenue Less Transaction Costs (RLTC)
Affirm evaluates its underlying gross unit profitability via **Revenue Less Transaction Costs (RLTC)**:

$$\text{RLTC} = \text{Total Revenue} - \left( \text{Credit Loss Provisions} + \text{Funding Costs} + \text{Processing \& Servicing Fees} + \text{Loss/Gain on Loan Commitments} \right)$$

- **RLTC as % of GMV:** Sits stably in the **3.75% to 4.00%** range (against long-term target band of 3.50%–4.20%).
- **RLTC as % of Total Revenue:** Represents **~42.4%** ($1.80B RLTC on $4.25B revenue), reflecting healthy gross cash conversion after accounting for all variable credit and balance sheet costs.^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/afrm-1-document-sources.md]

This short loss realization window allows Affirm to detect underperforming merchant cohorts within 60–90 days and dynamically adjust risk cutoffs.
- **Discretionary Category Exposure:** Affirm’s GMV skews toward consumer electronics, home furnishings, sporting goods, and travel. These discretionary categories are sensitive to macroeconomic conditions and tariff shocks, making real-time SKU risk scoring essential to protect unit margins.

## Capital Intensity, Funding Marketplace & Rate Sensitivity

Affirm operates a hybrid capital marketplace, distributing credit risk across three primary funding mechanisms to maintain high capital turnover and asset-light operations.

```
                     Affirm Capital Marketplace Structure
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ Total Capital Funding Capacity: $28.2 Billion (130+ Institutional Investors) │
 └──────────────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────┘
                                        │
        ┌───────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┐
        │                               │                               │
        ▼                               ▼                               ▼
┌──────────────────────┐    ┌──────────────────────┐    ┌──────────────────────┐
│  Warehouse Credit    │    │ Forward-Flow Whole   │    │ Asset-Backed         │
│  Facilities (~25%)   │    │ Loan Sales (~40%)    │    │ Securitizations (~35%)
│ Floating SOFR + 200b │    │ 100% Off-Balance S呵 │    │ Revolving & Static   │
│ Committed bank lines │    │ Instant gain-on-sale │    │ AAA / A rated notes  │
└──────────────────────┘    └──────────────────────┘    └──────────────────────┘
```

1. **Funding Breakdown ($28.2B Capacity):**
   - **Forward-Flow Whole Loan Sales (~40% of capacity):** Direct off-balance sheet sales to institutional investors (pension funds, insurance managers, private credit funds). Affirm earns upfront Gain on Sale (~101–103% of par) and ongoing ~100–150 bps servicing fees with zero balance sheet credit retention.
   - **Asset-Backed Securitizations (~35% of capacity):** Multi-billion revolving and static ABS trusts rated AAA/A by Fitch and S&P. Overcollateralization and credit enhancement covenants ensure low-cost term liquidity.^[investing-fundamentals/company-analyses/afrm-1-document-sources.md]
   - **Warehouse Credit Facilities (~25% of capacity):** Committed revolving credit lines from major commercial banks (Morgan Stanley, Barclays, Goldman Sachs) providing origination liquidity at floating SOFR + 175–250 bps.
2. **Capital Turnover & Portfolio Velocity:**
   - The weighted average duration of Affirm’s loan book is **~4.8 months**.
   - With an average portfolio duration of ~0.4 years, capital turns over **~2.3x per year** (contrasted with ultra-short payroll liquidity models like `[[dave]]` which turn over 35x+ annually, and multi-year legacy revolving cards). Supporting $48B in annualized GMV requires ~$20B in active systemwide principal, but Affirm’s on-balance-sheet equity capital deployed for risk retention is only ~$2.5B, yielding an unlevered Return on Tangible Equity (ROTE) exceeding 25%.
3. **Two-Sided Interest Rate Repricing Gap:**
   - **Asset Side (Loans):** Consumer loans are fixed-rate installment contracts (average duration ~4.8 months) with APRs between 0% and 36%.
   - **Liability Side (Funding):** Warehouse credit facilities and floating-rate ABS notes reprice immediately with benchmark SOFR.
   - **Asymmetric Rate Response:** When the Federal Reserve cuts interest rates, Affirm’s funding expense declines immediately, while fixed-rate consumer APRs take 4–6 months to reprice downward, yielding a **direct +30 to +50 bps expansion in RLTC margin**. Conversely, during rate hikes, Affirm mitigates margin compression by raising merchant MDRs and lifting consumer APR caps (e.g., from 30% to 36% in 2023).

## Growth Drivers

1. **Affirm Card Top-of-Wallet Penetration:** The Affirm Card transitions Affirm from an occasional checkout button into an everyday payment method. Card GMV is compounding at >100% YoY, expanding transaction frequency across in-person grocery, fuel, travel, and dining.
2. **Apple Pay Embedded Distribution:** Native iOS integration allows millions of iPhone users to select Affirm installment financing with Face ID across all Apple Pay web and app checkouts without requiring individual merchant integrations.
3. **Federal Reserve Rate Easing Windfall:** Lower benchmark rates reduce warehouse borrowing costs and widen securitization spreads, expanding RLTC margin per dollar of GMV.
4. **International Expansion (UK & Europe):** Expanding into the UK and EEA creates geographic diversification and leverages existing global merchant contracts.
5. **Affirm for Business (B2B POS):** Financing SMB procurement orders ($2,000–$25,000 basket sizes) unlocks an enormous addressable B2B market with attractive MDR take rates.

1. **Proprietary SKU-Level Machine Learning Underwriting Engine:** Unlike credit card issuers that evaluate static FICO scores and assign revolving credit limits without item-level visibility, Affirm evaluates each transaction in real time (<100ms) with item-level SKU data (e.g., airfare vs electronics vs apparel). This allows Affirm to optimize approval rates while keeping 60+ day delinquencies at ~1.60%.
2. **Two-Sided Network Scale & Integration Lock-In:** As 24M+ consumers join Affirm, merchants are compelled to integrate Affirm to capture conversion; as more merchants join, Affirm Card utility expands. Deep checkout integrations with Shopify (`[[shop]]`), Amazon, and Apple Pay create substantial competitive moats against sub-scale fintechs.
3. **Moat Vulnerabilities & Counter-Theses:**
   - *Bank Partner Single-Point-of-Failure:* Reliance on Cross River Bank and Celtic Bank creates regulatory vulnerability if FDIC enforcement actions impact bank-fintech partnerships.
   - *Platform Disintermediation:* If Apple or Amazon were to internalize balance sheet lending, Affirm’s distribution could face headwinds (though Apple previously shuttered its in-house Apple Pay Later product in favor of partnering with Affirm).
   - *Valuation & Market Structure Context:* In light of analytical frameworks on `[[valuations]]`, `[[pvgo]]`, and `[[funding-short-squeeze]]`, Affirm’s operational leverage and capital efficiency support an expanding terminal value multiple.
