## Bull Case

*Independent bull analyst, verbatim. The analyst used only the memo's data (no web search); "derived" marks the analyst's own arithmetic.*

### 1. Secular TAM and structural market position

- **Penetration is low.** E-commerce is 4-29% of retail across SEA and Brazil, against 52% in China. Shopee's GMV still grew +28.4% in Q2 2026 to $38.3B, at a $127.4B FY2025 base, so the runway is long.
- **Shopee holds a dominant share.** It had ~52% of SEA GMV in 2024 and more than 50% in every SEA market except Indonesia (46%), plus more than 50% in Taiwan. Orders grew +27.5% to 4.2B in Q2. Share is being taken on volume, not just on price.
- **The newest entrant is fading.** Temu's ASEAN MAU fell 22% y/y in 3Q26. Shopee and TikTok both raised fees in Q4 2025 and again in 2026. Leaders raising fees together does not look like a price war.
- **Monee's lending TAM is still opening up.** The loan book grew +62% to $11.1B while 90+ day NPLs held at 1.0%. In a region where most people have little access to credit, the lender with first-party transaction data is underwriting growth without loosening standards.

### 2. Revenue quality

- **Take rate is rising.** Core marketplace revenue rose +65.6% to $4.26B on +28.4% GMV, for a core take rate of ~11.1%. MS expects 11.3% in Q3. Shopee Brazil raised its fixed commission from R$4.00 to R$4.50 on Oct 1, 2026, which helps that trend.
- **Gross margin is holding up as the mix shifts.** It was 45.6% in Q2 2026, against 44.7% for FY2025, while lower-margin lending and logistics grew fast.
- **Credit is clean.** The 90+ NPL ratio was 1.0% on a loan book that grew 62%. Provisions rose +71.5% to $555M, ahead of loan growth. That looks like conservative reserving, not a sign of deteriorating loans.
- **Cash is real.** FY2025 operating cash flow was $5.02B, against $1.61B of net income (3.1x, derived). Net cash and investments are ~$11.5B, about 23% of the ~$50B market cap (derived). H1 2026 buybacks were $578M, which annualizes to ~2.3% of the market cap (derived).
- **Garena is stable.** Q2 bookings grew +15.5% to $763.5M, with 666M QAU and 68.1M payers (10.2%). The 3Q26 Sensor Tower read was -5%, but September was +15% y/y with MAU +23%.

### 3. Platform and product expansion (Q2 2026)

- **Content commerce:** content-driven orders reached 25% of SEA physical orders, up 50%. That is Shopee's direct answer to TikTok, and it is working at scale.
- **Logistics:** instant-delivery cost per order fell 20% in Indonesia. Management says the warehouse spending pays off by Q4 and sets a higher bar for 2027.
- **Brazil:** MAU grew +11%, against MELI's +16%, after fee increases. Shopee is holding user growth in a market it entered against a strong local incumbent.
- **Sea Cloud:** this is a new business line built on infrastructure Sea already owns, so the launch does not need big new capex. That base is ~25k GPUs, 150k+ servers, 40+ data centers and more than 100MW of capacity, with ~500 staff already in place for two years.
- **Monee listing option:** Monee earned $1.02B of adj EBITDA in FY2025 at a 26.9% margin. DBS values it at $18-22B, while the share price implies ~$6B. A listing, even in 2027 or later, would put a market price on that gap.

### 4. Why "reinvestment never ends" helps the bull case

- **The stock already prices something worse than that scenario.** At $78 the market implies ~$4.5B of 2028 adj EBITDA. Suppose Shopee stays at 0.7% EBITDA/GMV on MS's $224B 2028 GMV: that gives $1.57B. Add Garena held flat at ~$1.9B and Monee at ~$1.8B (the Q2 run-rate of $1.15B growing toward DBS's ~$1.5B 2027 figure), less ~$0.2B of "other" losses. The total is ~$5.1B (derived), about 13% above what the price implies.
- **The spending is producing results.** Over the same period, S&M rose +64.5% and core marketplace revenue rose +65.6%. Q2 adj EBITDA of $917M beat the ~$874M estimate even with that spending.
- **Sea can fund this longer than its rivals.** $5.02B of operating cash flow plus $11.5B of net cash pays for each new program internally. Temu's 22% MAU decline shows what happens to a rival that can't keep spending.
- **Holding margins down protects share.** Shopee kept more than 50% of most markets while choosing a 0.7% margin. Management calls 2-3% long-term "quite within reach", so that upside is an option that costs nothing at today's price.
- **Shopee is priced at almost nothing.** The SOTP values Shopee at ~$5-15B, or ~0.09x GMV. The bear scenario is mostly priced in already.

### Summary

SE trades at ~7.5x 2027 adj EBITDA, against a 3-year average of ~18x NTM EV/EBITDA, after falling 39% YTD. A CEO memo moved the stock 9% while peers were flat. The memo said nothing about capex, timing or venue. The fundamentals still show +48% revenue growth, a rising take rate, 1.0% NPLs and 3.1x cash conversion. Even if Shopee margins stay at 0.7% indefinitely, the bear scenario's 2028 EBITDA comes in above what $78 implies. The Nov 10 Q3 print is the first test: watch for a take rate of 11.3%, adj EBITDA of ~$1.0B, and Shopee on track for its $1B FY2026 target.

## Bear Case

*Independent bear analyst, verbatim. The analyst used only the memo's data (no web search); "derived" marks the analyst's own arithmetic.*

### 1. Reinvestment never ends

- Shopee's adj EBITDA/GMV was 0.69% in FY25, 0.58% in 3Q25 and 0.67% in 2Q26. It has not moved in six quarters. MS needs 0.85% in 2027 and 1.1% in 2028 to reach $6.35B of 2028 EBITDA.
- Q2 Shopee revenue rose $1.82B y/y, but segment adj EBITDA rose only about $28M ($227M to $255M, derived). That is roughly a 1.5% incremental margin. S&M grew 64.5% to $1.66B, which is 21.3% of revenue.
- Each threat brings a new program. In March 2026 management guided Shopee EBITDA "no lower than 2025", which on ~25% GMV growth implies about 0.55% of GMV, and the stock fell 17% that day. DBS (Oct 1) then flagged quick commerce, VIP and the Migoo AI app, and put 2027 e-commerce EBITDA 5% below consensus.
- Sea Cloud is the newest program. Sea already runs ~25k GPUs across 40+ data centers drawing over 100MW, plans to adopt Vera Rubin, and has disclosed no capex figure. "Other services" already lost $46M in Q2 (+236% y/y), and the memo's bear case carries -$0.65B for that line. The market took $5B off the stock (-9.1%) on the day of the memo while peers were flat.

### 2. Competitive displacement and pricing pressure

- TikTok's ASEAN MAU grew 15% vs Shopee's 5% (Sept 2026). J&T's SEA parcel volume rose 74% in 4Q25 on TikTok volume, and Momentum Works projects TikTok will rival Shopee's parcel volume in 2026. Content plus checkout takes demand before it reaches Shopee search.
- Pinduoduo became the #1 shopping download in Malaysia and dropped its free-shipping minimum in Aug 2026. DBS estimates 10-13% of Shopee GMV is exposed and puts Q3 GMV 2-3% below consensus.
- In Brazil, Shopee MAU grew 11% vs Mercado Libre's 16%. From Oct 2 to Oct 21, MELI gained 9.3% while SE fell 18%.
- The take-rate gains depend on ads, and AI shopping agents such as Meta's Muse go around ad placement and take their own cut. Shopee earns 0.67% of GMV vs Alibaba's ~2.5%, so it has little margin to give up.

### 3. Non-volume revenue hides slower organic growth

- Q2 revenue grew $2.53B y/y ($5.26B to $7.79B). Core marketplace revenue grew 65.6% on GMV growth of 28.4%, so the implied take rate rose from ~8.6% to ~11.1% of GMV (derived). Had core revenue grown in line with GMV, it would have been ~$3.30B instead of $4.26B. About $0.96B, 38% of group revenue growth, came from fee increases, and those have a ceiling.
- Monee added $0.52B of revenue (21% of growth) by growing the loan book 62% to $11.1B. Provisions rose about $231M to $555M, and Monee adj EBITDA rose only about $33M (derived). Margin fell from 28.9% to 20.5%. This growth is bought with credit risk, and Indonesia's 40% DTI cap on BNPL arrives in 2027.
- Remove both items and group revenue grew about 24% ($4.38B to $5.43B, derived), not 48%. Group adj EBITDA grew 10.6%.
- The volume trend is down. GMV growth goes from 28% in Q2 to MS estimates of 25% in Q3, 20% in 2027 and 16% in 2028, and MS expects a 3-4% FX headwind in Q3 (USD/IDR +9% y/y).

### 4. Multiple compression

- The 7.5x 2027 multiple assumes 29% EBITDA growth ($3.975B to $5.118B). Suppose GMV grows below 18% and adj EBITDA grows 8%: 2027 EBITDA is then ~$4.29B. At 6x, a low-growth EM retail/conglomerate level, EV is $25.8B. Adding the $11.5B of net cash gives about $58 per share.
- The net cash deserves a haircut. It includes $2.41B of restricted cash, $1.05B of current convertible notes, and capital Monee needs to fund 62% book growth. Taking $3B off gives about $54.
- Earnings risk is worse. Morningstar cut net income estimates 28-36% after raising its tax assumption to 38%. Cutting MS's 2027 EPS of $4.21 by 28% gives $3.03, and 15x that is about $45. Sea has beaten EPS in 0 of its last 8 quarters.
- The 3-year average of ~18x EV/EBITDA was set in a high-growth period and is not a fair reference for 16% GMV growth. The governance discount should widen: Forrest Li controls ~59% of votes, Tencent's 17-20% stake is an overhang, the CFO filed a 10b5-1 sale, and the CEO is starting capital-intensive side businesses without disclosed budgets.
- The memo's bear case, $4.0B of 2028 EBITDA at 7x, gives about $55. Today's price already implies $4.5B, 29% below MS.

### Summary

Sea's revenue growth looks much better than its earnings. About 60% of Q2 revenue growth came from fee increases and lending, and Shopee and Monee together converted only ~$60M of $2.3B in added segment revenue into EBITDA. The margin rise from 0.67% to 1.1% that sell-side targets around $150 require has not happened in six quarters, and each new threat (TikTok, Pinduoduo, MELI, AI agents) comes with a spending response, now including a GPU cloud. If GMV slows toward 16-18% and EBITDA growth stays near 10%, a 6x multiple plus a haircut on cash points to $45-58, or 25-40% below today's price.

## Arbitrator Assessment

*Arbitrator, verbatim. Before ruling, the arbitrator received both reports plus neutral arithmetic checks:*
- *the VAS (logistics) revenue decline that may explain part of the take-rate jump*
- *the bear's double-counted tax cut*
- *the bull's "$5.1B" not being the memo's full bear case*
- *the bull's Brazil and Temu points cutting the other way*

**Verdict in one line:** reinvestment is real and margin expansion has not shown up yet, but the price already discounts most of that. A starter position is justified; full conviction is not.

### Shared Facts, Divergent Interpretations

| Fact | Bull interpretation | Bear interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| Core marketplace revenue +65.6% against GMV +28.4% (implied take rate ~8.6% to ~11.1%) | Pricing power. Leaders are raising fees together, and Brazil's R$4.50 commission adds more. | About $0.96B of growth (38% of the group's) came from fee hikes, and those have a ceiling. |
| Shopee adj EBITDA/GMV of ~0.67-0.69%, flat for six quarters | A deliberate choice that protects >50% share. The 2-3% long-term margin is a free option. | A structural trap. MS needs 0.85% in 2027 and 1.1% in 2028, and nothing shows that happening. |
| S&M +64.5%; Shopee and Monee segment EBITDA up only ~$61M on ~$2.34B of added revenue | Spending is matched by revenue (+65.6%) and Q2 EBITDA beat. | The incremental margin is ~1.5% at Shopee, so the growth is being bought. |
| Monee loan book +62% to $11.1B, 90+ NPL 1.0%, provisions +71.5% to $555M | Clean credit and conservative reserving. | Credit-funded growth, with margin down from 28.9% to 20.5% and Indonesia's DTI cap coming in 2027. |
| $78 implies ~$4.5B of 2028 adj EBITDA; ~$11.5B net cash | The market prices in worse than flat margins forever, and the cash pays for a longer fight than rivals can afford. | The price is already 29% below MS, the cash needs a ~$3B haircut, and the memo's bear case is $55. |

### The 3 Critical Divergence Assumptions

**1. Shopee margin trajectory**
- **Bull version:** Warehouse spending pays off by Q4 2026. Margin can rise toward 1%+, and if it doesn't, flat margins are already priced.
- **Bear version:** New programs keep arriving (quick commerce, VIP, Migoo, Sea Cloud), so EBITDA/GMV stays at or below 0.7%.
- **Why it's the crux:** This is the gap between the MS path ($6.35B of 2028 EBITDA, ~$112 base) and the bear path ($4.0B, ~$55). Nothing else in the model moves value as much.
- **Verdict weight: Lean bear, moderate.**
  - Six flat quarters and a ~1.5% incremental Shopee margin in Q2 are hard evidence.
  - Management's own FY2026 Shopee target of $1B against ~$160B+ of FY2026 GMV works out to ~0.62%. Their guidance implies a lower margin this year, not a higher one.
  - Sea Cloud adds a cost line with no capex disclosed, and "other" already lost $46M in Q2 (+236%).
  - The bull's only counter is a promise ("pays off by Q4") that has not been tested yet.

**2. Fee increases stick without losing share**
- **Bull version:** Two rounds of fee hikes and GMV still grew +28.4%, with orders +27.5%. Temu is fading.
- **Bear version:** TikTok, Pinduoduo and MELI are taking share, so fee gains will reverse or will need offsetting spend.
- **Why it's the crux:** If monetization holds while GMV grows above 20%, the revenue line carries the stock even at flat margins. If GMV slows to 16-18% at the same time, every scenario moves toward the bear case.
- **Verdict weight: Lean bull, slight.**
  - The deciding fact is that GMV grew 28.4% after fee hikes. Rival MAU figures only predict GMV; this is GMV itself.
  - The orchestrator's notes cut both ways:
    - Value-added services (logistics) revenue fell 9%, so part of the core take-rate jump is probably reclassification. That weakens the bull's "11.1%" and the bear's "$0.96B from fee hikes" equally.
    - The cleaner measure is still up. Total Shopee revenue/GMV rose from ~12.7% to ~14.6% (derived: $5.59B/$38.3B against $3.77B/$29.8B).
    - The bull's competitive evidence is weaker than it looks:
      - Brazil MAU +11% against MELI's +16% is share loss.
      - Temu's MAU drop reflects PDD moving users to the Pinduoduo app, not retreat. That app is #1 in Malaysia, and DBS puts 10-13% of Shopee GMV at risk there.
  - This lean flips if Q3 GMV growth lands below ~23% (DBS: 2-3% below consensus).

**3. Where the valuation floor sits**
- **Bull version:** Even if reinvestment never ends, 2028 EBITDA is ~$5.1B, above the $4.5B the price implies, so the downside is priced in.
- **Bear version:** 6x on slower growth, with a haircut on cash, gives $45-58.
- **Why it's the crux:** It decides whether being wrong on assumption 1 costs 10% or 40%.
- **Verdict weight: Lean bull, moderate.** Both sides overstate their numbers:
  - The bull's $5.1B uses MS's base-case GMV and Monee figures. With the memo's own bear inputs the figure is ~$4.0B, so the floor is ~$55, not $78.
  - The bear's $45 applies Morningstar's tax-driven cut to an MS EPS figure that already includes the March tax change, so it double-counts. Drop that number.
  - The bear's $54-58 rests on 6x for a business with ~23% of its market cap in net cash and a Monee unit DBS values at $18-22B, against ~$6B implied in the price.
  - Rerunning the memo scenarios with more weight on the bear case (45/45/10 instead of 30/50/20) still gives ~$92 expected value against $78. The downside to the bear case is ~-29%.
  - So the stock is cheap against its probability-weighted value even after conceding assumption 1. The bull is wrong that downside is "priced in", though.

> **Reviewer's note (orchestrator, added after the ruling):** the ~$92 in assumption 3 is an **end-2027** value (0.45 × $55 + 0.45 × $112 + 0.10 × $165 ≈ $92). Discounted to today at the memo's 11% required return (÷1.133), it is **~$81**, only ~4% above $77.93. At bear-heavy weights the stock is roughly fairly priced, not cheap. The memo's original 30/50/20 weights give ~$93 today. So the "Lean bull, moderate" verdict on assumption 3 holds only if the base case keeps at least ~50% weight. That in turn depends on assumption 1, where the arbitrator leans bear. Treat assumption 3 as closer to *Too close to call* until the Q3 print.
>
> Separately, management framed the US$1B FY2026 Shopee figure as a milestone to "achieve" (a floor). MS models ~$1.07B (0.67% of GMV). The ~0.62% the arbitrator derives is the margin the floor implies, not a forecast.

### Closing Assessment

To own SE, an investor has to be right that Shopee's EBITDA/GMV stays at or above ~0.65% while GMV keeps growing above 20%. Margin expansion is not required, but new programs like Sea Cloud must not push the margin lower. At $78, that outcome alone justifies the price, but the evidence leans against expansion and the floor is ~$55, not today's price.

That supports a starter position, about one-third of full size, with defined downside of roughly 30%. Add to half size after the Q3 print (est. Nov 10) if:
- Shopee EBITDA/GMV is at least 0.68%
- GMV growth is at least 24%
- Sea Cloud spending is bounded or disclosed

Go to full size only after two consecutive quarters of Shopee margin above 0.75%. Exit or cut if FY2026 Shopee EBITDA misses the $1B target or Sea Cloud capex turns out to be large and undisclosed.
