#### 2. Energy Commodity Resurgence & Stagflationary Risks
* **Overview:** Commodity researchers analyze supply-side tightness in crude oil, upstream depletion rates, and the threat of mid-70s style second-wave stagflation.
* **Key Observations & Data:**
  * **"Oil Wants Revenge" (Jason Perz - *Against All Odds*):**
    * *Collapsing Low-Energy Assumptions:* Decades of low energy cost assumptions are ending. Upstream depletion rates, Persian Gulf supply friction, and refining capacity constraints create a structural energy price squeeze ("Oil Wants Revenge") that directly feeds into global transportation and logistics COGS.
  * **1970s Style Stagflationary Impulse:**
    * *Fed Rate-Cut Prohibition:* Spiking energy input costs paired with $35T+ US federal debt create a classic stagflationary impulse (slowing GDP growth + sticky 3.5%+ inflation). This strips the Federal Reserve of its ability to execute rate cuts without triggering currency debasement.
* **Sources:** Against All Odds Research (*Oil Wants Revenge*); Kaoboy Musings (*Weekly KAOS, 7/24/26*); ChinaTalk (*WarTalk: Groundhog Day in Iran*).
