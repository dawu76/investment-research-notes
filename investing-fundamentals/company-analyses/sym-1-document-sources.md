# Symbotic Inc. (NASDAQ: SYM) — Document Sources

**Date:** October 9, 2026

> Symbotic went public on June 7, 2022 by merging with SVF Investment Corp. 3, a SoftBank-sponsored SPAC. Its fiscal year ends on the last Saturday of September. The FY2021 10-K below was filed by the SPAC before the merger and covers the shell company, not the operating business; Symbotic's own FY2021 results appear as comparatives in the FY2022 10-K. FY2026 (ended September 26, 2026) results are due in late November 2026, so FY2025 is the latest annual filing.
>
> Symbotic files its 10-Ks as inline XBRL HTML, not PDF. EDGAR links below were checked against the EDGAR filing index on 2026-10-09. The company's IR site (ir.symbotic.com) also hosts PDF renderings, but it returned HTTP 403 to automated requests, so those links are marked unverified.
