Engineer hat ("can I trade this with realistic costs and constraints?") - sometimes we need to be quite precise here, especially if things are marginal or turnover is high.

Architect hat ("how does this fit with everything else?") - obviously it's important to think clearly about how big to trade the different things, but our estimates of risk (volatility and correlation) are noisy, so imprecision is forced on us.
