# Ted Oakley: Not A Normal Market | The Julia La Roche Show [Ted Oakley]

Detailed overview of the interview featuring Ted Oakley, founder of Oxbow Advisors, on *The Julia La Roche Show*, published on July 16, 2026.

---

### I. Executive Summary
Ted Oakley, founder and managing partner of Oxbow Advisors, argues that current markets are highly speculative and structurally imbalanced, driven largely by momentum in a few tech/semiconductor stocks. He warns of a looming generational bear market that could see a 40%+ correction to revert to the mean, while highlighting attractive long-term value in undervalued sectors like energy and gold miners. Oakley advises maintaining high cash levels and short-term Treasuries to weather the correction.

### III. Detailed Key Takeaways

#### 1. The Speculative Tech Bubble and Risk of Correction
Oakley emphasizes that a handful of semiconductor and tech stocks have hijacked the S&P 500, masking significant underlying economic risks.
*   **Extreme Standard Deviation:** The index is trading three standard deviations above its long-term historical mean.
*   **Retail Gambling:** Complacency is exacerbated by retail speculation in single-stock leveraged ETFs and zero-day options (0DTE), which pumps up artificial volatility.

> "We're about three standard deviations higher than the norm. And that's way out of line... you would have at least 40, 45% selling. That gets back to a normal level."
> — *Ted Oakley*

#### 2. Under-allocation to Value and Safety
*   **Demographic Risks:** Older investors (the over-70 demographic owns roughly one-third of all stocks) are dangerously over-allocated to equities, leaving them vulnerable to a sudden correction.
*   **Valuation Discipline:** Oakley warns against abandoning valuation frameworks to chase momentum, drawing parallels to investors buying Intel in late 1999 before it lost 80% of its value.

> "Complacency is at an all-time high... three-quarters of all financial assets are currently held in stocks."
> — *Ted Oakley*

#### 3. Energy and Gold: The True Value Plays
*   **Oil to $100+:** Structural under-investment in fossil fuels will keep supply tight and push crude prices back above $100.
*   **Energy Royalties:** Royalty companies (Northern Oil & Gas, Kimbell Royalty) offer ~10% dividend yields without exposure to capital expenditures.
*   **Gold Miners:** Miners trade at highly attractive free-cash-flow yields, presenting a massive spread relative to the physical gold price.

### IV. Portfolio Positioning & Action Items
*   **Reduce Equity Beta:** Trim high-beta technology and semiconductor positions. Increase cash and short-term Treasuries to preserve capital.
*   **Long Energy Royalties:** Position in Northern Oil & Gas and Kimbell Royalty to capture high dividend yields and protect against inflation.
*   **Accumulate Gold Miners:** Buy Agnico Eagle (AEM) on dips to capture expanding margin spreads as extraction costs remain flat relative to high spot gold prices.
