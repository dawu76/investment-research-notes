# Cloudflare, Inc. (NYSE: NET) — Equity Analyst Report

**Report Date:** May 2026 | **Analyst:** AI Investment Research
**Primary Data Sources:** FY2025 10-K (filed Feb 26, 2026), Q1 2026 Earnings (May 7, 2026), Q4 2025 Earnings (Feb 10, 2026)

---

## Executive Summary

Cloudflare has built one of the most strategically positioned technology platforms in public markets: a globally distributed network running in 330+ cities across 125+ countries that serves simultaneously as a content delivery network, a cybersecurity stack, and a developer compute layer. Every new product built on this shared infrastructure improves the returns on Cloudflare's sunk network cost — the defining characteristic of a platform with compounding economics.

Revenue reached $2.17 billion in FY2025 (+30% YoY) and accelerated to $639.8 million in Q1 2026 (+34% YoY). The company is deeply net retention-positive: 118–120% dollar-based net revenue retention (NRR) indicates that existing customers organically expand their spending. Large enterprise customers (spending $100K+ annually) grew 25% YoY to 4,416 as of Q1 2026 and now represent 72% of total revenue. Gross margins are stable at ~75%. Non-GAAP operating margins are expanding toward 15%, with a clear management path to 20%+ over the medium term.

The company is navigating a significant inflection point: in May 2026, Cloudflare announced 1,100 layoffs (~20% of workforce) to restructure around "agentic AI-first" operations, reflecting conviction that AI will replace certain roles and that capital redeployment toward product and infrastructure is the higher-return use of resources. This is a high-conviction management bet. **Cloudflare is best described as the operating system for the global internet — a platform that every business connecting to the internet increasingly depends on for security, speed, and developer tools.**

## What They Sell and Who Buys

### Products

**Application Services (CDN, Performance, DDoS, WAF):** The original and still-foundational product suite. Cloudflare operates one of the world's largest anycast networks, routing traffic intelligently to minimize latency. Products include content delivery, DDoS mitigation, Web Application Firewall (WAF), bot management, load balancing, and API gateway. These are deployed as a unified cloud service — no hardware required — and are typically purchased as subscriptions with tiered plans.

**Cloudflare One (Zero Trust / SASE):** The enterprise security platform. Cloudflare One converges Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA), Secure Web Gateway (SWG), Cloud Access Security Broker (CASB), remote browser isolation, and Magic WAN (SD-WAN) into a single SASE platform. This is Cloudflare's fastest-growing segment and its largest driver of large enterprise expansion. Cloudflare was recognized as a Visionary in the Gartner 2025 Magic Quadrant for SASE Platforms.

**Developer Platform (Workers, R2, D1, Pages, Workers AI):** A serverless edge computing platform. Cloudflare Workers allows developers to run JavaScript/WASM code at the edge (in 330+ PoPs globally), eliminating cold starts. R2 is S3-compatible object storage with no egress fees. D1 is a distributed SQLite database. Workers AI provides GPU-accelerated inference at the edge. This platform had nearly 4,000% YoY growth in AI usage in Q3 2025 and is a key strategic bet for the next generation of internet application architecture.

**Network Services (Magic Transit, Magic WAN, Magic Firewall):** Network-level security and connectivity services for enterprises running large IP footprints, branch offices, or hybrid networks. These are typically larger ACV contracts and represent Cloudflare's most complex enterprise deployments.

**Cloudflare for AI:** A suite of security, gateway, observability, and rate-limiting tools specifically for AI applications and agents, introduced 2025. Targets enterprises deploying LLMs, RAG pipelines, and autonomous agents.

### Customer Profile

- **SMBs and developers:** Attracted by the free tier; many convert over time to paid plans. The developer community creates a pipeline of future enterprise buyers.
- **Mid-market enterprises:** Procuring WAF, DDoS, and Zero Trust tools, typically $10K–$100K annual spend.
- **Large enterprises ($100K+ ARR):** 4,416 customers as of Q1 2026 (72% of total revenue). 42% of Fortune 500 companies are paying Cloudflare customers.
- **Geography:** U.S.-centric with meaningful international exposure; the company does not break out geography in detail, but the product is globally deployed.

### Customer Motivation

- **Security buyers** need Zero Trust and SASE to replace legacy VPNs and perimeter security in a cloud-first, work-from-anywhere world.
- **Infrastructure/platform buyers** need performant, globally distributed CDN and networking that scales elastically without hardware procurement.
- **Developers** use Workers because it offers the fastest cold-start times among serverless edge runtimes and a generous free tier that creates enormous goodwill and bottom-up adoption.
- **AI teams** use Workers AI and R2 because the edge-native architecture eliminates egress costs and reduces inference latency to end users globally.

## How They Make Money

**Revenue Model:** Subscription-based SaaS/PaaS; >99% of revenue is recurring. Cloudflare sells subscriptions to its platform on monthly or annual commitments, with enterprise contracts typically multi-year. Revenue is recognized ratably over the contract period.

**Segment Reporting:** Cloudflare does not break out revenue by product line in its financial statements. The company reports one revenue segment. However, management qualitatively distinguishes the growth of Zero Trust/SASE, developer platform, and application services.

**Revenue Scaling Mechanism:** Cloudflare's land-and-expand model is the key economic driver. Customers typically enter via one product (often WAF or CDN, or the free tier), then expand to additional products within the platform. The 118–120% NRR demonstrates that the average customer spends materially more in year 2 than year 1.

**Historical Revenue:**

Revenue growth has been remarkably consistent in the 29–34% range from FY2023–Q1 2026, showing durable structural growth rather than lumpy cyclical demand.

## Revenue Quality

**Recurring Revenue:** Essentially 100% subscription. No significant transactional or one-time revenue components.

**Net Revenue Retention (NRR):** 118–120% consistently. This is a premier indicator of product-market fit in enterprise SaaS — it means existing customers collectively expand their spend by 18–20% annually without requiring any new customer acquisition to sustain double-digit growth.

**Remaining Performance Obligations (RPO):** RPO grew 48% YoY in Q4 2025; Current RPO grew 34% YoY. RPO is a leading indicator of future revenue locked under contract — 48% RPO growth significantly ahead of 30% recognized revenue growth indicates a growing revenue backlog. Q4 2025 saw the largest-ever ACV deal ($42.5M annual), and new ACV grew nearly 50% YoY — the fastest since 2021.

**Diversification:** Cloudflare has no disclosed single-customer concentration. Revenue comes from hundreds of thousands of customers globally. The business is diversified across sectors (tech, finance, healthcare, government, etc.) and geographies.

**Cyclicality:** Minimal. Security spending is one of the last budget items enterprises cut. CDN/performance is infrastructure-grade. The subscription model smooths any macro-driven new logo slowdowns.

**Concentration Risk:** Cloudflare's platform dependency on a limited number of internet gateways (AWS, Azure, GCP for egress) is infrastructure risk rather than revenue concentration risk.

As S&M and R&D grow slower than revenue, operating leverage is substantial. Management has cited a long-term operating margin target consistent with peers in the 20–30% range.

## Capital Intensity

**CapEx:** Cloudflare owns significant physical infrastructure (servers, fiber interconnects, power systems). This is a meaningful capital commitment that distinguishes it from pure-software SaaS peers. However, the infrastructure is amortized across millions of customers and products, creating shared-cost economics.

**Free Cash Flow:**
| Period | FCF | FCF Margin |
|--------|-----|-----------|
| FY2025 | $260.6M | 12.0% |
| Q4 2025 | $99.4M | 16.2% |
| Q1 2026 | $84.1M | 13.2% |

FCF conversion is positive and growing. The company is profitable on a FCF basis and does not require external capital for operations or growth. Management has guided to improving FCF margins over time as operating margins expand.

**Balance Sheet:** Cloudflare has historically maintained a strong cash position from its equity raises (IPO, follow-on) and convertible note issuances. The company does not pay dividends and does not engage in buybacks; capital is reinvested in R&D and infrastructure.

**Working Capital:** Subscription businesses collect cash upfront (deferred revenue), which creates a structural float. The company's deferred revenue balance is significant and represents future recognized revenue already billed.

## Growth Drivers

### 1. Zero Trust / SASE Enterprise Expansion

The transition from legacy perimeter security (firewalls, VPNs, on-premise security appliances) to cloud-native Zero Trust is a multi-year secular shift. Cloudflare One is a full single-vendor SASE platform that competes with Zscaler's modular approach and Palo Alto's Prisma Access. Cloudflare's advantage: its security stack runs natively on the same network as its CDN and developer platform — unified architecture, single control plane. Gartner recognizes Cloudflare as a SASE Visionary (2025). The enterprise SASE market is expected to exceed $20B by 2028.

Q4 2025 saw the company's largest-ever ACV deal at $42.5M/year and new ACV growth nearly 50% YoY — the fastest since 2021 — validating the enterprise expansion thesis.

### 2. AI Infrastructure and Developer Platform

Cloudflare Workers AI grew ~4,000% YoY in Q3 2025. The company is positioning itself as the preferred runtime for AI agents: edge inference (low latency, near-user), R2 storage (no egress fees for AI training and inference data), and the new Cloudflare for AI security layer (protecting and governing AI applications). In May 2026, CEO Matthew Prince stated that AI agent traffic on Cloudflare's network had surged 600% in 3 months internally, and cited this as the rationale for the workforce restructuring.

If agentic AI traffic materializes at the anticipated scale, Cloudflare's edge network is structurally positioned to be the infrastructure layer that AI agents run on — analogous to what AWS S3 was for the first wave of cloud applications.

### 3. Geographic and Vertical Expansion

Cloudflare's global PoP network reaches 330+ cities in 125+ countries. Its products are accessible from anywhere without hardware deployment. As enterprises in Asia-Pacific, Latin America, and EMEA modernize their security and networking stacks, Cloudflare's cloud-first model is positioned to capture this spend without the physical infrastructure constraints faced by traditional vendors.

Additionally, new verticals — government, critical infrastructure, financial services — are increasing adoption as regulatory requirements (FedRAMP, NIS2 in Europe) mandate Zero Trust postures.

### 4. Bottom-Up Developer Flywheel

Cloudflare's free tier and developer-first ethos create a continuous pipeline of paid customers. Enterprise procurement increasingly involves developer-led evaluation. When an enterprise's developers have already deployed Workers and trust the platform, the security team's procurement of Cloudflare One faces reduced friction. This bottom-up-to-top-down motion is a structural advantage over enterprise-sales-first competitors.

**Structural vs. Cyclical:**
- SASE expansion: structural (secular shift from perimeter to Zero Trust)
- AI infrastructure: structural (new workload category, early innings)
- Geographic/vertical: structural (global addressable market underpenetrated)
- Growth rate variation quarter-to-quarter: partly cyclical (enterprise procurement cycles, macro)

## Competitive Edge

### Moat Classification: Platform Integration + Network Scale + Data Intelligence + Switching Costs

**Platform Integration (Primary Moat):**
Cloudflare's most differentiated advantage is unified architecture. When security and networking run on the same network fabric as CDN and developer compute, the product quality is materially better than stitched-together point solutions. A request passes through DDoS scrubbing, WAF, load balancing, and CDN caching in microseconds — because all of these are services on the same Anycast network, not separate hops. Zscaler, Palo Alto, and Akamai approximate this integration, but none have built it natively on a single global network from scratch the way Cloudflare has.

**Network Scale:**
330+ PoPs in 125+ countries and 100+ Tbps network capacity. Cloudflare is directly peered with most major ISPs and networks globally, enabling it to reduce latency for its customers' users. Building comparable network coverage would require $5–10B+ in capital expenditure and a decade of peering relationships.

**Threat Intelligence Data Advantage:**
Cloudflare processes a claimed 20% of all web traffic. This gives its threat intelligence models a view of attack patterns that smaller networks simply cannot observe. Each new DDoS attack, novel exploit, or bot campaign that Cloudflare absorbs improves its WAF and bot management capabilities for all customers. This is a genuine data network effect — more customers → more traffic → better threat intelligence → better security → more customers.

**Switching Costs:**
Enterprise customers who deploy Cloudflare One (ZTNA, SWG, CASB) integrate it deeply into their identity providers (Okta, Azure AD), their SD-WAN architecture, and their security operations workflows. Migrating away requires replacing an integrated stack, retraining teams, and accepting a gap period with reduced coverage. The multi-product platform approach maximizes switching costs relative to single-product purchases.

**Developer Community:**
Millions of active developers have built on Workers and familiarity with the Cloudflare platform. This community creates evangelism and pipeline that is impossible to replicate through enterprise sales alone.

**Financial Evidence of Moat:**
- 118–120% NRR sustained over multiple years (customers voluntarily expand spend)
- Q4 2025: new ACV growth ~50% YoY, largest-ever deal at $42.5M — evidence of enterprise pricing power
- RPO grew 48% YoY — durable multi-year contract commitments, not just annual renewals
- 42% of Fortune 500 are paying customers, with stated goal to reach 50%+
- 73% YoY growth in $1M+ deals in Q1 2026 — customers deepening platform commitment

**Moat Vulnerabilities:**
1. **Hyperscaler bundling:** AWS/Azure/GCP are packaging WAF, CDN, and even basic Zero Trust tools with cloud agreements. Large enterprises consolidating on a single cloud provider may reduce Cloudflare budget. This is a real and growing competitive pressure.
2. **Zscaler / Palo Alto depth in security:** These companies have deeper security platform capabilities in specific areas (endpoint, SIEM, SOC tools) that Cloudflare does not yet fully address. For large enterprises with complex security requirements, Cloudflare is often a complement rather than a replacement.
3. **Infrastructure cost:** Unlike pure-software SaaS, Cloudflare's margins are capped by the cost of maintaining a global network. The 74–75% gross margin, while excellent for infrastructure, is lower than pure-software SaaS peers.
