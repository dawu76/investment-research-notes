**Pull quotes:**

## 4. The "four hats" solo-operator framework

In the same exchange, robotkris introduced (and linked his own blog post, "Everything Everywhere All at Once") a mental model for solo/small-team quant operators: at least four distinct "hats" — Engineer (can I actually trade this given realistic costs/constraints — needs precision), Architect (how does this fit with the whole portfolio — inherently noisy given estimation error in vol/correlation), plus two more roles referenced but not fully quoted in the thread. The point: apply precision only in the layer that warrants it rather than uniformly.

**Sentiment:** Well-received as a useful conceptual framework for reasoning about where rigor matters.

**Pull quote:**
- robotkris: *"as solo operators we have to do (at least) four jobs that require different thinking. You just need to apply the detail in the right layer."*
