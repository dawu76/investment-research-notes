3/28/26, 8:39 PM | tibor [reply]: You can calculate some greeks, for sure. But it's more difficult than it looks to do this consistently:
If you decide to do it on your own all the way through (from live to historical) it doesn't matter how close your greeks are to others.

But it is often practical to have the same (or similar) greeks what your broker provides, and this is far from trivial.  They rarely share the fine details about their calculations (think about Risk Free Rate, DTE calculations, additional heuristics for one-sided markets).  And it gets particularly difficult for shorter dated trades (0 to 7 DTE).


In particular, I already had fairly accurate business day calculations (via quantlib), RFR extracted from the chain and the sw infra there to calculate Greeks and IVs. Yet it took almost a month full time effort to get from 85% accurate to 98% accuracy.

Final note: comparing is also a challenge if you haven't already purchased historical data, because it is a pain to obtain it from IBKR.
