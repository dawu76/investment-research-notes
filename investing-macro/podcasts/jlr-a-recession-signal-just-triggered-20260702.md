# Henrik Zeberg: A Recession Signal Just Triggered | The Julia La Roche Show [Henrik Zeberg]

Detailed overview of the interview featuring Henrik Zeberg, macroeconomist at SwissBlock, on *The Julia La Roche Show*, published on July 2, 2026.

---

### I. Executive Summary
Henrik Zeberg asserts that the U.S. economy is quietly rolling over, hidden by a massively exuberant stock market that is experiencing a blow-off top. He notes that a structural recession signal in his coincident indicators has just flashed for the first time since 2022, suggesting the market top is near and a severe bear market alongside a strong dollar and plunging equities is coming within a year. Zeberg warns that structural deterioration in the labor market, negative revolving consumer credit, and housing contraction are the "quiet hand" of economic decay, while the "loud hand" of financial asset inflation creates a massive speculative distraction.

### III. Detailed Key Takeaways

#### 1. The Quiet Hand: Economic Deterioration
Zeberg emphasizes that the real economy, driven by the consumer, is weakening significantly, a process he calls the "quiet hand." While the financial market is booming, underlying metrics like full-time job losses (despite headline payroll gains) and rising credit card delinquencies indicate extreme consumer vulnerability.
*   **paycheck to Paycheck Reality:** The median consumer has no financial buffer to absorb shocks. High interest rates have gradually ground down the consumer's ability to spend.
*   **Gradual Decline:** Economic rollover is slow and almost invisible. It mirrors the lead-up to the 2008 financial crisis in 2006 and 2007.

> "The median consumer right now in the US will simply get thrown overboard by a $6,000 extra payment... they won't be able to pay their bills, which we by the way also see right now. We see the delinquency rates going up."
> — *Henrik Zeberg*

#### 2. The Structural Recession Call
A key proprietary coincident indicator of Zeberg's just flashed a structural recession signal, which has historically preceded recessions and massive market tops with high accuracy.
*   **Indicator Cross:** His market macro indicator crossed the equilibrium line on July 1, 2026.
*   **Confirmation Needed:** To make the call of "imminent recession," Zeberg is waiting for liquidity indicators to roll over further and short-term interest yields to decline.
*   **Consensus Blindness:** Wall Street models show only a 10.4% chance of recession, matching the consensus blindness in December 2007 when the official recession probability was under 6%.

> "That moment is actually today. That was when the coincident indicator crosses the equilibrium line, which is right now... nobody actually sees it coming, but that's where we are."
> — *Henrik Zeberg*

#### 3. The Blow-Off Top and Risk Rotation
Before the final crash, Zeberg expects capital to rotate from big tech into small caps (Russell 2000) and ultimately the riskiest assets like crypto, creating a final blow-off top before a Nasdaq 2000-style crash.
*   **The Loud Hand:** The financial market acts as a magician's trick, distracting from real economic rot.
*   **Nasdaq 2000 Parallel:** The AI bubble will burst in a classic blow-off fashion, resulting in a Nasdaq correction of 50%+ once liquidity dries up.

> "We're going to see that the dollar is going to strengthen... you're going to see the AI bubble burst and you're going to see the Nasdaq having a like a 2000 kind of event."
> — *Henrik Zeberg*

#### 4. The Sovereign Dollar and Asset Trends
*   **U.S. Dollar (DXY):** Zeberg predicts a temporary drop in the DXY to 93/94 during the final stage of the blow-off top, followed by a massive surge to 120+ as global liquidity freezes.
*   **Gold & Bitcoin:** Gold will act as a monetary hedge and rise toward $6,900, while Bitcoin is highly vulnerable to the liquidity crunch and will suffer in the broader bust.
