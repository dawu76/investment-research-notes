## D) Regulatory & Bank Sponsorship Disclosures

1. **Industrial Bank Partnership (WebBank):** WebBank (a Utah-chartered industrial bank) originates Sezzle-branded installment loans and issues the Sezzle Anywhere virtual Mastercard. WebBank exports Utah interest rates and credit structures under Section 27 of the Federal Deposit Insurance Act (FDIA), insulating Sezzle from state-by-state usury rate caps.
2. **FDIC Interagency Third-Party Lending Guidance (2023–2024):** WebBank operates under heightened supervisory oversight regarding third-party fintech origination platforms, requiring formal credit policy approval and capital reserve compliance.
3. **Direct State Lending Licenses:** Sezzle holds state lending, financing, and money transmitter licenses across 48+ U.S. states and Canadian provinces, enabling hybrid direct and partner origination.
4. **OCC National Bank Charter Application:** In 2026, Sezzle initiated formal preparations to apply for a full National Bank Charter with the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC), targeting insourced balance sheet deposits, nationwide interest rate preemption, and elimination of partner bank origination fees.
5. **CFPB & TILA Regulatory Standing:** Subject to CFPB market monitoring and data collection; subscription fees ($17.99/mo) and convenience fees structured to comply with Truth in Lending Act (TILA / Regulation Z) disclosure rules.
