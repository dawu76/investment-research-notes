# Constellation Energy ($CEG): Document Sources

**Compiled:** October 20, 2026. **Issuer:** Constellation Energy Corporation (SEC CIK 1868275; registered as Constellation Newholdco, Inc. from July to November 2021). Constellation separated from Exelon on February 1, 2022, so FY2021 is the first fiscal year it reported as a standalone registrant. It files combined reports with its operating subsidiary, Constellation Energy Generation, LLC. **Fiscal year:** calendar year. **Major event:** acquired Calpine Corporation on January 7, 2026.
