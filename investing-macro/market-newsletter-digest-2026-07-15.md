# Market and Investment Newsletter Digest — July 15, 2026

## 1. Fintech Blockbuster: Stripe & PE Advent Submit Buyout Offer for PayPal

The fintech sector was electrified by reports that Stripe (privately held payments giant) and private equity firm Advent International have submitted a joint acquisition proposal for PayPal.

*   **The Buyout Offer:** Stripe and Advent submitted an all-cash offer of **$60.50 per share**, valuing PayPal (`PYPL`) at approximately **$53.4 billion**. This represents a **28% premium** to Tuesday's closing price. The proposal includes roughly **$50 billion in committed financing**, with Stripe, Advent, and partners expected to contribute **$17 billion in equity**. The PayPal board is scheduled to meet as early as July 20 to review the proposal.
*   **Valuation Context:** Prior to the bid, PayPal had been heavily discounted by the market, trading at a cheap **8x forward earnings** as if permanent decline was structural. 
*   **Burry's Assessment:** Michael Burry argued that the $60.50 bid is far too low and represents only an opening offer. According to his valuation methodology, PayPal's true intrinsic value is in the **IV8-IV10 range**. Under this framework:
    *   **IV10** stands at **$75–$80**.
    *   **IV8** stands at **$110–$115**.
    *   Adding a control premium, Burry argues a fair buyout price should be closer to **$100 per share** (which is still below his IV8). He is not selling.

> [!NOTE]
> A PE/Stripe acquisition of PayPal represents a significant validation of large-cap fintech cash flows, suggesting that the sector's depressed valuations have created highly attractive targets for private capital.

## 2. IBM’s Most Historic Crash: Budgets Pivoting from Software to AI Hardware

On Tuesday, July 14, 2026, IBM experienced a historic sell-off that rattled the broader technology sector.

*   **The Sell-off:** IBM stock fell **26% at its lows**, dropping from $312 on July 7 to $213 on July 14. This represents the **worst single-day decline for IBM since at least 1968** (and potentially in its entire history). 
*   **The Trigger:** A CEO letter flagged that corporate IT capital expenditures are being aggressively reallocated. To secure supply-constrained AI infrastructure, enterprise budgets are shifting away from traditional application software and toward memory, servers, and storage.
*   **Historical Parallel:** Michael Burry compared the drop to the 1968 Go-Go era and the 1973–1974 Nifty Fifty crash (where Polaroid fell 91%, Xerox 71%, and IBM 58%). In February 2026, IBM suffered its fifth largest daily decline (-13.1%) due to the "Anthropic COBOL news" (which automated COBOL migration, threatening IBM’s legacy mainframe services).
*   **Market-wide Dispersion:** IBM’s crash triggered massive dispersion. While software giants (IBM, Adobe, ServiceNow, Oracle) were crushed under budget reallocations, hardware and memory players surged:
    *   **SK Hynix** rose **27%** on the same day.
    *   **Dell** rose **7%**.
    *   **SanDisk** rose **5%**.
*   **Korean Market Pain:** Despite the global hardware rally, the South Korean **KOSPI** (where Samsung and SK Hynix represent over half of the index's weight) triggered its seventh circuit breaker of 2026 on Monday. The KOSPI fell 8.95% to close at 6,806.93, then lower on Tuesday to 6,662.93, falling into formal bear-market territory (down 20% from its June record, though still up 67% YTD).

## 3. Pershing Square USA & Howard Hughes Float Strategy

Investor Bill Ackman detailed two specific strategic trades currently running under the Pershing Square umbrella.

*   **Pershing Square USA (`PSUS`) Discount:** Ackman highlighted that `PSUS` (which raised $5 billion in its IPO) currently trades at a **21% discount** to its net asset value (holding Microsoft, Amazon, Uber, and Meta). He committed **$250 million of his own money** to the IPO, arguing that buying `PSUS` is a pure spread-arbitrage trade independent of underlying equity direction.
*   **Howard Hughes Holdings (`HHH`) Float play:** In June 2026, `HHH` acquired insurer **Vantage**. Ackman is attempting to replicate Warren Buffett's Berkshire playbook: utilizing cash from `HHH` real estate lot sales to build Vantage's insurance float, routing the float into short-term Treasuries, and investing surplus capital into common stocks.

## 4. U.S.-Iran Naval Conflict Escalation & Strait of Hormuz Blockade

Military operations between the U.S. and Iran have intensified, shutting down the primary global oil transit corridor.

*   **Military Updates:** CentCom executed a 7-hour wave of precision airstrikes on Tuesday night targeting Iranian coastal defenses, missile/drone launch sites, and naval capabilities. The IRGC retaliated using cruise missiles to strike and set fire to a major U.S. military logistics center at **Mina Abdullah in Kuwait**, while also targeting U.S. Fifth Fleet facilities in Bahrain. Jordan intercepted three Iranian ballistic missiles.
*   **Hormuz Blockade:** The Strait of Hormuz is almost entirely sealed off to commercial traffic, with transits falling to **under 4 million barrels per day** (an 8 mbpd reduction from typical levels). The IRGC threatened to block all alternative regional export routes (such as Saudi pipelines) if the U.S. blockade continues.
*   **Energy Prices:** Brent crude is nearing **$88/bbl** (rebounded $14/bbl from its MOU lows) and WTI is trading **above $80/bbl**. Average U.S. retail gasoline prices are projected to rise back **above $4/gallon** by the end of the week.
*   **Strategic Reserves:** The U.S. Strategic Petroleum Reserve (SPR) has declined close to the **300 million barrel** level, approaching the statutory congressional minimum floor of 254 million barrels.
*   **Algos & Volatility Capping:** Despite the escalation, oil prices remain capped below $100 due to extreme volatility (high OVX). The high volatility triggers Value-at-Risk (VaR) limits, forcing quantitative algos to de-gross their oil exposure, limiting immediate price rises.

## 5. Macro Economy: CPI Disinflation vs. Sticky Core PPI

The macro markets are attempting to price a temporary drop in headline inflation against structural energy shocks.

*   **The June CPI Print:** Headline June CPI fell **0.4% MoM** (the first monthly decline since 2020), slowing core YoY to **2.6%**. The print caused CME FedWatch rate expectations to price an **86% probability of a September Fed hold** (up from 60%). 
*   **Headline vs. Core PPI:** June headline PPI printed at **-0.277%**, reflecting the drop in energy during the brief ceasefire window. However, core PPI printed at **+0.2%**, demonstrating that underlying disinflation is a "head fake" and core prices remain sticky. 
*   **The Warsh Fed:** Chairman Kevin Warsh testified that the CPI print is not "mission accomplished." He is actively dismantling forward guidance (refusing to guide rate paths) and considering ending post-meeting press conferences.
*   **China Disappointment:** China's Q2 GDP grew **4.3% YoY**, missing the 4.5% consensus and target range. First-half property investment fell **18%**, the steepest decline on record since 1992.
*   **Market Breadth:** S&P 500 rose 0.4% to 7,543, but breadth was exceptionally narrow. **312 S&P names fell on the day**, and the index excluding AI names was down 0.6%. S&P 500 EPS consensus growth is set at 22% YoY, but the median stock is expected to grow only 9% (and only 17% excluding Amazon, Google, and Nvidia).

## 6. Big Pharma Psychedelic Buyout Spree Legitimization

A massive structural validation event is underway in the psychedelic biotechnology sector, driven by policy shifts.

*   **Eli Lilly in Buyout Talks:** Bloomberg reports that Eli Lilly is in talks to acquire **AtaiBeckley** (a leading developer of next-generation psychedelic therapies) at a premium to its **$2 billion market value**. 
*   **Neuroscience Pivot:** Lilly, which historically built Prozac, is positioning psychedelics as the next frontier in neuroscience, challenging traditional antidepressants.
*   **Legitimacy and Regulatory Tailwinds:** The buyout talks follow a successful Phase 3 trial by Definium Therapeutics (an LSD-based major depressive disorder treatment), which saw its stock surge 60% in a session and triple YTD. Long-term tailwinds are driven by the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) under RFK Jr., which has pushed for institutional acceptance of psychedelics for veteran PTSD and addiction.

## 7. Aswath Damodaran's Country Risk — 2026 Edition

Aswath Damodaran released his annual Country Risk update, adjusting his corporate valuation models for a downgraded mature market.

*   **ERP Framework Adjustment:** Following Moody's downgrade of the United States from **Aaa to Aa1**, Damodaran modified his baseline Mature Market Premium calculation.
*   **The July 2026 Numbers:** 
    *   Mature Market Premium is calculated at **4.20%** (implied S&P 500 ERP of 4.42% minus the U.S. default spread of 0.22% based on the Aa1 rating).
    *   The mature S&P 500 index level stood at **7,499.36** on July 1, 2026.
    *   The emerging market relative risk measure is set at **1.55**.
*   **Country Risk exposure:** Damodaran argues that companies should be valued using equity risk premiums weighted by their geographic revenue/operations mix rather than country of incorporation.
