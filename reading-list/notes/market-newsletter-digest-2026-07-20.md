## 1. AI Frontier Economics, Commodity Intelligence & Geopolitical Strategy

### Ben Thompson (Stratechery): R&D vs. Inference COGS & Token Efficiency
- **COGS Re-enters Tech Economics:** Unlike traditional software with zero marginal costs, AI inference introduces variable Cost of Goods Sold (COGS). While open-weight models (Kimi K3, DeepSeek V4) eliminate fixed R&D costs for adopters, serving inference incurs significant compute COGS.
- **Fungible Intelligence vs. Token Commodities:** Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang frames GPUs as "token factories," but in the reasoning era (chain-of-thought tokens), tokens are not fungible commodities. Kimi K3, for example, generates more reasoning tokens per task than Claude/GPT models, making raw token pricing ($3 input / $15 output per 1M tokens) less predictive of total cost than **unit cost per intelligence output**.

```
    [ Model Footprint ] + [ Inference Efficiency ] + [ Memory (MLA) ]
                           + [ Token Efficiency ]
                                    ||
                 ===> Lowest Marginal Cost of Intelligence
```

- **Commodity Market Dynamics:** In a commoditized intelligence market, price is driven down to the marginal cost of the highest-cost supplier required to clear demand. Frontier labs with superior model capability, serving scale, and token efficiency hold lower marginal costs per intelligence unit, insulating their long-term margins.

### China's WAIC Strategy & Open-Source Geopolitical Push
- **Commoditizing the Software Complement:** At WAIC Shanghai, Premier Xi Jinping urged global adoption of open-source AI. By pushing open-weight models globally, China aims to commoditize software model layers to drive demand toward its dominant physical manufacturing, industrial robotics, and hardware export ecosystems.
- **Asian AI Capital Wave:** Moonshot AI is preparing a Hong Kong IPO at a **$30B+ valuation** following Z.ai’s (Zhipu) 1,000% stock surge this year. DeepSeek completed a $428M Series A funding round at a **$51.82B valuation**. Alibaba previewed Qwen 3.8 Max (2.4T parameters), joining Kimi K3 in open-weight frontier scaling.
- **Sources:** *Ben Thompson (Stratechery)* ("Who’s Afraid of Chinese Models?"), *Asia Tech Review* ("Moonshot takes the limelight..."), *Citrini* ("All Along the AI Watchtower").

## 3. Geopolitical Oil Shock & Macro Inflation Framework

- **Energy Supply Disruptions:** VLCC (Very Large Crude Carrier) tanker transits through the Strait of Hormuz dropped to near zero (2–3 daily transits), leaving the choke point functionally closed.
- **Crude Prices:** Brent crude broke above **$90/bbl ($90.25 peak)** and WTI surged to **$85.75/bbl**. 3-2-1 refining crack spreads expanded sharply as low-sulfur diesel and jet fuel supply tightened.
