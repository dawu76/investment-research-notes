# MacroVoices #536 Larry Mcdonald: The Migration is Upon us

Detailed overview and analysis of the *MacroVoices* podcast (Episode 536) featuring **Larry McDonald**, founder of *The Bear Traps Report* and author of *How to Listen When Markets Speak*, hosted by Erik Townsend and Patrick Ceresna. The episode was released on June 11, 2026.

---

### I. Executive Summary
Larry McDonald argues that the U.S. economy is entering a "transitory trance" reminiscent of Q4 2021, ignoring structural inflationary forces. Annualized super core CPI is running at **5.2%**, signaling a higher inflation regime driven by the Strait of Hormuz shipping closure, massive AI capex, and federal deficits. This structural shift is triggering the **Great Migration** out of financial assets (growth stocks, technology, long bonds) and into hard assets (value, materials, energy, and select healthcare). On equity markets, McDonald warns of near-term indigestion caused by massive IPO and secondary offerings: a **$200–$250 billion immediate raise** (SpaceX, Google secondary, Anthropic, OpenAI) which represents **6% of U.S. GDP** (compared to Facebook’s 2012 IPO representing less than 1% of GDP). Even more critical is the **$3 trillion restricted share overhang** unlocking over the next 12 months. McDonald outlines high-conviction plays: **Agnico Eagle (AEM)** as a gold miner value play, **Intuitive Surgical (ISRG)** as an under-owned medical AI data play, **Tourmaline Oil (TOU.TO)** to monetize trapped Canadian gas for AI data center turbines, and holding the **Sprott Physical Uranium Trust (SRUUF)** over volatile miners during market drawdowns.

### III. Detailed Key Takeaways

#### 1. The Transitory Trance and Super Core CPI Accrual
McDonald notes that the market is in a "transitory trance" similar to the fourth quarter of 2021, when the consensus believed inflation would quickly dissipate.
*   **Sticky Inflation:** Annualized over the last three months, Super Core CPI (which strips out volatile shelter and energy) is running at **5.2%**, indicating headline CPI is heading toward 5%–6% (or even 6%–8% if geopolitical conflicts persist).
*   **The Debt Limit on Fed Hikes:** While inflation pressures would normally force the Fed to hike, the fiscal reality prevents it. Interest on federal debt is projected to hit **$1.1 trillion** over the next 12 months (compared to $300 billion when the Fed started hiking in 2021-22). This fiscal choke point means the yield curve must steepen (twos/thirties steepener).

> "Annualized the last three months of the super core... you're coming out to 5.2%... inflation headline's going to be 5, 6... more like 6, 7, 8% a year from now... [But] the Fed really can't hike. Never forget interest on the debt today is at $1.1 trillion."
> — *Larry McDonald*

#### 2. The Speculative IPO Indigestion and Trillion-Dollar Lockup Overhang
The equity market is experiencing severe indigestion due to a massive wave of capital raises.
*   **The Size Comparison:** In 2012, Facebook’s $100 billion IPO was less than 1% of U.S. GDP. Today, the SpaceX IPO at a $1.8–$2 trillion valuation represents **6% of U.S. GDP**.
*   **Indigestion:** Between SpaceX ($80B raise), Google’s secondary offering ($80B), OpenAI, and Anthropic, the market faces a **$200–$250 billion immediate cash draw**. To participate, institutional managers are selling highly liquid mega-cap tech stocks (Mag 7).
*   **The $3 Trillion Overhang:** The real threat is the $3 trillion of restricted early-stage VC and insider equity that will become unrestricted 6 to 12 months post-IPO. For SpaceX, the lockup schedule is highly aggressive, expiring near December 2026. This creates a massive supply overhang that could lead to 40%–50% drawdowns, similar to Facebook’s post-IPO lockup expiration in 2012.
*   **CFO Warnings:** Chief Financial Officers (CFOs) are taking advantage of high stock prices by issuing massive convertible bonds (which contain equity dilutive options), a trend that matches the peak of late 2021.

> "Facebook 2012... was less than 1% of GDP. The SpaceX IPO would be 6% of GDP... net-net, a lot of people have to sell stocks... to make room for these beasts... [And] 6 to 12 months after... you have three trillion of capital that gets unlocked."
> — *Larry McDonald & Erik Townsend*

#### 3. The Great Migration: Rotations into Hard Assets and Value
Higher inflation and a multipolar world are forcing a capital migration out of paper certificates and growth software into hard assets and value.
*   **Healthcare Rotation:** Healthcare has shrunk from 16% of S&P 500 market cap to just 8%, despite aging baby boomers (now averaging 70 years old and controlling $79T in wealth). Quants have crowded long into high-momentum semiconductors and short into low-momentum healthcare/staples, creating massive valuation disconnects.
*   **Intuitive Surgical (ISRG):** A high-conviction AI healthcare play. It controls the ultimate proprietary data repository in robotic surgery (enabling doctors to operate remotely across borders), representing a data advantage comparable to Tesla's autonomous driving data.
*   **Tourmaline Oil (TOU.TO):** Hyperscalers plan to build 800–1000 data centers, but face local environmental and grid pushback (NIMBY). Tourmaline holds "trapped gas" in Canada and is negotiating with hyperscalers to construct private gas turbines on-site next to natural gas fields to power data centers off-grid.

> "Baby boomers... control 79 trillion of wealth... [Yet] healthcare as a percentage of the S&P 500 has gone from 16% to 8%... [ISRG] have the best data... [Tourmaline] natural gas is trapped... hyperscalers are in discussions... to create private turbines near that natural gas."
> — *Larry McDonald*

#### 4. Gold and Uranium: Vetting the Commodity vs. the Producer
*   **Gold (Agnico Eagle - AEM):** central banks in emerging markets (e.g. Turkey) are dumping gold to raise cash to fund energy imports. This "hot money flush" has pushed Agnico Eagle down 40%, trading at a cheap 5.9x EV/EBITDA. The company produces massive free cash flow ($6–$7B) and is buying back $2B in stock.
*   **Uranium (Sprott Physical Uranium Trust - SRUUF):** Deficits are structural for 2027–2028. However, mining companies (Cameco, NexGen, Denison) tend to over-promise on production timelines, and there is a massive brain drain of engineering talent leaving resource mining for crypto or AI. During broad market panic events (such as margin calls), volatile retail-heavy miners collapse 35%–45%. McDonald suggests holding the physical commodity (SRUUF) and only rotating into the miners after a major capitulation event.

> "NextGen, Cameco... tend to over-promise on production... a lot of the brain power has moved into Bitcoin mining or AI... [For uranium] you want to be in the commodity if you expect volatility, and rotate into the miners during that big drawdown."
> — *Larry McDonald*

#### 5. Presidential Oil Jawboning and Speculator Capitulation
Erik Townsend highlights that oil prices (WTI down to $90.03/bbl) remain flat despite kinetic escalations in the Strait of Hormuz. This is due to President Trump's constant jawboning about an "imminent peace deal" (tweeting about it 38-39 times on Truth Social since February 2026). Speclators are terrified of being caught long and losing $10/bbl on a tweet. This lack of speculator participation means that when inventories are fully depleted, the physical market will force a violent and sudden price spike as specs scramble to get back in.

> "Trump has said a peace deal... is imminent... 38 times since February 28th... speculators have been scared out... when the physical market really does have to resolve an imbalance... you're going to see the speculators all diving in at once."
> — *Erik Townsend*

### IV. Portfolio Positioning & Action Items

1.  **Avoid Crowded Tech IPO Allocations:** Do not chase the initial SpaceX, OpenAI, or Anthropic IPO allocations at peak valuations ($2T). Wait for the lockup expirations to trigger drawdowns, targeting entries 40%–50% below issue price in the secondary market.
2.  **Long Value and Hard Assets:** Rotate out of growth index funds into cash-flow-generative value equities (Berkshire Hathaway, Occidental Petroleum) and energy infrastructure.
3.  **Long Healthcare via XLV Option Collars:** Implement Patrick Ceresna's trade of the week: Buy XLV (healthcare ETF) and overlay an options collar (e.g., Buy August 145 Puts, Sell August 165 Calls) to capture the valuation rotation while capping downside risk.
4.  **Buy Trapped Natural Gas Assets:** Accumulate Tourmaline Oil (TOU.TO) to capitalize on off-grid hyperscaler data center partnerships.
5.  **Hold Physical Commodities Over Miners:** Own the Sprott Physical Uranium Trust (SRUUF) rather than volatile miners (Cameco, NexGen) to avoid retail-driven margin liquidations.
