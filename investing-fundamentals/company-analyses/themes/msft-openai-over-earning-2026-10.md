# Is Microsoft Over-Earning From OpenAI? ($MSFT, FY2026)

**Question (from a reader note the user supplied):** Microsoft's FY2026 10-K disclosed, for the first time, revenue from OpenAI of **$24.1B** "inclusive of revenue-sharing payments" in a year when Azure passed $100B. Leaving concentration aside: how much of Microsoft's current earnings, and especially its earnings *growth*, comes from parts of the OpenAI relationship that are **contractually finite and unusually profitable**?

**Short answer:**
* **On the level of earnings: only slightly.** The finite, high-margin pieces (the revenue share received and free use of OpenAI's models) are an estimated ~2-3% of FY2026 operating income. Including the much larger Azure compute business, OpenAI is about 5-9% of operating income (estimated range).
* **On growth: yes, materially, in two ways.**
  1. OpenAI supplied roughly a quarter to a third of Microsoft's FY2026 revenue and operating-income growth, and about half of Azure's growth.
  2. The revenue share is set to ramp sharply and then stop. It is capped (reportedly $38B) and ends by 2030 at the latest; if OpenAI hits its plan, the cap is reached around 2028. That flatters FY2027-FY2028 growth and leaves a cliff of up to ~$2 of EPS when it ends.
* **Adjusted EPS growth was also helped by non-operating AI gains.** It excludes OpenAI gains, but not the $3.2B gain on Microsoft's Anthropic stake.

Figures from filings are cited; everything marked "estimate" is this page's arithmetic on stated assumptions.

## 2. The contract: which pieces are finite

| Term | Before April 27, 2026 | After the April 27, 2026 amendment | Finite? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue share **from OpenAI to Microsoft** | ~20% of OpenAI revenue (reported) until AGI verified by an expert panel; "payments made over a longer period" (October 2025) | Same percentage, **through 2030**, "independent of OpenAI's technology progress," **subject to a total cap** (The Information: **$38B**) | **Yes:** ends 2030 or at the cap |
| Revenue share **from Microsoft to OpenAI** (~20% of Azure OpenAI and Bing revenue, as reported) | Paid ("reciprocal revenue-sharing arrangements", September 2025 10-Q) | **Eliminated** | One-time step-up in Microsoft's margins |
| IP license to OpenAI models and products | Through 2032, exclusive; API exclusivity on Azure until AGI | Through 2032, **non-exclusive** | **Yes:** ends 2032 |
| Azure compute | ROFR removed (October 2025); OpenAI committed **$250B** of incremental Azure | Azure stays "primary"; products ship on Azure first unless Microsoft can't or won't support them; OpenAI may serve customers on any cloud | Commitment-based, not exclusive |
| Equity | ~27% after the recapitalization | "Major shareholder"; ~25% at June 30, 2026 | Ongoing |

The 10-K wording changed to match. The September 2025 10-Q described "reciprocal revenue-sharing arrangements"; the FY2026 10-K says Microsoft "will continue to receive revenue-sharing payments."

## 3. What the $24.1B is made of (estimate)

The 10-K doesn't split the figure. Two data points bound the revenue share inside it:
* **Leaked OpenAI documents (Ed Zitron, November 2025; unverified):**
  * Revenue share received was $493.8M in CY2024 and $865.9M in the first nine months of CY2025 ($411.1M in Q3 CY2025).
  * OpenAI's inference spend on Azure was $3.77B in CY2024 and $8.67B in the first nine months of CY2025 ($3.65B in Q3).
  * These payments are only about half of 20% of OpenAI's reported revenue (CY2025 revenue $13.07B, per audited figures reported by Fortune). That suggests either netting against Microsoft's reciprocal payments or a narrower revenue base.
* **Gross 20% view:** OpenAI revenue for July 2025-June 2026 is roughly $20B (H2 CY2025 ~$8B estimate + H1 CY2026 ~$12.4B reported by the WSJ: Q1 $5.7B, Q2 $6.7B). 20% of that is ~$4B.

**Estimate:** the revenue share was ~$2-4B of the $24.1B (base case ~$3B). The other ~$20-22B is Azure compute and services bought by OpenAI.

## 4. How much of current earnings (FY2026, estimate)

Assumptions:
* Revenue share earns a ~95% margin.
* Compute earns a 30% / 44% / 55% gross margin (low / base / high).
* An overhead allocation of 5% of OpenAI revenue.
* A 19% tax rate and 7.45B diluted shares.

The 44% base comes from Intelligent Cloud's **incremental** gross margin in FY2026: gross profit +$13.8B on revenue +$31.5B. The segment's FY2025 gross margin was 62.2%. The incremental business, much of it OpenAI, is far less profitable than legacy Azure.

| Scenario | Revenue share | Compute margin | Pretax contribution | % of operating income | After-tax EPS | % of adjusted EPS |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Low | $2.2B | 30% | ~$7.5B | 4.8% | ~$0.81 | 4.7% |
| Base | $3.0B | 44% | ~$10.9B | 7.0% | ~$1.19 | 6.9% |
| High | $4.1B | 55% | ~$13.7B | 8.8% | ~$1.49 | 8.6% |

Of this, the **contractually finite, unusually profitable piece** (the revenue share) is only ~$2-4B pretax: **~1.3-2.5% of operating income**.

The model license (royalty-free since April 2026, finite in 2032) adds margin inside Copilot and Azure OpenAI that can't be separated out. Microsoft's own research spending on frontier models ("R&D compute capacity, AI talent, and data", 10-K) is the cost of replacing it.

## 5. How much of earnings growth (estimate)

* **FY2025 OpenAI revenue isn't disclosed.** The leaked figures give ~$8.3B for July 2024-June 2025: inference $7.49B plus revenue share $0.76B, excluding any training compute. A range of **$8-11B** is used here.
* **OpenAI revenue growth of ~$13-16B** therefore equals:

  | Share of FY2026 growth | Estimate |
  |---|---|
  | Microsoft's +$50.1B revenue growth | ~26-32% |
  | Intelligent Cloud's +$31.5B | ~42-50% |
  | Azure's growth (≥$29B, derived from ">$100B" and +41%) | ~45-54% |

* **Operating income:** in the base case, OpenAI's contribution rose ~$7.3B, about **27% of the +$26.7B** increase.
* **Adjusted EPS growth quality:**
  * Adjusted other income swung from −$0.14B to **+$4.17B**, including a **$3.2B gain on Microsoft's Anthropic investment** in Q4. Microsoft's adjusted figures exclude only OpenAI gains. That swing is worth ~$0.47 of EPS, about **15% of the $3.15 adjusted-EPS increase**.
  * Remove the Anthropic gain and the revenue share from both years. "Durable" adjusted EPS grows ~**18%** ($14.05 to $16.62), against the reported 22%.

## 6. The forward problem: a revenue share that ramps, then stops

Revenue share is 20% of OpenAI revenue, assuming the gross basis applies after April 2026 now that Microsoft no longer pays OpenAI, with a $38B total cap.

| Year | OpenAI revenue (plan: ~$33B → $62B → $100B, reported projections) | Payment | Cumulative | OpenAI at roughly half of plan | Payment | Cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026 | ~$33B | ~$6.6B | $6.6B | ~$30B | $6.0B | $6.0B |
| 2027 | ~$62B | ~$12.4B | $19.0B | ~$40B | $8.0B | $14.0B |
| 2028 | ~$100B | ~$19.0B (cap reached) | $38.0B | ~$50B | $10.0B | $24.0B |
| 2029 | n/a | $0 | $38.0B | ~$60B | $12.0B | $36.0B |
| 2030 | n/a | $0 | $38.0B | ~$70B | $2.0B (cap) | $38.0B |

**What this means:**
* At ~95% margin, a ~$19B payment year is worth **~$2 of EPS** after tax, ~11-12% of FY2026 adjusted EPS.
* On OpenAI's plan, that income rises from ~$3B (FY2026) to ~$19B (2028) and then **drops to zero**. FY2027-FY2028 EPS growth would be flattered by several points a year, followed by a cliff.
* If OpenAI grows more slowly, the ramp is gentler but still ends by 2030.
* If the cap counts payments already made (~$1.4B through September 2025 per the leaked documents), it binds slightly earlier.
* The percentage, the cap amount and its start date come from press reports, not filings.

The elimination of Microsoft's payments to OpenAI (April 2026) is a smaller, permanent margin gain. It still boosts growth once, in the twelve months after the change, and the amount isn't disclosed.

## 7. The bigger exposure isn't finite: it's the compute contract

The ~$20-22B of Azure compute bought by OpenAI is **not** contractually finite: there is the $250B incremental commitment, and ~45% of RPO was OpenAI at December 2025. It also isn't unusually profitable, judging by the 44% incremental Intelligent Cloud gross margin. Its risks are different:
* **Counterparty:**
  * OpenAI ran a 2025 operating loss of ~$20.9B (Fortune) and a Q2 2026 operating loss of ~$12B on ~$7B of revenue, per Beachman in [[market-newsletter-digest-2026-09-27]].
  * Its Azure bill is funded by capital raises, while Microsoft funds the capex: $115.9B in FY2026.
* **Non-exclusivity:** OpenAI can now serve customers on any cloud, so Azure's share of OpenAI's future compute is no longer guaranteed beyond contracted amounts.
* **Depreciation:** servers are depreciated over "two to six years" (10-K). If AI accelerators have shorter economic lives, today's compute margins are overstated, an accounting rather than a contractual form of over-earning. See [[ai-capex-financing-risk-semianalysis-2026-10]] and [[ai-compute-commencement-wall-and-refinancing-trap]].
* Context: Beachman estimated OpenAI at ~70% of Microsoft's AI revenue ($24B of $34B). [[market-newsletter-digest-2026-09-22]] carried Zitron's estimate that OpenAI and Anthropic are more than 70% of hyperscaler AI revenue.

## 8. Verdict

* **Current earnings:** Microsoft is **not materially over-earning** on the finite, high-margin pieces: ~1-3% of operating income in FY2026. Including compute, OpenAI is ~5-9% of operating income, most of it on a multi-year contract.
* **Earnings growth:** Microsoft **is** leaning on OpenAI.
  * About a quarter to a third of FY2026 revenue and operating-income growth, and about half of Azure growth, came from one customer that loses money and is no longer exclusive.
  * About 4 points of the 22% adjusted EPS growth came from items that won't recur: the Anthropic gain and the revenue share. Durable growth was ~18%.
* **The next two years:** the capped revenue share is the clearest case of **temporarily inflated growth**. It may add up to ~$2 of EPS at its 2028 peak and then disappear. Analysts extrapolating FY2027-FY2028 growth should treat it as non-recurring.

**What would change the answer:**
* Microsoft disclosing the split of the $24.1B or the revenue-share amount.
* The cap's exact terms and start date.
* OpenAI's actual 2026-2028 revenue: faster growth means a bigger, earlier spike and cliff.
* OpenAI shifting compute to other clouds.
* Evidence on AI server economic lives.

**Watch list:**
* OpenAI revenue in each Microsoft 10-K and 10-Q related-party note, plus accounts receivable versus revenue for collection stress.
* OpenAI's share of commercial RPO.
* Intelligent Cloud incremental gross margin.
* The "Other, net" line for equity gains, OpenAI and Anthropic.
* Any 8-K describing amended OpenAI terms.

**Data caveats:**
* The leaked figures are unverified, and their implied effective rate (~10%) is about half the reported 20%.
* The $38B cap and the 20% rate come from press reports, not filings.
* OpenAI's 2026-2028 revenue projections have changed several times.
* FY2025 OpenAI revenue to Microsoft isn't disclosed; the ~$8-11B range here is an estimate.
