# Sea Limited ($SE): Investment Memo

**Date:** October 21, 2026. **Price:** $77.93 (late-session reading; intraday low $76.00, a new 52-week low). **Market cap:** ~$50B on 638M diluted shares; **EV** ~$38B after ~$11.5B of net cash and investments (MS's net cash figure). **Street:** Morgan Stanley Overweight $153 (Oct 5); Morningstar fair value $152, 5 stars (fair value set Mar 4); DBS Hold $105 (Oct 1, bear case $71); Maybank Buy $150 (Oct 19); Bernstein Buy $150 (Oct 20); BofA Buy $130 (Oct 21, single source). **Next catalyst:** Q3 2026 results, estimated November 10 (one source says November 17; Sea had not confirmed the date as of October 21).
Business detail is in [[se-2-equity-report]]. Sources: [[se-1-document-sources]].

`contested: true`: the Street's ~$150 targets assume Shopee's margin rises from 0.67% to ~1.1% of GMV by 2028. DBS ($105) and the October tape assume reinvestment continues. Sections 2 and 3 explain the gap.

## 1. Executive Summary

**Thesis.** Sea owns the leading e-commerce marketplace in Southeast Asia and a fast-growing lender built on its transaction data. Both are growing 30-60% and are profitable. Garena's game earnings pay for much of the reinvestment. At ~$78 the stock trades at ~7.5x 2027 adjusted EBITDA (MS estimates), about 18.5x MS's 2027 EPS and below MS's own bear-case value ($80). The market is pricing roughly 2028 adjusted EBITDA of ~$4.5B, about 29% below MS's $6.35B (section 4). That is consistent with Shopee's margin staying near today's level and Sea Cloud adding losses. The thesis works if Shopee's margin rises even modestly and management stops opening new spending fronts. It fails if 2026's pattern of new investment programs (warehouses in March, quick commerce and the Migoo AI assistant, Sea Cloud in October) keeps repeating.

**Bull case.** Shopee reaches ~1.3% EBITDA/GMV by 2028 as ads and fees rise while warehouse spending pays off. Monee keeps growing 30%+ with stable credit and lists at $18-22B (DBS's estimate). Sea Cloud mostly monetizes GPUs Sea already owns. Value ~$165 at end-2027.

**Bear case.** Pinduoduo, TikTok Shop and AI shopping agents force Shopee to keep subsidizing, so its margin stays ~0.7%. Monee's provisions rise with off-Shopee and Brazil lending, Free Fire fades, and Sea Cloud becomes a GPU capex program. Value ~$55 at end-2027.

**Target investor.** Growth at a reasonable price, with tolerance for emerging-market, FX and governance risk. Forrest Li holds ~59% of the votes (March 2025) and Tencent holds a large non-voting stake, so outside shareholders have little influence over capital allocation. Not suitable for income investors (no dividend) or anyone who cannot sit through 30-50% drawdowns, which this stock has had several times.

### The fears, in order of importance

1. **A new capital-intensive bet, announced in the worst possible week.** Sea Cloud was announced as an "AI-first enterprise platform." Data Center Dynamics called it a "neocloud." The memo gave no capex, customers, pricing or funding plan. Investors had just watched CoreWeave's bond scare and a 20-28% weekly drop in neocloud stocks (October 12-16; see [[docn-3-investment-memo]] and [[akam-vs-docn-20261015]]), and they assumed the worst. The Q2 call (August 11) never mentioned AI, cloud or GPU spending, so this was a surprise. Facts that cut against the fear:
   * Sea already runs ~25,000 GPUs, 150,000+ servers, 40+ data centers and 100+ MW for its own businesses, so Sea Cloud may mainly sell spare or shared capacity.
   * Sea rents rather than buys most of its physical assets.
   * Maybank called the AI spend "manageable."
   But the "other services" segment already lost $46M in Q2 (up 236% y/y), and MS models it losing $123M in 9M26. Sea Cloud will show up there.
2. **Management keeps finding new ways to spend.** This is the deeper issue, and it predates October.
   * March 3: Shopee guided 2026 EBITDA "no lower than 2025," added a warehouse build-out and a mid-to-high-30s tax rate. The stock fell 17% and Morningstar cut its fair value 23%, from $198 to $152.
   * Q2: revenue grew 48% but adjusted EBITDA only 10.6% and net income 10.6%. S&M rose 64.5% and provisions 71.5%. EPS of $0.70 missed ~$0.83-0.86 consensus. MarketBeat counts 0 EPS beats in 8 quarters.
   * October: DBS's downgrade named more spending (quick commerce, VIP, Migoo), and Sea Cloud came three weeks later.
   Each episode moves margin expansion further out. The market has stopped giving credit for the 2027 "higher benchmark" management promised in March.
3. **The Monee IPO was read as a distraction rather than a catalyst.** The memo's wording is loose: Monee is "approaching the point where it can stand on its own two feet... Going public could give it the resources and the room." DBS sees no listing before 2027 and lists real obstacles:
   * licenses in eight markets
   * MAS approval for anyone holding more than 5% of MariBank
   * Indonesia's 40% debt-to-income cap on BNPL from 2027
   * arm's-length terms with Shopee, whose users and payment flows Monee depends on
   Splitting off the fastest-growing, highest-margin unit also raises a question the market doesn't like: how profitable is Shopee on its own? DBS's sum of parts ($18-22B for Monee against ~$6B implied in the price) is the bull's counter.
4. **Competition is changing shape.**
   * Pinduoduo's main app (not Temu) entered Malaysia in December 2025, removed its free-shipping minimum in August 2026 and leads shopping-app downloads there.
   * TikTok MAU grows ~3x faster than Shopee's in ASEAN (+15% vs. +5%, September).
   * Meta's Muse shopping agent (US/Canada only so far) points to a future where an agent, not Shopee's search page, captures purchase intent. That would undercut ads, Shopee's main margin lever. MS lists "potential Muse disruption" among the concerns. The threat to Shopee is inference, not reported fact (see [[market-newsletter-digest-2026-09-22]]).
5. **Supply overhangs (minor).** Insiders file Form 4s almost daily (CCO ~1,000 shares a day, Garena president 40,000, CFO 10,000), all under 10b5-1 plans adopted months ago. These are small next to holdings, but the steady flow of headlines hurts sentiment. MS also cites a "potential Tencent monetization" overhang. No 2026 Tencent sale or filing was found; its last sale was January 2022. Treat that as speculation.

**What did not happen:** no guidance cut, no earnings miss in October, no credit event, no regulatory action. The fundamentals reported so far (Q2) are intact: GMV +28%, NPL 1.0%, Garena bookings +15.5%. The stock is pricing what management might spend next, not what the business earned.

## 3. Business Model & Unit Economics

**Revenue and profit by segment (FY2025):** Shopee $16.6B revenue (72%), $0.88B adjusted EBITDA (25% of segment EBITDA). Monee $3.79B (17%), $1.02B (29%). Garena $2.41B (11%), $1.66B (47%). Group adjusted EBITDA was $3.44B. Geography: Indonesia is the largest Shopee market (~34% of Shopee MAU), Brazil the main growth market, and Taiwan the most developed.

| Unit economics (Q2 2026) | Value | Derivation |
|---|---|---|
| Shopee average order value | ~$9.1 | $38.3B GMV / 4.2B orders |
| Shopee core marketplace revenue per order | ~$1.01 | $4.26B / 4.2B (core take rate ~11.1%) |
| Shopee adjusted EBITDA per order | ~$0.06 | $255M / 4.2B (0.67% of GMV) |
| Monee provision for credit losses / Monee revenue | ~40% | $555M / $1,403M |
| Monee adjusted EBITDA margin | 20.5% | $288M / $1,403M (26.9% in FY2025) |
| Monee 90+ day NPL | 1.0% | stable q/q |
| Garena bookings per paying user (quarter) | ~$11.2 | $763.5M / 68.1M paying users (10.2% pay ratio) |

**Operating leverage is negative by choice in 2026.** Revenue grew 48% in Q2 and adjusted EBITDA 10.6%. MS expects EBITDA growth to reach 15% in Q3 and 29% in 2027 (adjusted EBITDA $5.12B), as Shopee's take-rate gains outrun reinvestment. Each 10 bp of Shopee EBITDA/GMV on 2027 GMV of ~$193B (MS) is ~$190M of EBITDA, about 4% of group 2027 EBITDA.

**Working capital and cash conversion.** Shopee runs negative working capital (it collects from buyers before paying sellers). Monee absorbs capital: the loan book grew $1.9B in H1 2026. FY2025 operating cash flow of $5.02B was 3.1x net income, but that includes the float. Morningstar's free cash flow to the firm drops from $3.79B (2025) to $1.79B (2026e). Sea does not disclose a cash conversion cycle.

## 4. Valuation: What $78 Assumes

* **Multiples at $77.93:** ~9.6x 2026 and ~7.5x 2027 MS adjusted EBITDA; ~8.7x 2027 MS ModelWare EBITDA (MS called 11x "attractive" at $95). P/E is ~27x 2026 and ~18.5x 2027 (MS EPS $2.90 / $4.21), or ~19.6x on Morningstar's 2027 EPS ($3.98). SE's three-year average NTM EV/EBITDA was ~18x (MS, FactSet).
* **Reverse valuation:** an investor who wants an 11% annual return and expects SE to trade at 10x 2028 adjusted EBITDA at end-2027, with ~$12B of net cash, needs $78 × 1.133 ≈ $88/share then. That is ~$57B of equity and ~$45B of EV, which implies **2028 adjusted EBITDA of ~$4.5B**, against MS's $6.35B and Morningstar's $6.36B. The price assumes the Street's 2028 numbers are ~29% too high.
* **Sum of the parts at $78 (EV ~$38B):** value Garena at 6x 2026e EBITDA ($1.89B → ~$11B). If Monee is worth 10x 2026e EBITDA (~$12B), Shopee gets ~$15B, or ~14x its 2026e EBITDA ($1.07B) and ~0.09x GMV. If Monee is worth DBS's $18-22B, Shopee gets $5-9B. Either way the market is paying little for Shopee's margin expansion.
* **Street vs. price:** MS's probability-weighted DCF (base $155 at 80%, bear $80 at 10%, bull $210 at 10%) puts only 10% weight on the bear case, and the stock now trades below it. DBS's bear case is $71.

## 5. Competitive Position & Moat

* **Market position:** ~52% of Southeast Asian e-commerce GMV in 2024 and above 50% in every Southeast Asian market except Indonesia (46%). Over 50% in Taiwan. In Brazil, Shopee app MAU is growing 11% vs. Mercado Libre's 16% (September 2026, Sensor Tower via MS). Shopee had 534M worldwide MAU in September 2026, +5% y/y.
* **Moat classification:** network effect (Shopee; Morningstar Narrow), cost advantage in logistics (in-house costs below third-party rates; instant-delivery cost per order -20% in Indonesia), data advantage in credit (Monee underwrites with Shopee transaction histories; NPL ~1% while the book grew 62%). Garena: none (single title). Regulatory: MariBank licenses are a modest barrier.
* **Durability evidence:** share has held through TikTok's entry and fee increases have stuck (Shopee and TikTok both raised fees in Q4 2025 and 2026). Against that, Shopee's margin has not risen (0.69% of GMV in FY2025, 0.67% in Q2 2026, 0.58% in Q3 2025), so the network effect has not yet produced pricing power that survives competition.
* **Key competitors and differentiation:**
  * **TikTok Shop/Tokopedia:** content-led and the fastest-growing. Shopee's answer is its own livestream and video commerce (25% of SEA physical orders).
  * **Pinduoduo/Temu (PDD):** cross-border low prices. Shopee's answer is local sellers plus faster delivery.
  * **Lazada (Alibaba):** distant number three.
  * **Mercado Libre:** in Brazil. Shopee competes on price and leads in low-ticket categories.
  * **AI shopping agents (Meta Muse, ChatGPT shopping):** Shopee has integrated with ChatGPT (June 2026) and built Migoo.

## 6. Top 3 Growth Drivers & Sensitivities

1. **Shopee margin ramp (take rate and ads, logistics payback).** TAM impact: none on volume, but each 10 bp of EBITDA/GMV is ~$190-225M a year in 2027-28. Probability-weighted, ~1.0% in 2028 (bear 0.7% at 30%, base 1.1% at 50%, bull 1.3% at 20%). Milestones: Q4 2026 margin after the warehouse build-out (management said it would "pay off by Q4"), Brazil commission increases, and ad take rate disclosures.
2. **Monee loan growth at stable credit.** TAM: 50-70% of Southeast Asia unbanked or under-banked; digital lending growth ~17% a year 2024-30 (Bain/Google/Temasek). MS models DFS revenue +37% in 2027 and +24% in 2028. Probability-weighted 2028 margin ~19.5% (17% / 20% / 22%). Milestones: NPL and provision ratio each quarter, Indonesia's BNPL debt-to-income cap (2027), the Philippines MariBank ramp, and any Monee listing filing.
3. **GMV growth in Brazil and ASEAN.** TAM: e-commerce at 4-29% of retail in major Southeast Asian markets vs. 52% in China. MS has GMV +26% in 2026, +20% in 2027 and +16% in 2028 ($224B). Milestones: Q3 GMV (MS $40.3B; DBS $39.2-39.6B), the 2027 GMV guide in March 2027, and Sensor Tower MAU against TikTok and Pinduoduo.

**Pre-mortem.** If this investment fails over three years, the most likely reason is that Sea kept reinvesting at the moment the margin was supposed to show up. The 2027 guide resets lower again, Sea Cloud absorbs cash, and the stock rerates as a low-margin conglomerate controlled by a founder. In that world Shopee's 2028 EBITDA/GMV is still under 0.8% and EV/EBITDA settles at 6-8x.

**Assumptions that must hold:**
1. Shopee's EBITDA/GMV reaches at least ~0.9% in 2027 and ~1.0% in 2028.
2. Sea Cloud's capex stays under ~$1B a year and is mostly self-funded, without equity or a large debt raise.
3. Monee's 90-day NPL stays at or below ~1.3%.
4. Garena's bookings stay roughly flat or better.
5. No large Tencent block sale at a deep discount (the "overhang" never becomes an event).

## Bottom Line

October's selloff was mostly about Sea, not the market. A vague Sea Cloud announcement landed in the week neoclouds were collapsing, on top of a year of margin resets, a DBS downgrade and a steady flow of insider-sale headlines. Nothing in the reported numbers has broken. The price now assumes 2028 EBITDA ~29% below the Street, which is close to Shopee's margin never improving. That makes the risk/reward favorable if the Q3 call (est. Nov 10) caps Sea Cloud spending and points Shopee's margin up. If management instead discloses a large GPU program or guides 2027 margins flat, the bear case (~$55) becomes the base case. Waiting until Nov 10 costs some upside if the call goes well, but it removes the largest unknown.

**Stress test ([[se-4-stress-test]]).** The arbitrator leaned bear (moderate) on Shopee's margin trajectory. The deciding facts: six flat quarters, a ~1.5% incremental Shopee margin in Q2, and a US$1B Shopee target that equals only ~0.62% of 2026 GMV. It leaned slightly bull on fee increases sticking, since GMV grew 28% after two fee rounds and total Shopee revenue/GMV rose from ~12.7% to ~14.6%. On the valuation floor it leaned bull, but at bear-heavy weights (45/45/10) the discounted value is ~$81, about where the stock trades. Its sizing: a one-third starter position, half after a Q3 print showing Shopee EBITDA/GMV ≥0.68%, GMV ≥24% and bounded Sea Cloud spend, and full only after two quarters above 0.75%.
