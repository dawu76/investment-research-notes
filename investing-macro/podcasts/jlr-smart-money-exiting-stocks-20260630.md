# Andrew Pancholi: Smart Money Is Quietly Exiting Stocks | The Julia La Roche Show [383]

### I. Executive Summary
Andrew Pancholi outlines his multi-frequency mathematical cycles framework for forecasting major turning points in equities, commodities, and global geopolitics. He notes that while the broad 100-year cycle suggests the 2020s are mimicking the 1920s bull market leading to a long-term peak in 2029, near-term indicators show "smart money" is quietly exiting U.S. equities (evidenced by falling open interest in Dow futures). Pancholi identifies the third to fourth week of July 2026 as a major turning point, warning that a failure to hold could trigger a deeper correction. In commodities, he projects a massive rise in WTI oil to **$183–$185/barrel** in late 2026 driven by a 36-year Gulf War cycle (timed from Saddam Hussein's 1990 invasion of Kuwait). Additionally, he predicts gold will bottom in September 2026 before launching a historic rally to **$6,900/oz** by March 2027, while remaining highly bearish on Bitcoin. Finally, Pancholi explains how flying Boeing 747 wide-body jets informs his approach to portfolio management—emphasizing threat management, checklist discipline, and defining exits before entry.

### III. Detailed Key Takeaways

#### 1. The Multi-Frequency Cycles Framework
Pancholi uses overlapping mathematical cycles to map human behavior and market trends:
*   **100-Year Cycle:** Dictates macro financial crises (e.g., 1807 Embargo Act panic $\rightarrow$ 1907 Rich Man's panic $\rightarrow$ 2007-2008 GFC). This suggests the 2020s behave like the 1920s, heading toward a major secular peak in **2029** (100 years from the Sept 1929 top).
*   **90-Year Geopolitical Cycle:** Double the 45-year cycle, representing intense polarization, anti-migration sentiment, and populist rises (mirroring the 1930s). The cycle points to **2029** (90 years from the 1939 outbreak of WWII) as a high-risk window for global conflict.
*   **250-Year Empire Cycle:** Governs the death and rebirth of sovereign systems. For the U.S., 250 years from the American Revolution (1765 Stamp Act) leads to **2015-2017**, which brought Brexit and Trump 1.0. The cycle indicates a transition of global dominance away from the West peaking in **2026**.
*   **144-Year Cycle:** Tracks major election disputes (e.g., the contested 1876 election matching the contested 2020 election).

> "History if not repeats, certainly rhymes... We're broadly following the 1920s... in a big bull market that culminates potentially in 2029... 90 years from the outbreak of the Second World War takes us to 2029."
> — *Andrew Pancholi*

#### 2. Near-Term Equities: Smart Money Outflow
Despite the S&P 500 making record highs in June 2026, Pancholi warns of hidden distribution.
*   **COT Divergence:** Commitment of Traders (COT) data on Dow Futures reveals that the "smart money" is quietly withdrawing from U.S. equities, with both longs and shorts decreasing.
*   **July Tripwire:** The third to fourth week of **July 2026** is highlighted as a critical cycle junction. If the index fails to form a bottom here and continues lower, it indicates a structural top rather than a healthy pullback.
*   **Year-End Target:** If the summer correction holds, S&P **7,800 / 7,853** remains a viable target for late 2026.

> "One of the key indicators... is suggesting the smart money is leaving the US equity market right now... I'm becoming more cautious as to this top being more than just a pullback and a buying opportunity."
> — *Andrew Pancholi*

#### 3. Geopolitical War Cycles and $183 Oil
Pancholi forecasts a violent escalation in global energy conflicts based on military cycle overlays:
*   **36-Year Gulf War Cycle:** 36 years from Saddam Hussein's August 1990 invasion of Kuwait. This cycle hits in **August 2026**, suggesting a rapid escalation of the Iran-Red Sea conflict.
*   **45-Year Iranian Theocracy Cycle:** 45 years from the 1981 release of the U.S. embassy hostages, which timed the April 2024 escalations.
*   **Oil Target:** Using geometric channel analysis, Pancholi sets an oil target of **$183–$185/barrel** for WTI if the Gulf conflict intensifies in Q3/Q4 2026.
*   **Aviation Warning:** Airline stocks are highly vulnerable to oil price spikes and airspace restrictions in Q3/Q4 2026.

> "We're approaching the 36-year cycle from the invasion of Kuwait... that Iran situation isn't going to end soon and could well see a very rapid escalation... I have a target oil price of $183 to $185."
> — *Andrew Pancholi*

#### 4. Gold and Bitcoin Forecasts
*   **Gold Target ($6,900):** Gold is expected to remain soft and correct into **September 2026** (following a 36-year gold cycle). From this low, a major rally is projected into **March 2027**, targeting **$6,900/oz** due to oil-driven inflation and geopolitical instability.
*   **Bitcoin (Bearish):** Bitcoin is showing highly bearish COT setups, suggesting the asset will continue to struggle and trend lower into the autumn.

> "Gold... we're probably going to be soft into round about September this year... then I could see a very sharp move up into March 2027... I do have a target of 6,900."
> — *Andrew Pancholi*

#### 5. Threat and Error Management: The Pilot's Parallel
Pancholi details how flying Boeing 747 jets applies directly to trading:
*   **Checklist Discipline:** Pilots fly by checklists; traders must execute using similar rigid rules to eliminate emotional biases.
*   **Exits Before Entries:** A pilot never takes off without knowing their primary destination and alternates (e.g., Boston if New York weather closes). Traders must define their exit points before placing a trade.
*   **No Black Swans:** Pancholi argues that the COVID-19 pandemic was not a black swan; it was a predictable 100-year cycle mirroring the 1918 Spanish Flu, 1817 Cholera, and 1620 Smallpox.

> "When we fly a big jet... we know our exit before our entry... Why is one industry got a 100% success rate and why is the other got a 5% success rate? [It's] risk management and situational awareness."
> — *Andrew Pancholi*
