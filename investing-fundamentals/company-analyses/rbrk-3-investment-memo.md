# Rubrik, Inc. (NYSE: RBRK) — Investment Memo

**Ticker:** NYSE: RBRK | **Sector:** Cybersecurity / Data Security

## 1. Executive Summary

### Investment Thesis
Rubrik is a high-conviction, long-duration growth investment for investors with a 3-5 year horizon. The company has successfully repositioned legacy enterprise data backup as a mission-critical cybersecurity platform, creating deep switching costs and a land-and-expand revenue engine that sustains >120% NRR. With $1.46B in subscription ARR (+34% YoY), 80%+ gross margins, $237.8M in free cash flow (18% margin), and a clear path toward sustained FCF generation, Rubrik is crossing into a phase where durable, profitable growth is increasingly visible. The secular tailwinds of ransomware proliferation, cloud data sprawl, and regulatory mandates (NIS2, SEC cyber rules) structurally support multi-year demand.

### Bull Case
Rubrik expands its platform moat through rapid adoption of security modules (Identity, DSPM, Threat Analytics), sustains NRR >120%, and grows ARR toward $3B by FY2029. As FCF margins expand to 25%+, the market re-rates the stock from a high-growth software name to a compounding platform business — similar to CrowdStrike's trajectory.

### Bear Case
ARR growth decelerates below 25% as Cohesity/Veritas integration stabilizes competitive dynamics, hyperscalers enhance native backup security features, and budget-conscious enterprises trade down. GAAP losses persist due to SBC, and the market de-rates from premium multiples — a 40-50% drawdown scenario.

### Target Investor Profile
- **Primary:** Growth-oriented institutional and sophisticated individual investors with 3-5 year horizons
- **Secondary:** Thematic cybersecurity investors seeking platform leaders with defensible economics
- **Not suitable for:** Income investors (no dividend, GAAP losses), value investors (elevated multiples), short-term traders

## 3. Competitive Position & Moat

### Market Position
The enterprise data protection and cyber resilience market is estimated at $20B+ TAM, growing at 12-15% CAGR, with a faster-growing security overlay.

**Gartner Recognition:** Named a Leader in the 2025 Magic Quadrant for Backup and Data Protection Platforms — positioned furthest in Completeness of Vision.

**Market Share Dynamics (FY2025-2026):**
- Rubrik and Veeam are the two fastest-growing vendors gaining share
- Cohesity-Veritas merger created a large private competitor with combined >$1B ARR — the most credible scaled alternative
- Legacy vendors (IBM, Dell EMC, Commvault) continue to lose share in modern cloud environments

### Moat Classification

| Moat Type | Evidence | Durability |
|-----------|----------|------------|
| **Switching Costs** (Primary) | NRR >120%; 2,805 enterprise customers with >$100K ARR representing 87% of total | HIGH — replacing core data protection infrastructure is a 12-18 month project |
| **Platform Breadth** | 4 product pillars (Protection, Threat Analytics, DSPM, Identity); unique cross-workload coverage | MEDIUM-HIGH — breadth creates integration lock-in |
| **Data-Informed AI** | Anomaly detection improves with behavioral baselines; each customer's data makes models better | MEDIUM — early-stage moat; depends on AI product execution |
| **Brand/Trust** | Security is trust-based; Rubrik brand is strong among CISOs | MEDIUM — requires sustained execution |

### Key Competitors

| Competitor | Type | Threat Level | Notes |
|------------|------|-------------|-------|
| Cohesity + Veritas | Private | HIGH | Combined entity has scale; integration execution is the wild card |
| Veeam | Private | MEDIUM-HIGH | Largest install base; catching up on security features |
| Commvault ($CVLT) | Public | MEDIUM | Co-exists; both win in large enterprise; Commvault's "Cleanroom" product targets recovery use case |

| Druva | Private | LOW-MEDIUM | Cloud-native, narrower; Rubrik wins on breadth |

### Differentiation
Rubrik's key advantage is being the **only vendor** that combines immutable backup + AI threat detection + DSPM + Identity in a unified cloud platform. Competitors either focus on backup OR security — Rubrik does both. This "security-led backup" narrative resonates with CISO-controlled budgets.

## 5. Risk Framework with Failure Modes

### Top 5 Risks

| Rank | Risk | Probability | Severity | Mitigation |
|------|------|------------|----------|------------|
| 1 | ARR Growth Deceleration | MEDIUM | HIGH | Already decelerating (39%→34%); monitor trend vs. guidance |
| 2 | Cohesity/Veritas Integration Success | MEDIUM | HIGH | If merger executes cleanly, creates a scaled, AI-backed private competitor with combined Veritas enterprise relationships |
| 3 | Hyperscaler Native Competition | LOW-MEDIUM (near-term), HIGH (long-term) | HIGH | AWS Backup, Azure Backup improving; lack of cross-cloud breadth today but trajectory matters |
| 4 | GAAP Profitability Gap (SBC Dilution) | HIGH (certainty of occurrence) | MEDIUM | Heavy SBC dilutes shareholders; non-GAAP narrative may lose credibility if FCF stalls |
| 5 | Enterprise IT Budget Compression | MEDIUM | MEDIUM | Macro-driven; security budgets are stickier than other IT, but not immune to CIO freeze |

### Failure Mode Detail

**Risk 1 — ARR Deceleration:**
- Trigger: New logo growth slows as market saturates top-1000 enterprises; NRR compresses as customers resist security module upsell in tight budgets
- Impact: Multiple compression from 15-20x ARR to 8-10x ARR; 40-50% stock decline
- Mitigation: Monitor quarterly net new ARR (not % growth); $100K+ customer CAGR; security module attach rate

**Risk 2 — Cohesity/Veritas:**
- Trigger: Cohesity successfully integrates Veritas NetBackup (massive installed base), launches competitive SaaS platform with integrated threat analytics
- Impact: Increased churn in mid-market; slower new logo wins; pricing pressure
- Mitigation: Rubrik's multi-cloud breadth and Identity product are differentiators Cohesity doesn't yet match; watch for Cohesity IPO/funding news

**Risk 3 — Hyperscalers:**
- Trigger: AWS Backup or Azure Backup adds AI threat detection, cross-SaaS coverage, and immutable storage at fraction of Rubrik price
- Impact: Existential long-term threat in 5-10 year timeframe if Rubrik doesn't continue innovating
- Mitigation: True multi-cloud heterogeneity (Rubrik supports ALL clouds simultaneously); identity and DSPM layers; hyperscalers are unlikely to compete in cross-cloud threat detection

**Risk 4 — SBC / GAAP Dilution:**
- Trigger: Share count grows 5-8% annually from SBC; GAAP EPS never achieves sustainability
- Impact: Institutional investors require GAAP profitability; sustained SBC drives NAV dilution
- Mitigation: FCF is the right metric; SBC as % of revenue should decline over time

**Risk 5 — IT Budget Compression:**
- Trigger: Macro recession causes CIOs to defer renewals or renegotiate contracts downward
- Impact: NRR compresses; new logo ACV declines
- Mitigation: Ransomware recovery is considered non-discretionary by most boards post-2023; NIS2 mandates create compliance-driven demand in Europe

### Pre-Mortem
> "If this investment fails in 3 years, the most likely reason is: Rubrik's ARR growth decelerated below 20% as Cohesity/Veritas successfully integrated and re-engaged enterprise accounts with a competitive SaaS offering, while hyperscaler native tools eroded the SMB segment. With revenue growth insufficient to justify a premium multiple, and persistent GAAP losses from SBC, the stock re-rated to 6-8x revenue, delivering a -50% drawdown from entry."

### Key Assumptions That Must Hold
1. NRR sustains above 115% (ideally >120%)
2. Platform cross-sell (Identity, DSPM, Threat Analytics) drives incremental ARR from existing base
3. Gross margins sustain above 78% GAAP, expanding toward 83%+
4. FCF margins expand toward 20-25% by FY2028
5. Cohesity/Veritas integration takes 2-3 years to fully compete at Rubrik's breadth
6. Hyperscalers do NOT launch comprehensive cross-cloud threat-detection-native backup in the next 3 years
