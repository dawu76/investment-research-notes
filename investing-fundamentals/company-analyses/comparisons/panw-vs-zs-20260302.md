## Part 1: Incremental Investment Dollars — Which Is the Better Bet?

**The short answer:** PANW is the better risk-adjusted investment for most long-term investors adding to the sector today. ZS is the better bet if you want pure-play upside with higher concentration risk.

### The Bull Case for PANW Today

PANW is executing one of the most ambitious strategic pivots in enterprise software history — converting 70,000+ firewall customers into full-platform subscribers, and filling the last major gap (identity) with the CyberArk acquisition. The FCF machine (37–38% margin on $9.2B revenue) is exceptional. A platform NRR of 119% on ~1,550 customers — still a small fraction of the total base — means the upsell runway is enormous. The RPO of $15.8B provides extraordinary multi-year revenue visibility. You are buying a business with defensive FCF characteristics priced more like a mature compounder than a high-growth story.

The key CyberArk insight: identity is the #1 attack vector (80%+ of breaches involve compromised credentials). CyberArk is the gold standard in privileged access management. PANW just bought the category leader in the fastest-growing attack surface and bolted it onto a platform of 70,000 customers. The synergy setup is compelling even if integration is messy in the near term.

### The Bull Case for ZS Today

ZS is the purest proxy for the secular shift to zero trust networking — the architectural replacement of the legacy VPN/firewall perimeter model. At 34% SSE market share with 115% NRR, ZS has a proven land-and-expand engine. The Zero Trust Exchange handles 500+ billion daily transactions (nearly 1 trillion AI transactions in calendar 2025), creating a data moat that trains better threat detection models. Q2 FY2026 demonstrated strong execution: $815.8M revenue (+26% YoY), $3.36B ARR (+25%), 728 customers at $1M+ ARR (up from 664), RPO of $6.1B (+31%), and Americas $1M+ deal count 2x YoY. ZFlex flexible consumption TCV reached $290M+ in Q2 (+65% sequentially), signaling enterprises are making large multi-year platform bets. If you believe enterprise zero trust adoption is still early innings (it is — most enterprise perimeters still run legacy VPNs), ZS is the direct beneficiary.

### The Risk That Separates Them

ZS's top risk scored **20** (severity 5 × probability 4) — **competitive bundling**. Microsoft, PANW, and CRWD all have SSE-adjacent capabilities they are willing to discount or bundle to win platform deals. ZS's single-product exposure to this bundling creates a binary risk: either ZS wins by proving its architectural superiority justifies a standalone contract, or the SSE market commoditizes faster than expected.

Q2 FY2026 added a second significant risk to watch: **organic ARR deceleration** (net new ARR ex-Red Canary +7% YoY in Q2, improving to +10% in H1 vs. +1% in prior H1). Combined with calculated billings decelerating to +18% YoY (from 32%), the market is right to scrutinize whether H2 FY2026 core ARR re-accelerates as management expects. ZS management pointed to strong pipeline and seasonality, but the burden of proof is on Q3 FY2026 results (April 2026).

PANW's top risk scored **15** — **CyberArk integration**. This is meaningful but more controllable: integration failure is a known risk with defined remediation paths. Platform bundling commoditization is structural and harder to reverse.

### Bottom Line

**Preferred for incremental dollars:** PANW on valuation-adjusted risk/return. ZS if you are already overweight PANW and want purer leverage to the zero trust secular thesis.

The one condition to flip the preference: if the market enters a genuine risk-on, growth-at-any-price regime where high-multiple pure-plays are rewarded, ZS will outperform PANW meaningfully on a 12-month price-return basis. PANW's comparative advantage is durable fundamentals and downside protection; ZS's comparative advantage is leverage to growth sentiment and the zero trust adoption cycle.

## Part 2: Macro & Industry Conditions

### ZS Outperforms When...

| Condition | Why ZS Benefits | Mechanism |
|-----------|----------------|-----------|
| **Cloud-first migration accelerating** | ZS is the network backbone for cloud workloads and remote users — every cloud migration is a ZS sales motion | TAM expansion into previously addressable accounts |
| **Remote/hybrid work structurally elevated** | ZPA is the VPN replacement; distributed workforces can't be secured with a perimeter | Greenfield demand, not replacement cycles |
| **Risk-on, growth-premium environment** | High EV/revenue multiple expands when market rewards growth over profitability | Multiple expansion drives returns even with modest estimate beats |
| **Regulatory/compliance tailwinds** | ZIA's inline inspection and DLP are often the path of least resistance to GDPR, NIS2, CMMC compliance | Mandated spend vs. discretionary |
| **Best-of-breed budget cycles** | When CISOs have budget flexibility, they prefer best-of-breed — ZS wins the SSE category head-to-head | Average deal sizes expand; NRR stays elevated |
| **AI application proliferation** | More application traffic, more data egress, more API calls → more to inspect and secure inline | Volume expansion in the Zero Trust Exchange |

### ZS Underperforms When...

| Condition | Why ZS Struggles | Mechanism |
|-----------|-----------------|-----------|
| **Enterprise budget austerity** | CISOs consolidate vendors; ZS "best-of-breed SSE" loses to PANW "full platform at a discount" | Platformization deals close at ZS's expense |
| **Microsoft E5 bundle adoption** | M365 E5 includes Defender, Entra, and Microsoft Entra Internet Access (ZTNA equivalent) — "good enough" for M365-centric orgs | Reduces greenfield ZS opportunity |
| **PANW Prisma SASE aggressive pricing** | PANW bundles SSE into platformization deals at discounts ZS cannot match standalone | ZS loses competitive bakeoffs to bundled PANW |
| **Risk-off / quality rotation** | ZS has the highest EV/revenue multiple among peers — not GAAP profitable — and de-rates fastest | Multiple compression outweighs any earnings beat |
| **ARR growth deceleration** | Market expectations baked in at 20%+ ARR growth; total ARR at +25% is fine, but core ex-Red Canary at +21% and net new ARR ex-RC at +7% in Q2 are bears watching | Any further deceleration in organic ARR triggers estimate cuts |
| **Billings deceleration** | Calculated billings slowed to +18% YoY in Q2 FY2026 (from 32% in Q4 FY2025) — a 2–3 quarter lead indicator for revenue | If Q3 billings don't rebound above 22%, consensus ARR estimates for H2 FY2026 require cuts |
| **Recession / delayed cloud migrations** | If enterprises defer new cloud/hybrid investments, ZS's greenfield motion slows — unlike PANW's renewal-heavy base | NRR at risk if module expansion pauses |

The deceleration from 32% → 18% is the most significant risk signal in Q2. Below 15% YoY is a hard red flag. |
| **#5** | **FCF margin** | ~27% (FY2025); 21% Q2 FY2026 (qtr); guided 26.5–27% FY2026 | Market expects gradual expansion toward 30%+. FY2026 guided FCF margin of 26.5–27% is flat vs. FY2025, signaling integration costs are absorbing the operating leverage. |

| **#7** | **International revenue mix** | **~43%** (EMEA 28% + APJ 15%, Q2 FY2026) — shifted from ~49% at FY2025 | Americas surged to 57% of revenue (+31% YoY) as the US drove record $1M+ deal volumes. The ZS international diversification edge vs. PANW has narrowed: PANW is 33% international, ZS is now 43% (down from 49%). Still an advantage, but less pronounced. |
| **#8** | **ZIA + ZPA + ZDX + Red Canary/SPLX attach rate** | Not separately disclosed | Platform density metric. 3+ pillar customers have dramatically lower churn. Red Canary and SPLX (acquired Q1 FY2026, ~$692M) are the newest upsell vectors — cross-sell rate into installed base is a key emerging indicator. |

**ZS red flag triggers:**
- NRR below 113%
- Net new ARR below $140M in any quarter (calibrated to Q2 FY2026 actual of $155.5M)
- Organic ARR growth (ex-Red Canary) failing to re-accelerate above 22% by Q4 FY2026
- Calculated billings below 18% YoY (Q2 FY2026 actual: +18% — already at the edge)
- Any disclosure of continued elevated Red Canary churn beyond Q3 FY2026
- Any disclosed large customer churn above $5M ARR (signals competitive displacement)
