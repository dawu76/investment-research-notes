# Sea Limited ($SE): Equity Report

**Price:** $77.93 (October 21, 2026, late-session reading from stockanalysis.com; intraday low $76.00, a new 52-week low), down 18% from $95.19 on October 2. Closes on the three big down days were $93.77 (Oct 16), $88.47 (Oct 19) and $80.43 (Oct 20). **Prior 52-week range:** $77.05-$171.09 (Morningstar; Morgan Stanley shows a $193.47 high, which likely includes an earlier intraday print). **Market cap:** ~$43B on Morningstar's basic share count (~557M ADS-equivalent) or ~$50B on Morgan Stanley's 638M diluted shares. **Net cash:** ~$11.5B implied by MS (EV $49.2B vs. market cap $60.8B at $95.19). Cash plus short-term investments were $9.10B on June 30, 2026, with $2.76B more in long-term investments. **Next report:** Q3 2026 on November 10, 2026 (tentative).

## Executive Summary

Sea runs three businesses in Southeast Asia, Taiwan and Brazil. **Shopee** is the region's largest e-commerce marketplace (FY2025 GMV $127.4B; ~52% of Southeast Asian GMV in 2024 per Morningstar/Momentum Works). **Monee** lends to Shopee's buyers and sellers, and increasingly to people outside Shopee (loan book $11.1B, +62%). **Garena** publishes games, mainly Free Fire. FY2025 revenue was $22.9B (+36%), net income $1.6B and adjusted EBITDA $3.4B.

The mix of revenue and profit is lopsided. Shopee earns about 72% of revenue but only ~25% of segment adjusted EBITDA, because it runs at ~0.7% of GMV against a long-run target of 2-3%. Garena, a one-hit gaming franchise, earns the most profit ($1.66B adjusted EBITDA in FY2025). Monee grows fastest but is a credit business whose margins are falling as it lends to riskier, off-platform borrowers. The investment case is that Shopee's margin roughly triples over five years while Monee compounds. The risk is that competition from TikTok Shop, Temu/PDD and Mercado Libre forces Shopee to keep reinvesting, so the margin never arrives.

**In plain English:** Southeast Asia's Amazon-plus-bank, funded by a mobile game. It is profitable and growing ~35-48%, but the market doubts management will ever stop reinvesting the profits.

## What They Sell and Who Buys

* **Shopee (e-commerce; FY2025 revenue $16.6B, 72% of group):** a mobile-first marketplace where third-party sellers (mostly small merchants plus brand "Mall" stores) sell to consumers in Indonesia, Taiwan, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Brazil. Shopee also sells logistics (SPX Express, fulfillment, instant delivery), advertising, a VIP membership with free delivery, and livestream/short-video shopping. Buyers want the lowest price with free, fast shipping. Sellers want traffic. In Q2 2026, content-driven orders (livestream and video) were 25% of Southeast Asian physical-goods orders and grew 50%+ (Morningstar, Aug 12, 2026).
  * **Market position:** above 50% GMV share in every Southeast Asian market except Indonesia (46%), and over 50% in Taiwan (Morningstar). In Brazil, Shopee app MAUs grew 11% y/y in September 2026, against 16% for Mercado Libre (MS, Sensor Tower).
* **Monee (digital financial services; FY2025 revenue $3.8B, 17%):** SPayLater (buy-now-pay-later at Shopee checkout), SPayLater off-Shopee, cash loans, SME loans to Shopee sellers, ShopeePay wallet, MariBank digital banks (Singapore, Philippines licensed July 2026) and insurance. Customers are under-banked consumers and small merchants. Morningstar estimates 50-70% of Southeast Asia is unbanked or under-banked.
* **Garena (digital entertainment; FY2025 revenue $2.4B, bookings $2.9B, 11%):** Free Fire, a battle-royale shooter built for low-end Android phones, with 100M+ average daily active users (Q2 2026 release). Players buy in-game items. Biggest markets are ASEAN, Latin America and India (Free Fire MAX returned to India in 2025). Morningstar estimates Free Fire is 60-70% of Garena revenue. The pipeline includes Tencent-licensed titles (Monster Hunter Outlanders, targeted for 2026; Palworld Online).
* **New in October 2026, Sea Cloud:** an "AI-first enterprise platform" for outside customers, built on Sea's internal cloud by about 500 staff over two years (CEO memo, October 20, 2026 6-K). There is no revenue, pricing, capex or customer data yet. The in-house base is already large: about 25,000 GPUs, more than 150,000 servers, 40+ data centers and more than 100 MW of critical IT load. At NVIDIA's AI Day Singapore (September 22-23, 2026), Sea was named the first ASEAN enterprise to adopt Vera Rubin, with no size disclosed (CRN Asia, Cloudnews). Data Center Dynamics described Sea Cloud as a "neocloud subsidiary."

## How They Make Money

* **Shopee:** transaction-based. Core marketplace revenue (commissions, transaction fees and ads) was $4.26B in Q2 2026 (+65.6%), an ~11.1% take rate on $38.3B GMV. Value-added services revenue (mainly logistics) was $0.68B (-9.0%). Shopee raises seller fees regularly. Shopee Brazil lifted its fixed commission on items under R$80 from R$4.00 to R$4.50 on October 1, 2026 (MS). MS expects the core take rate to rise to 11.3% in Q3 2026. Morningstar models mid-teens by the mid-2030s, driven mainly by ads.
* **Monee:** interest and fee income on a $11.1B loan book ($10.0B on balance sheet, $1.1B sold or funded off-book), plus payments fees. It is spread-based and recurring while the book is outstanding, but sensitive to credit losses.
* **Garena:** in-game purchases (microtransactions). Revenue is recognized over the life of the virtual items, so bookings lead revenue. It is hit-driven and swings with content (anime collaborations such as Naruto and Jujutsu Kaisen drive spikes).

## Revenue Quality

* **Recurring vs. one-off:** most revenue repeats but is not contracted. Shopee's commissions recur with consumer spending (13.9B orders in FY2025, so no customer concentration). Monee's interest income is the most predictable stream while the book grows. Garena's is the least predictable: one title, content-driven, with history of collapse (bookings fell 40% in 2022 and 34% in 2023 after the India ban and fading engagement; Morningstar).
* **Geographic concentration:** Indonesia is the largest market for Shopee (181M Shopee MAU in September 2026, ~a third of worldwide MAU; MS/Sensor Tower). Brazil is the main growth market outside Asia. Currency exposure is broad: MS expects a ~3-4% FX headwind to Q3 2026 GMV as the rupiah weakened 9% y/y.
* **Growth is slowing on tougher comparisons:** GMV growth was ~30% in Q1 2026 and 28.4% in Q2, with ~25% guided for 2026. MS models 25% for Q3, then 20% in 2027 and 16% in 2028. Revenue growth (+48% in Q2) is running well ahead of GMV because take rates are rising and Monee is growing.
* **Profit quality is mixed:** adjusted EBITDA grew only 10.6% in Q2 against 48% revenue growth. Shopee and Monee are spending on users (S&M +64.5% to $1.66B, or 21% of revenue) and Monee provisions rose 71.5% to $555M. The effective tax rate is moving to the mid-to-high 30s as more entities turn profitable (management, March 2026), so net income grows more slowly than EBIT.

## Cost Structure

* **Gross margin:** 44.7% in FY2025 ($10.24B gross profit) and 45.6% in Q2 2026. Cost of revenue is mostly logistics and fulfillment for Shopee, payment-processing and funding costs for Monee, and content licensing/royalties for Garena.
* **Operating expenses (Q2 2026):** sales and marketing $1.66B (21.3% of revenue; shipping subsidies, vouchers, VIP benefits and Monee user acquisition), provision for credit losses $555M (7.1% of revenue; ~40% of Monee revenue), G&A $394M (5.1%), R&D $314M (4.0%). Operating income was $650M (8.4% margin).
* **Operating margin trend:** 0.9% (2023), 2.9% (2024), 8.1% (2025, Morningstar basis). Morningstar models 9.1% for 2026 and 12.7% for 2030. Operating income grew 33.3% in Q2, which is far behind revenue.
* **Scalability:** the platform costs (tech, G&A) scale well: R&D grew only 5.6% in Q2. The variable costs do not: shipping subsidies, vouchers and credit losses grow with volume. Management decided in March 2026 to rent warehouses close to customers for faster delivery. That spending runs through operating expenses rather than capex, because Sea rents its facilities. Management said those investments should pay off by Q4 2026 and set a higher bar for 2027 margins (Morningstar, March 4, 2026).

## Capital Intensity

* **Capex is light by design.** Sea rents logistics hubs, sorting centers, warehouses and trucks (Morningstar; CEO on the Q2 call: "we don't own land or warehouses"). This keeps capex low but moves logistics investment into operating expenses.
* **Working capital is favorable in e-commerce and heavy in fintech.** Shopee collects from buyers before paying sellers, so its float funds the business. Monee's loan growth absorbs capital: the book rose from $9.2B (December 2025) to $11.1B (June 2026). Funding comes mainly from MariBank deposits, the balance sheet and off-book sales.
* **Cash conversion:** FY2025 operating cash flow was $5.02B against $1.61B of net income. That gap includes Shopee's payables float and Monee-related flows, so it overstates owner cash. H1 2026 operating cash flow was $2.56B. Morningstar's free cash flow to the firm falls from $3.79B (2025) to $1.79B (2026e) as the loan book and investments grow, then recovers to $2.3B (2027e) and $3.3B (2028e).
* **Balance sheet:** cash $3.53B and short-term investments $5.57B (June 30, 2026), with $2.76B of long-term investments. Convertible notes of ~$1.05B were classified as current at December 2025. Sea repurchased $578M of stock in H1 2026 ($416.8M in Q2) under a $1.0B program.
* **Sea Cloud is the new swing factor.** Running AI workloads for outside customers usually means owning or leasing GPU capacity, which is far more capital-intensive than anything Sea does today. The memo gave no capex or funding plan.
* **Returns:** Morningstar shows ROIC of 45.7% (2025) and ~60% (2026-30e), flattered by negative working capital in e-commerce. ROE is 12.7% (2025) and 14.8% (2026e). MS's ROE is 18.8% (2025) and 15.5% (2026e).

## Growth Drivers

* **Structural:**
  * **E-commerce penetration** in Southeast Asia (4-29% of retail across major markets vs. 52% in China; eMarketer via Morningstar) and Brazil. Morningstar models a 13% ten-year GMV CAGR, with a 16% CAGR for 2025-34 in the core regions.
  * **Take-rate and ad monetization:** Shopee's core marketplace take rate was ~11.1% in Q2 2026, and MS expects 11.3% in Q3 2026. Core marketplace revenue grew 65.6% against 28.4% GMV growth. Management says ad take rates have "meaningful optimization potential."
  * **Shopee margin ramp:** adjusted EBITDA/GMV of 0.67% (Q2 2026) toward 2-3% long term. Management called the path to "1-plus percent" "relatively straightforward" (CEO, Q2 call). MS models 0.85% in 2027 and 1.1% in 2028. Morningstar uses 2.5% by 2035.
  * **Monee credit penetration:** loan book +62% y/y. Growth comes from SPayLater off-Shopee, offline cash loans, larger loans as users build credit history, and new markets (Brazil, Philippines). MS forecasts DFS revenue +56% (2026), +37% (2027) and +24% (2028).
  * **Brazil:** Shopee is growing "well above the market" (CEO). MS's report "Sea's Samba" asks whether Brazil becomes Sea's largest market.
* **Cyclical/episodic:**
  * **Garena content cycles:** Free Fire revenue fell 5% y/y in Q3 2026 (Sensor Tower), then grew 15% y/y in September with a Naruto return. The 10th anniversary in 2027 and Tencent-licensed launches are upside, but these are hit-driven.
  * **FX:** a strong dollar against the rupiah and baht reduces reported growth (2026 headwind).
  * **Interest rates:** rate cuts in Malaysia and Thailand compressed Monee margins in 2025 (Morningstar, Nov 2025).
* **Optional:** a Monee IPO (DBS values Monee at $18-22B on 12-15x 2027 adjusted EBITDA of ~$1.5B, against ~$6B implied in Sea's price) and Sea Cloud (no disclosed economics).

## Competitive Edge

* **Moat classification:** Morningstar rates the group Narrow, from Shopee's two-sided network effect (more buyers bring more sellers and selection, which brings more buyers). Garena and Monee get no moat: Free Fire is one hit with no proven repeat, and Monee is small next to regional banks.
* **Evidence for:**
  * Shopee keeps 50%+ GMV share in most markets despite TikTok Shop's entry and the Tokopedia merger.
  * Shopee and TikTok both raised fees in Q4 2025 and 2026. Morningstar reads this as a rational market where higher revenue can offset competitive costs.
  * Shopee says its in-house logistics costs are below third-party rates, and instant-delivery cost per order fell ~20% in Indonesia (Q2 call).
  * Monee's 90-day NPL ratio has held at ~1.0-1.1% while the book grew 62%. That suggests Shopee's transaction data helps underwriting.
* **Evidence against:**
  * Shopee's margin (0.67% of GMV) is a fraction of Alibaba's (~2.5% per Morningstar) or Mercado Libre's. A strong network effect usually shows up as pricing power, and Shopee still has to subsidize shipping and vouchers to hold share.
  * TikTok Shop was projected to match Shopee's parcel volume in 2026 (Momentum Works via Morningstar, Feb 2026). J&T Express's Southeast Asian parcel volume rose 74% in Q4 2025, mostly from TikTok Shop.
  * TikTok MAU grows faster than Shopee's (+15% vs. +5% in ASEAN, September 2026; MS).
  * PDD's Pinduoduo app is gaining downloads in Malaysia and Singapore even as Temu tapers (MS). Pinduoduo's main app launched in Malaysia in December 2025, dropped its free-shipping minimum in August 2026 and reached No. 1 in shopping-app downloads there. DBS estimates 10-13% of Shopee's GMV is in markets exposed to it (October 1, 2026 downgrade).
  * Shopee's response costs money: quick commerce, VIP benefits and its Migoo AI shopping assistant are the spending DBS cites for forecasting 2027 e-commerce adjusted EBITDA 5% below consensus.
  * Switching costs for buyers are low (Morningstar's bear case).
* **Emerging threat:** AI shopping agents such as Meta's Muse, which completes purchases for users and takes a cut, could route buyers around Shopee's search and ad placements (see [[market-newsletter-digest-2026-09-22]] and [[market-newsletter-digest-2026-09-24]]). MS lists "potential Muse disruption" among Sea-specific concerns. Meta's Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp are heavily used in Southeast Asia, which makes this more relevant to Shopee than to most marketplaces.
* **Durability assessment:** Shopee's scale advantage looks durable. Its pricing power does not yet. The moat shows up in market share and in the ability to raise fees, but not yet in margins. Until Shopee's EBITDA/GMV moves above ~1%, the claim that Southeast Asian e-commerce will look like China's (profitable leaders) is a forecast, not evidence.
