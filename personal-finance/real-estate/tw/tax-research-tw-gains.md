## Context: Entire 觀海極品 Complex Is 工業宅

The entire 觀海極品 complex — all 9 buildings, spanning street addresses at both 民族路31之X號 and 民族路29弄X號 — sits on land zoned **乙種工業區 (Type B Industrial Zone)**. PLEX, which tracks this building specifically, names it "觀海極品(工業區)" and states verbatim: **「土地分區為工業區，為工業宅，由關平建設所興建」**. Housefun lists a sold unit in the complex as "工廠/乙種工業區". A Mobile01 forum thread on Zhuwei housing explicitly groups 觀海極品 with 關渡大國 as buildings on industrial land: **「竹圍地區大都是10年左右的大廈，又有兩種：工業用地和一般住宅用地，其中關渡大國和觀海極品好像都是工業用地」**.

The two separate Housefun database entries (building ID 836 at 民族路29弄2, 66號 and building ID 27174 at 民族路31號) are a cataloguing artifact — different street-facing entrances to the same 工業宅 parcel — not evidence of a residential subsection with different zoning. See [Appendix](#appendix-industrial-zone-classification--research-notes) for sourcing detail.

**Implication for all research:** The wide price range across databases (38.9–46.8万/坪) reflects floor-level, unit-size, and transaction-timing variation *within a single 工業宅 complex*, not a blend of residential and industrial pricing tiers. Use same-building comparables (民族路31之25號 transactions) rather than the whole-complex average to anchor the appraisal.

### Key Price Trend Observation

The 觀海極品 complex has appreciated only modestly — roughly **5–10% total from 2022 to 2026** — while the district-wide mainstream residential market rose ~17% in the same period. This is consistent with how 工業宅 properties behave: the restricted buyer pool (cash buyers or non-bank financing only) dampens both price level and price momentum relative to the conventional residential market.

The market condition in H2 2022 (the inheritance registration period) was "**價穩量縮**" (prices stable, transaction volume down ~38%) — meaning prices did not meaningfully decline or spike between May and August 2022.

## Implications for Tax Plan

### Taiwan Side (Phase 2 — HSTT cost basis)

The government-assessed values (公告現值 + 房屋評定現值) used for HSTT cost basis are unrelated to FMV. The market value research here does not change Phase 2 calculations. The plan's estimated assessed values of NT$3–4.5M (land) + NT$500K–800K (building, central NT$700K) remain the relevant basis figures.

### US Side (Phase 3 — IRC §1014 stepped-up basis)

- **Effective date for US purposes:** May 2022 (date of death) — not the 登記日 of July/August 2022. In practice the price difference is immaterial since Tamsui 工業宅 prices were flat in that window.
- **FMV estimate:** NT$13–14M for a ~34坪 unit at May 2022 prices (~NT$40万/坪) — living unit only. Add NT$900K–NT$1,200K if a parking space is included in the inherited property.
- **USD equivalent:** At the May 2022 TWD/USD spot rate (approximately NT$29.5/USD) → **~$441K–$475K USD** (central: ~$460K) for a 34坪 unit.
- **Capital gain observation:** With a 2026 sale price of roughly NT$14–14.5M and a 2022 FMV of roughly NT$13–14M, the TWD gain is small (~NT$0.5–1.5M). The USD gain will be dominated by the TWD/USD exchange rate change from 2022 to 2026, not by property appreciation — consistent with the plan's currency effect note.

### Taiwan Side: Old System (April 2012 Purchase)

If the property was purchased in 2012, the **Old System applies** (pre-Jan 1, 2016 acquisition). Two separate taxes replace HSTT: (1) building income tax on the building's share of the gain, and (2) Land Value Increment Tax (LVIT / 土地增值稅) on the government-assessed land appreciation since the original purchase date. Neither tax touches the full market-vs.-assessed gap that drives HSTT's large base.

*Combined holding period: April 2012 purchase → May 2022 death (~10 years) + post-inheritance hold ~2022–2026 (~4 years) = **~14 years** via the tack-on rule. The 20-year long-hold discount does not apply.*

**Building income tax** (split uses 2026 assessed values at time of sale — not inheritance values):

### Old System vs. New System: 2012 Purchase Comparison (30坪)

| System | Taiwan tax (NT$) | Taiwan tax (USD) | Difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HSTT — New System** | ~NT$1,140K | ~$38,000 | — |
| **Old System (2012 purchase)** | ~NT$622K | ~$20,700 | **Old System saves ~NT$518K (~$17,300)** |

**Why Old System saves money:** HSTT taxes the full NT$7.6M market-vs.-assessed gap at 15%. The Old System never touches that gap — it only taxes the much smaller building gain (~NT$1.06M) and the government-assessed land increment (NT$1.9M), for a combined Old System base of ~NT$3.0M versus NT$7.6M under HSTT. Even at similar or slightly higher marginal rates on those smaller bases, the Old System total is far lower.

**FTC implication:** With Old System total Taiwan tax at ~$20,700 USD, the Foreign Tax Credit is smaller than under HSTT. However, at all plausible US gain levels for a 30坪 unit, the FTC still fully covers the federal income tax (see US side below).

### US Side: Capital Gain / (Loss) — Exchange Rate Sensitivity

The US gain/loss is acutely sensitive to the 2026 TWD/USD rate because the cost basis is locked at NT$29.5/USD (May 2022) while proceeds are converted at the 2026 closing rate. The **breakeven rate is approximately NT$30.2/USD** (unit only) — if the TWD is weaker than that at closing, the sale produces a USD loss and no US income tax exposure on the property gain.

**Unit only (parking excluded):**

| 2026 FX rate | US basis | 2026 gross proceeds | Less expenses | Amount realized | **US Capital Gain/(Loss)** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NT$29/USD (TWD strengthens) | $406,780 | $434,483 | ($10,345) | $424,138 | **+$17,358** |
| NT$30/USD (base case) | $406,780 | $420,000 | ($10,000) | $410,000 | **+$3,220** |
| NT$31/USD (TWD weakens) | $406,780 | $406,452 | ($9,677) | $396,775 | **−$10,005 (loss)** |
| NT$32/USD (TWD weakens more) | $406,780 | $393,750 | ($9,375) | $384,375 | **−$22,405 (loss)** |

*US basis = NT$12.0M ÷ NT$29.5 = $406,780. Gross proceeds = NT$12.6M ÷ [2026 rate]. Selling expenses = NT$300K ÷ [2026 rate]. Breakeven: (NT$12.6M − NT$0.3M) ÷ $406,780 ≈ NT$30.2/USD.*

### Federal Tax on US Gain (Where Gain Is Positive)

The Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) from Taiwan HSTT (~$37,500) far exceeds any plausible federal income tax on the gain, so the FTC eliminates federal income tax in all positive-gain scenarios. Residual US exposure is limited to NIIT and California state tax.

*Illustration: NT$29/USD scenario, unit only, $17,358 USD gain:*

| Layer | Rate | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term capital gains tax | 15% | $2,604 |
| Foreign Tax Credit (FTC from HSTT ~$38K) | Offsets income tax | ($2,604) — FTC eliminates this entirely |
| **Net federal income tax** | | **$0** |
| Net Investment Income Tax (NIIT) | 3.8% | **$660** — not offset by FTC |
| California (if CA resident, ~10% ordinary rate) | ~10% | **$1,736** — no CA foreign tax credit |
| **Total US tax (positive-gain scenario)** | | **~$2,396** |

*If the 2026 rate is NT$30.2+ (unit only) or NT$30.6+ (with parking) and results in a USD loss: no federal or California income tax is owed. A capital loss can offset other capital gains; up to $3,000/year can offset ordinary income, with any balance carrying forward.*

*US-side exposure (NIIT + California) is identical across both Taiwan regimes because the US gain depends only on the 2022 FMV and the 2026 exchange rate — not on whether mother bought in 2012 or 2015. **Central conclusion:** For a 30坪 unit with a 2012 purchase date, the **Old System saves ~$17,300 USD** in Taiwan tax relative to HSTT. In either scenario, Taiwan tax is the dominant liability; US federal exposure is eliminated by the FTC; and total US exposure reduces to NIIT + California — owed only if the TWD strengthens past ~NT$30.2/USD (unit only) or ~NT$30.6/USD (with parking) by closing.

### Why This Matters

The 乙種工業區 classification is legally significant regardless of how many database entries exist for the complex:

- Buyers cannot obtain standard residential bank mortgages (一般房貸). Financing is limited to cash or non-bank lenders, at loan-to-value ratios typically 50–60% vs. 70–80% for residential properties.
- The property is ineligible for government housing programs (新青安房貸, 重購退稅, rental subsidies).
- These restrictions structurally suppress price relative to comparable conventionally zoned condos of similar age and location in Zhuwei. The premium residential buildings in the area (e.g., 丰川 at ~57万/坪, 南加州 at ~50–54万/坪) trade higher partly because they may carry different zoning or were built more recently.
- A retroactive appraisal (不動產估價師 report) should explicitly document the 乙種工業區 designation so that an IRS examiner understands why the FMV is below what a surface comparison to Zhuwei residential prices might suggest.

### Historical Context

According to PLEX and forum discussion, the Zhuwei area was historically dominated by textile factories (紡織工廠), which is why much of the land along 民族路 retains an industrial zoning designation even though it has been entirely converted to residential use in practice. This is a common pattern in older Taiwanese urban districts where the original industrial character predates modern land-use reform.
