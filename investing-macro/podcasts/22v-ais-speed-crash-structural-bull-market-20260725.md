# AI’s Speed Crash: The Structural Bull Market Isn’t Over | 22V Research

Detailed overview of the market commentary video presentation by **Jordi Visser** (Head of AI Macro Research at 22V Research), published on July 25, 2026.

---

### I. Executive Summary

Jordi Visser analyzes the recent violent drawdown across mega-cap tech and AI momentum factors, arguing that the selloff represents a **"speed crash"** rather than the end of the structural AI bull market. He explains that algorithmic execution, systematic volatility-control strategies, and AI agents have transformed market microstructure, compressing what used to be multi-month market corrections into hyper-condensed multi-day liquidations. 

Crucially, corporate fundamentals remain robust—S&P 500 Q2 earnings are beating consensus by ~16%, credit spreads remain tight, and long-term compute demand is structurally constrained by memory supply shortages (SK Hynix, Samsung) that will persist through 2030. Visser advises investors to treat speed crashes as tactical buying opportunities in hardware bottleneck leaders rather than fundamental trend reversals.

### III. Detailed Key Takeaways

#### 1. The Anatomy of an Algorithmic "Speed Crash"
Visser details how modern market microstructure alters the speed and mechanics of equity drawdowns:
*   **Condensed Volatility Windows:** In modern markets governed by automated execution algorithms, CTA systematic models, and volatility-target funds, market corrections occur with unprecedented velocity. Multi-month cyclical selloffs are compressed into 5-to-10 trading days.
*   **Positioning Unwind vs. Fundamental Top:** Speed crashes are driven by factor crowding and forced de-leveraging rather than deterioration in economic fundamentals or corporate earnings power.

> "What we are experiencing is not a structural bull market top; it is a classic 'speed crash.' In an era dominated by systematic volatility-control funds and algorithmic execution, market corrections don't take months—they happen in days as leverage gets violently unwound."
> — *Jordi Visser*

#### 2. Robust Fundamental Earnings vs. Technical Panic
Despite sharp price declines in high-beta tech names, underlying macroeconomic data remains supportive:
*   **Strong Earnings Beat Rates:** S&P 500 Q2 earnings season data shows companies exceeding consensus EPS estimates by ~16%, supported by solid revenue expansion and cost discipline.
*   **Credit Market Confirmation:** Credit spreads (both high-yield and investment-grade) have remained calm and tight, confirming that fixed-income markets see zero signs of systemic credit stress or recessionary default risk.

> "If you look past the equity price noise at fundamental data, corporate earnings are beating by 16% and credit spreads are completely stable. Credit markets are telling you loud and clear that this is an equity factor rotation, not an economic crisis."
> — *Jordi Visser*

#### 3. Persistent Long-Term Compute Scarcity Through 2030
The long-term structural tailwind for AI compute infrastructure remains unchanged:
*   **Memory Supply Bottlenecks:** Physical production of High-Bandwidth Memory (HBM3e/HBM4) at key suppliers like SK Hynix and Samsung is fully booked years in advance. Memory silicon wafer constraints cannot be expanded overnight.
*   **Jevons Paradox in Token Demand:** As foundation model developers optimize inference efficiency and lower cost-per-token, total enterprise token consumption explodes exponentially, driving net higher aggregate hardware demand.

> "The compute scarcity story is far from over. High-bandwidth memory supply from SK Hynix and Samsung is locked up through the end of the decade. As AI agents scale and inference costs fall, the Jevons paradox ensures that aggregate demand for compute will outstripping supply well beyond 2030."
> — *Jordi Visser*

### IV. Portfolio Positioning & Action Items

*   **Overweight / Tactical Accumulation:**
    *   **Semiconductor Memory & Packaging Bottlenecks (`MU`, SK Hynix, `TSM`):** Accumulate Tier-1 hardware suppliers during speed crash drawdowns when valuations compress.
    *   **Broad S&P 500 & Equal-Weight Equities (`RSP`):** Maintain exposure to high-margin corporate earnings compounders benefiting from stable credit conditions.
*   **Underweight / Caution:**
    *   **High-Leverage Factor Momentum:** Avoid buying leveraged long-momentum ETFs during active systematic de-leveraging windows.
    *   **Non-Profitable Tech & Speculative Assets:** Maintain discipline against non-earning growth stocks vulnerable to rising real yields.
*   **Key Monitoring Indicators:**
    *   **Systematic Volatility Index (VIX & Vol-Control Flows):** Track VIX term structure and systematic fund re-leveraging triggers.
    *   **High-Yield Credit Spreads:** Monitor HY spreads for any signs of contagion from equity speed crashes.
    *   **Memory Wafer Allocation & Spot Pricing:** Watch DRAM/HBM contract negotiations for sustained pricing power.
