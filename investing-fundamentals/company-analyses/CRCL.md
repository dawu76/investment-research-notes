## Core Thesis Summary

Circle is the regulated tollbooth on the institutional tokenization of global finance. Supported by statutory moats under the **U.S. GENIUS Act**, an **OCC National Trust Bank charter**, and **EU MiCA compliance**, Circle is transitioning from an interest-rate spread asset into an institutional Layer-1 settlement platform (Arc Blockchain).

> [!WARNING]
> **Thesis Contested by Stage 4 Stress-Test (September 2, 2026):**
> While Circle commands an early structural moat, the Stage 4 stress-test arbitrated that CRCL's current valuation (~38x Net Retained Revenue, ~95x GAAP EPS) leaves zero margin of safety against macroeconomic rate easing (which directly threatens guided FY26 GAAP Net Income) or platform revenue execution lag ($37M actual H1 platform revenue vs. $310M–$330M exit guidance). Conviction is downgraded from aggressive Accumulate to Underweight / Speculative Tranche (0.5%–1.0%) pending Q3/Q4 software proof points.
